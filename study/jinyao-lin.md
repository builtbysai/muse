**Core engine: THE PHOTOGRAPH IS THE SCORE AND THE ETCHED LINE IS THE
INSTRUMENT.** Jinyao starts from photographs of nature and scenery (tzapac:
"Lin often uses photos of nature and scenery as references for inspiring
his artwork"), then re-grows the scene through generative systems of fine
etched lines: hatching, stippling, scribble, scallop scales, baroque
whorls, blade fans. What the code draws is never the object; it is the
atmosphere the object sits inside. Watchable behavior: a sky that is
nothing but horizontal hatch (Alley, f19); a cabin that is nothing but
stipple dots (forsaken, f17); a cloud field that is nothing but
cross-hatched scallops (Cloud Atlas, f13). Palette and density are the
parameters. The same system is plotted with a pen on paper (f12), minted
as a token on fxhash, projected room-size as immersive theatre (f02), and
live-minted by visitors from a gallery wall at Art Basel. A composer's
engine: one signal, many instruments.

**Signature devices (observed):**
1. **Etched-scene rendering (the fxhash batch, Feb 2022).** Four named
   series posted within hours on 2022-02-26, each a distinct atmosphere
   built from one line technique. Alley (fxhash token 3843, f19): an
   alleyway in converging one-point perspective, walls of vertical
   scribble, sky of fine horizontal hatch, a dark oval sun; f18 is a sepia
   mint of the same scene with a pale sun. forsaken (token 4788, f17): a
   tiny cabin and pines on cream, everything stippled dots, vast empty
   sky, the ground doubling as reflection. Evangel (f15): a dark oxblood
   ground with a black etched diagonal sweep, the most apocalyptic of the
   batch, least legible, most atmosphere. aura (f14): black ink veins and
   tendrils branching over a misty dotted ground, organic, nerve-like.
   The batch is a thesis: the same hand, four atmospheres.
2. **The scallop-scale unit (Cloud Atlas).** f13 (2022-03-01, "Cloud Atlas
   on fxhash"): a field of overlapping arched scales, each scale
   cross-hatched with fine lines, slate blue and pale cloud-white. The
   unit is the scale; the system is the tiling. Pushed later to extremes:
   f05 (2023-10-27) is the same unit as a radiant ivory-gold burst from a
   bright center; f06 (same day) is the unit as a molten red vortex
   spiraling into a black core. Palette is a mint-time parameter.
3. **Baroque whorls (Metaphysics).** f11 (2022-06-05, "Metaphysics."):
   dense turbulent whorls and paisley rosettes in grey, black and white,
   ornament as weather. f10 (same day, "landscape stype." [landscape
   style]): the same whorl system in indigo and pale blue on a cream
   crosshatched ground, rosette clusters like a blue landscape seen from
   above. Ornament stops decorating and becomes the subject.
4. **Blade-fan botanicals.** f09 (2023-10-26): dense clusters of sharp
   radiating blades in slate blue, like a jungle of paper fans. f07
   (2023-10-27): the identical system in autumn red, coral, and steel
   blue. The system reads botanical at print scale and becomes a storm at
   room scale: f02 (2024-10-26, "Metaphysics immersive theatre.") is a
   photograph of the blade-fan system projected huge on a gallery wall
   with floor projection, one viewer seated before it.
5. **The forsaken landscape (vast negative space).** f03 (2024-01-07,
   "Forsaken alone side the river"): glass-white pagoda forms on still
   water under a blue-grey sky, two thirds of the frame empty sky. f04
   (2023-10-27): a stippled treeline reflected in water under a slate
   twilight, sky nearly empty. f08 (2023-10-27): a grainy black mountain
   ridge against a lit sky with one glowing gap. The rule: the subject is
   tiny, the emptiness is the subject, and a reflection doubles whatever
   little there is.
6. **The composer's ear (audiovisual co-generation).** Bachelor's and
   master's in music; works cover "multimedia, installation art, drama,
   music and generative art NFTs" (tzapac). KEF's Journey Through Sound
   lists his "Intimate" as an experimental generative audiovisual
   project, live-minted in person at KEF Music Gallery Hong Kong,
   December 2022 (same KEF context as Marcelo Soria-Rodriguez and
   Andreas Rau per soundoflife). Co-author of a CHI 2025
   extended-abstracts paper on XR performance systems where "sensors in
   instruments and microphones send realtime data" to generate
   audiovisual art on stage. The music training is not biography; it is
   method: one input signal, sound and image as two readouts.
7. **The physical plotter print.** f12 (2022-03-02): a photograph of the
   Cloud Atlas scallop texture as a physical pen-plotted print lying on a
   wooden table. The digital system ends in ink on paper; the plotter is
   the final renderer. (Compare Zancan's plotter practice in the same
   tzapac piece: "code-based images exist as NFTs and can also be
   transferred to paper via a computer-directed pen plotter.")
8. **The platform as the practice.** akaSwap, co-founded with Aluan Wang,
   is not a side project; it is the continuation of the fxhash years by
   other means ("we felt that it was important to create a platform for
   everyone in Taiwan and the local area"). Features built for the
   community: akaDAO, Artist Clubs, gacha, bundles, akaVerse metaverse
   showrooms. The community is the medium he composes for.

**Failure modes (observed limits, for prompt grounding):**
- Legibility has a floor: Evangel (f15) pushes the etched sweep so far
  into atmosphere that the scene nearly vanishes; the mechanism needs a
  subject, however small, to anchor the hatch.
- Palette can flatten depth: the ivory-gold burst (f05) is luminous but
  shallower than the indigo whorls (f10); light palettes need the
  crosshatch ground to hold the forms.
- The forsaken landscapes only work because of grain and reflection:
  two thirds empty sky succeeds when the water doubles the subject (f03,
  f04) or the grain carries the light (f08). Pure emptiness would be
  nothing.
- Scale is a decision, not a slider: the blade fans read botanical on
  paper and apocalyptic on a gallery wall (f02). A prompt must commit to
  one scale and design for it.
