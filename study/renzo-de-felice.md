## M15. The new man and the left matrix

## Evolution

Phase 1, the Jacobin apprentice, 1953 to 1965. First essay on Jews in the Roman Republic, 1953, written in a Chabod seminar, Chabod urging its publication. Thesis on political thought in the first Roman Republic, supervisor Chabod, defended with honours on Tuesday 16 November 1954. Fellowship at the Istituto Italiano per gli Studi Storici, Naples, founded by Croce, directed by Chabod. The decisive encounter with Chabod and the deeper influence of Cantimori, "il maestro piu intimo", the most intimate master. Two 1960 volumes on revolutionary mysticism and the sale of national goods, published by Don Giuseppe De Luca's house. Italia giacobina, 1965, the closing statement of the Jacobin phase. Method already visible, DBI: biography as a probe of collective phenomena, attention to mass mentality and public opinion, hostility to conceptual generalisation, documentary and archival research, dignity given to the irrational in politics, use of the social sciences, impatience with moralism in historical judgement.

Phase 2, the Jewish doorway into Fascism, 1961 to 1966. Storia degli ebrei italiani sotto il fascismo, 1961, commissioned by the Unione delle comunita israelitiche. The Piccardi scandal, 1962: the book revealed Leopoldo Piccardi's participation in a 1939 racist juridical congress in Vienna, and the scandal shook the Radical Party. Refusal of the libera docenza, 1962, the commission including Giorgio Spini and Armando Saitta. The DBI Bocchini controversy, 1969, over his assistant Piero Melograni's entry, and De Felice's refusal to write the Bombacci, Bottai and Buffarini Guidi entries in solidarity. The Jewish thread runs from the Revolution through Fascism to Libya, Ebrei in un paese arabo, 1978.

Phase 3, the biography decades, 1965 to 1990. Eight tomes over thirty years; the long intervals, 1968 to 1974 and 1981 to 1990, mark the work's rhythm. The titles themselves are the argument: the revolutionary, the fascist, the duce, the ally. Each volume lands as a public event: 1965, Einaudi consultants uneasy, Bobbio, "avete pubblicato un volume su Mussolini... che acqua al mulino del fascismo ne porta parecchia"; 1974, the consensus volume, the sharpest academic attack, the INSMLI journal Italia contemporanea answering with the unsigned editorial "Una storiografia afascista per la 'maggioranza silenziosa'"; 1981, the totalitarian turn. Alongside: the interpretive syntheses, Le interpretazioni del fascismo, 1969, and Il fascismo, 1970; the foreign-policy studies, Alto Adige 1973, L'Italia fra tedeschi e Alleati 1973, Mussolini e Hitler 1975; D'Annunzio, 1978; the photographic history and the myth, with Goglia, 1981 and 1983; the Orient, 1988.

Phase 4, the public controversialist, 1975 to 1988. The interview as a weapon: Intervista sul fascismo, 1975, with Ledeen, best-seller, national scandal. The Ferrara interviews, Sunday 27 December 1987 and Thursday 7 January 1988, on the rhetoric of antifascism and the Constitution. The Linea rovente TV debate, Wednesday 6 January 1988. Lecture blockades, Tuesday 15 March 1988, January 1991, November 1992, the prolusione on racism and antisemitism delivered under police guard to a hall closed to students. Journalism at Corriere della Sera, 1968 to 1972 under Spadolini, and Il Giornale, from 1974, Montanelli.

Phase 5, the late synthesis and the grey zone, 1990 to 1996. The war volumes, 1990. The civil-war volume left unfinished. Rosso e Nero, 1995: the death of the fatherland, the minority Resistance, the grey zone. Italiani, amici nemici, 1996, with Bobbio and Rusconi. Fascismo, antifascismo, nazione, 1996. President of the Giunta centrale per gli studi storici, 1995. Premio Feltrinelli, 1995. The Molotov attack, February 1996, see C2. Death, Saturday 25 May 1996. Posthumous: the civil-war volume, 1997, edited by Gentile, Goglia and Missori; Fascismo, 1998, preface by Sergio Romano; Breve storia del Fascismo, 2001; the collected journalism, 2016 to 2019, edited by Parlato.

## Aesthetic logic

De Felice's work has one aesthetic, and it is the aesthetic of the pile. The document is the unit, the archive is the machine, the biography is the accumulation, and the pile is the argument, because nothing is asserted that the pile does not support, and the pile supports a life, and the life supports a phenomenon, and the phenomenon is Fascism, and Fascism is the pile. Against the slogan he sets the document, against the moral label he sets the fact, against the monolith he sets the stratification, and the setting-against is the method, and the method is the boredom with moralism, and the boredom is the discipline, and the discipline is the thirty years. His forms are all accumulative: the eight tomes, the anthologies, the carteggi, the photographic history, the bibliography as a map, the collected journalism in five tomes, and each form says the same thing, that history is made by piling, not by declaring. His public forms are the opposite of the pile and they are the same logic: the interview, the TV debate, the prolusione under guard, the trial that never ends, and each public form puts the work in the centre and lets the waves rise, and the waves are the controversy, and the controversy is the proof that the pile matters, because nobody trials a book that nobody reads. The grey zone is his late aesthetic and his truest: the majority is grey and still, the bright camps are small, and the piece that brightens the grey to make it interesting has missed the subject, because the grey is the subject, and the stillness is the history, and the history is the survival. The unfinished volume is his final form: the work outlives the author, the pupils pick up the pen in a different ink, and the different ink is not a flaw, it is the signature of the school, and the school is the fanning, and the fanning is the mantle, and the mantle passes, and the passing is the work.

## Load-bearing corrections

C0. The "six-volume" count: Stanley Payne's review says "With his six-volume political biography of Mussolini...", and study #437 repeated "six-volume" in its Network N1, line 151, and in its Future candidates top pick, line 229, and the #437 brief used it too. The count is wrong. The biography is FOUR volumes in EIGHT tomes, Einaudi, 1965 to 1997, confirmed by it.wikipedia, en.wikipedia and the Treccani DBI entry by Gentile, see D2. This file uses four volumes in eight tomes everywhere and does not repeat Payne's phrase except inside the quoted passage, where it is Payne's wording. The "six-volume" error is corrected here, not carried forward.

C1. Mussolini il fascista. Vol. I year: it.wikipedia and the Danish Historisk Tidsskrift article give 1966; the Treccani DBI gives 1967. For 1966: two sources against one, and the 1966 date fits the sequence, 1965, 1966, 1968. This file uses 1966 and notes the 1967 variant. UNRESOLVED at the level of a single year.

C2. Molotov attack night: the Controversie article and Corriere, 16 February 1996, give 15 February 1996; the DBI gives the night of 14 February 1996. For 15 February: the contemporary newspaper report and the Controversie article agree, and the attack was reported on the morning of the 16th. This file uses Thursday 15 February 1996, verified with date -d, and notes the 14 February variant. The two Molotov cocktails were thrown at his balcony.

C3. Page count of the Mussolini biography: en.wikipedia says about 6,000 pages; the Treccani DBI says 6,284 pages; it.wikipedia says 7,000 pages, "otto volumi per settemila pagine". For 6,284: the DBI gives the exact figure, and it is the most precise source. This file says "about six thousand pages" and gives the 6,284 figure with the 7,000 variant noted. The 7,000 figure is likely a round publisher number.

C4. Il fascismo e l'Oriente year: it.wikipedia gives 1988, Il Mulino, Bologna; a library catalogue record in search 10 gives 1989. For 1988: the Italian Wikipedia bibliography. This file uses 1988 and notes the 1989 variant. UNRESOLVED.

C5. Consensus qualifier: the brief's "genuine popular consensus" is corrected. De Felice's consensus was broad and sincere, but "largamente superficiale, passivo", largely superficial and passive, and it "non si trasformo mai, neppure nei momenti di maggior successo per il regime, in un 'consenso attivo'", never transformed, not even at the moments of greatest success for the regime, into an active consensus, DBI. This file carries the qualifier everywhere consensus appears, see M2 and D5. Do not write "genuine consensus" flat.
