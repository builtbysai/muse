## M1. Literature as action: the magazine is not a container, it is an act

Papini conceived literature as action, and the magazine is the proof. Treccani and both Wikipedias agree on the formula: Papini "concepi la letteratura come azione", he conceived literature as action, giving his writings "un tono oratorio e dissacrante", an oratorical and desecrating tone. Leonardo, first issue Sunday 4 January 1903, 25 issues to August 1907, was not a container for writing, it was an act in the world: its programme, on the first page of the first issue, says a group of young men, desirous of liberation, wanting universality, yearning for a superior intellectual life, gathered in Florence under the symbolic and auspicious name of Leonardo, to intensify their own existence, elevate their own thought, exalt their own art. Lacerba, 1913 to 1915, is the same doctrine at full volume: each issue is a sortie, each article a proclamation, each title a programme, each criticism a taking of the Bastille, each book a gospel, in Papini's own words quoted in Un uomo finito and it.wiki. The mechanism is that the words do things: the headline strikes, the paragraph advances and retreats, the issue lands like a blow, and the blow is the content, because the literature is the action, not the report of it. The watchable behaviour is a page that behaves like a battlefield, headlines advancing like sorties, paragraphs retreating and regrouping, and the reader is not reading about an action, the reader is inside one, because the magazine is the act.

**Takeaway:** let the words do things, and the piece should make each headline strike like a sortie.

## M2. The stroncatura: criticism as execution

A "saggio non critico", a non-critical essay, that does not judge a book but executes its author. Papini's 24 cervelli, 1913, the first series of "saggi non critici", judges 24 minds, praising some and demolishing others, and Stroncature, 1916, the second series, praises Shakespeare and Goethe and demolishes Boccaccio, Croce, Gentile, Benelli and Guido Mazzoni, the "passerotto agevolino", the little facilitated sparrow, and Testimonianze, 1918, is the third. The Portolano article says Papini was "mai stroncatore e dissacratorio" in the sense that he never stopped being a demolisher and a desecrator, and Treccani records that he had already, in 1911 and 1912, "intuito la forza dello scandalo come veicolo rapido per le idee nuove", intuited the force of scandal as a rapid vehicle for new ideas, per Luigi Baldacci. The mechanism is that the verdict is the event: the review does not describe the book, it kills the author or crowns him, and the killing is public, and the public killing is the point, because the stroncatura is criticism as execution, and the execution is performed in print, and the print travels. The watchable behaviour is a row of heads, books, busts, portraits, and a hammer that falls on some and crowns others, and the fall is the event, because the judgement is not a measurement, it is a blow.

**Takeaway:** put the heads in a row and let the hammer fall, and the piece should make the verdict the event.

## M3. The title as provocation: the book is sold before it is opened

A title that is already a punch, so the book is sold before it is opened. Il crepuscolo dei filosofi, 1905, the twilight of the philosophers, six philosophers demolished in turn, then philosophy itself declared dead. Un uomo finito, 1913, a finished man, the autobiography of a failure. Amiamo la guerra, 1914, let us love war. Chiudiamo le scuole, 1919, let us close the schools. Odiatevi gli uni con gli altri, Lacerba, hate one another. Almanacco purgativo 1914, 1913, the purgative almanac, a purge of the year. Il diavolo, 1953, the devil. The mechanism is that the title is the provocation and the provocation is the content: the reader buys the punch, and the book is the punch explained, and the explanation is secondary, because the title already did the work, and the work is the scandal, see M6, and the scandal is the distribution, because a title that hates, loves war, closes schools, kills God, travels faster than any argument. The watchable behaviour is titles that arrive one at a time, huge, and detonate, and the detonation is the reading, because the provocation is the content, and the content is the provocation.

**Takeaway:** let the title be the punch, and the piece should detonate one huge title at a time.

## M4. The self-invention: autobiography as indictment

Papini writes his own life as the case against himself. Un uomo finito, early 1913, Libreria della Voce, Florence: a solitary child becomes a young man who wants to become God and fails, "nato con la malattia della grandezza", born with the disease of greatness, and the book ends in the grave of the ambition: "Tutto e finito, tutto e perduto, tutto e chiuso... Qui e sotterrato un uomo che non pote diventare Dio... Io non chiedo ne pane, ne gloria, ne compassione. Ma chiedo e domando, umilmente, in ginocchio... un po' di certezza: una sola, una piccola fede sicura, un atomo di verita", all is finished, all is lost, all is closed, here is buried a man who could not become God, I ask for neither bread, nor glory, nor compassion, but I ask and beg, humbly, on my knees, for a little certainty, a single, a small sure faith, an atom of truth. The childhood is fixed in the opening: "Fin da ragazzo mi son sentito tremendamente solo e diverso... Ero, insomma, quel che le signore col cappello chiamano un 'bambino scontroso' e le donne in capelli 'un rospo'", from boyhood I felt tremendously alone and different, I was, in short, what the ladies with hats call a surly child and the women in their hair call a toad. The sequel is La seconda nascita, completed 1923, published posthumous 1958, the memoirs of the religious regeneration, the same life told after the reversal, see M5. The mechanism is that the autobiography is an indictment, not a celebration: the self is the accused, the greatness is the disease, the failure is the book, and the book is the trial of the self, because the man who wanted to become God writes his own sentence, and the sentence is the failure, and the failure is the work. Henry Miller, in Tropic of Cancer, admired exactly this: "Come fallito e una meraviglia", as a failure he is a marvel. The watchable behaviour is a figure that grows until it wants to fill the whole frame, then collapses to a speck asking for one atom of truth, and the growth is the ambition, and the collapse is the book, because the self-invention is the self-indictment.

**Takeaway:** let the figure grow until it wants to be God, then collapse it to a speck asking for one atom of truth.

## M5. The reversal: conversion as a public act

The complete about-face, performed in public, as a literary form. From Le memorie d'Iddio, 1911, God, weary of having created so much evil, wishes for the death of faith and therefore his own end, the peak of Papini's anti-Christian protest, later repudiated so fiercely that he had his daughter Viola hunt down surviving copies and burn them, to Storia di Cristo, in bookshops Thursday 31 March 1921, the life of a God who became man, written Tuesday 19 August 1919 to Saturday 9 October 1920, revised by Don Emanuele Magri and, in the third edition, 1922, by the Jesuit Father Enrico Rosa, translated into twenty-three languages, an international bestseller, over 40,000 copies in the first year. The introduction states the reversal in one sentence: "L'autore di questo libro ne scrisse un altro [Un uomo finito], anni fa, per raccontare la malinconica storia di un uomo che volle, per un momento, diventare Dio. Ora, nella maturita degli anni e della coscienza, lo stesso autore ha tentato di scrivere la vita di un Dio che si fece uomo", the author of this book wrote another, years ago, to tell the melancholy story of a man who wanted, for a moment, to become God. Now, in the maturity of years and of conscience, the same author has attempted to write the life of a God who became man. Carlo Bo on the conversion: "Non ci fu un tempo di meditazione, di deposito che desse frutti visibili... Papini usci dalla casa del diavolo [...] ed entro in chiesa di corsa, per di piu con l'aria di saperla cosi lunga da poter parlare direttamente con Dio e magari insegnare qualcosa agli altri...", there was no time of meditation, of deposit that gave visible fruit, Papini came out of the devil's house and entered church at a run, moreover with the air of knowing so much that he could speak directly with God and even teach something to others. The conversion was first confided to Cesare Angelini in a letter of Thursday 16 May 1918, and the dedication copy of Storia di Cristo given to Angelini is dated 28 March 1921, and Domenico Giuliotti was the participating witness of the return to faith. The same reversal runs in politics, anti-nationalist to nationalist, interventionist to penitent, and in the books themselves, Sant'Agostino, 1929, Il diavolo, 1953. The mechanism is that the reversal is the form: the same energy, the same oratorical and desecrating tone, turned through 180 degrees, and the turn is public, and the public turn is the book, because the conversion is not a private change of mind, it is a second book that answers the first, and the answer is the work. The watchable behaviour is a field that flips, black to white, attack to adoration, in one cut, with the same energy on both sides, and the flip is the conversion, because the man is the same man, and the direction is reversed.

**Takeaway:** flip the field in one cut, black to white, attack to adoration, with the same energy on both sides.

## M6. The scandal as vehicle: the spark that carries the idea

Scandal used deliberately as the fastest carrier for new ideas. Treccani Papini, quoting Luigi Baldacci: Papini had long "intuito la forza dello scandalo come veicolo rapido per le idee nuove", intuited the force of scandal as a rapid vehicle for new ideas. The instances are the blasphemous Lacerba articles and the trial for outrage to religion, see M17; "Amiamo la guerra", 1914, and the interventionist war hymn; the Discorso di Roma, 1913, and the Futurist evenings, see M20; Il diavolo, 1953, and the proposal for the Index. The mechanism is that the scandal is the distribution system: the idea cannot travel on its own, because the idea is new and the world is slow, and the scandal travels fast, because the scandal is an offence, and the offence is news, and the news carries the idea with it, and the idea arrives inside the outrage, because the outrage is the envelope, and the envelope is opened by everyone. The watchable behaviour is a small spark, one sentence, that sets off a chain reaction of outrage spreading outward, and the spreading is the distribution, because the scandal is the vehicle, and the vehicle is faster than the idea.

**Takeaway:** light one sentence and let the outrage carry it, and the piece should spread the scandal outward like a chain reaction.

## M7. The magazine as platform and faction-maker: founding a review to found a movement

Founding a review in order to found a movement, the review as the movement's body. ## Aesthetic logic

The Papini logic is that the reversal is the work, and the magazine is the weapon, and the letter is the life, and the fragment is the late style. He began as the surly child who wanted to become God and failed, and the failure was the first book, and the first book was the indictment of the self, and the indictment was the form, because the man who wanted to be God wrote his own sentence, and the sentence was the failure, and the failure was the work, and then the same man, with the same energy and the same oratorical and desecrating tone, turned through 180 degrees and wrote the life of a God who became man, and the turn was public, and the public turn was the second book, and the second book answered the first, and the answer was the work. The second logic is that the magazine is the movement: he did not write for a review, he made the review, and the review was the faction, and the faction was the movement, and the movement was the review, and the review opened, filled, and closed, and the closing was the movement's death, because Leonardo closed when it became commercial, and Lacerba closed two days before Italy entered the war, and the closing was the work, because the magazine was the act, not the container. The third logic is that the scandal is the distribution: the idea cannot travel on its own, because the idea is new and the world is slow, and the scandal travels fast, because the scandal is an offence, and the offence is news, and the news carries the idea with it, and the idea arrives inside the outrage, and the outrage is the envelope, and the envelope is opened by everyone. The fourth logic is that the letter is the life: the friendship with Soffici was conducted as a fifty-year correspondence, about a thousand letters, and the letters outlived every quarrel, the war that distanced them, the conversion that perplexed Soffici, the Fascism they both consented to differently, the post-war disgrace they both endured, and the conversation never stopped, even when the writers stopped agreeing, because the disagreement was inside the conversation, and the conversation was the friendship. The last logic is the fragment: the hands stopped, and the voice continued, and the continuing was the writing, because the writing does not need the hand, it needs the voice, and the voice is the writer, and the writer is the voice, and the words arrive without a writer visible, and the arrival is the dictation, and the dictation is the last form, and the last form is the fragment, and the fragment is complete, and the completeness is the point, because the fragment does not need the system, and the system is what failed.
