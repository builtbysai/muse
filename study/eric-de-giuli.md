## Core engine

THE PIECE IS A SPECULATIVE 2D PHYSICS SIMULATION AND PHYSICS IS THE BRUSH: from his own theoretical-physics research he codes simplified physical systems in two dimensions (chemical reactions for Calian, fluid flow for Zero, instability growth for the early dark work), starts each run from near-uniform conditions, and lets spontaneous symmetry breaking turn microscopic instability into watchable macroscopic structure. His own words: "'Zero' continues my attempt to translate scientific ideas into aesthetic form"; Calian asks "what is life", Atlas asks "how macroscopic patterns emerge from small scale chaos", Zero is "the dynamical unfolding of structure-building". Each piece is "its own world", real laws of physics "bent and broken for aesthetic purposes". The viewer watches the unfolding, never a precomputed image.

## Signature devices observed

1. **Order from nothing, the amplified micro-error (Zero, 2025).** The SOLOS text states it: "Beginning from nothing, the works reveal how order emerges from instability." Floating-point errors in the value zero, too small to register in ordinary use, are amplified until they cascade into visible patterns. "Perfectly uniform horizontal bands gradually destabilize, breaking apart, and giving rise to intricate, fluid-like formations." The randomness is sourced at the moment of execution from the viewer's own hardware: "Each rendering is therefore unique, with the smallest differences in numerical precision triggering new trajectories." Watchable behavior: flat bands tremble, rupture, then bloom into fluid color fields. In the Verse interview he adds that Zero removes the "violence in the motion" of earlier work; interest comes from "development in form and colour" instead. Each render is "a bit like different performances of the same musical score."
2. **The spectral script engine (Alien Tongues, 2021).** Outland: his first series "imagined messages from an alien civilization as spectral scratches organized by geometric syntaxes." Observed: f33 ("alien tongues 014 [detail]") shows single continuous thin white strokes forming glyph chains like cursive handwriting, each glyph a loop, kink, or crossing threaded on one baseline; f35 ("alien tongues 03a [detail]") shows rows of the same script running horizontally like lines of text. Black ground, white hairline strokes, zero decoration. The mechanism is calligraphy by algorithm: one continuous path per line, syntax implied by repetition and variation.
3. **The radial codex fan (Codex, 2021).** f30 ("codex 2101967766, The god codex"): hundreds of rays fan out from a bright glowing core at the bottom center, each ray densely studded with tiny script glyphs along its length, the whole fan bounded by an arc, like a peacock tail of text. f32 ("alien codex 2020597771"): the same fan grammar with sparser rays carrying distinct glyph types (crosses, circles, boxes), a dotted arc across the top. The watchable behavior is density as meaning: glyphs pack so tightly near the core that they read as light, thinning to isolated marks at the rim. Sold 1/200 on OpenSea.
4. **The smoke humanoid (Spectral beings / Phantoms, 2021).** f21 (video poster): a pale blurry humanoid with one arm extended, formed of smoke against black, mid-pose like a dancer. f23 ("Phantoms, coming soon" poster): a faint vertical wisp column, barely visible. f27 ("Spectral beings, preview" poster): a symmetric ghostly figure, glowing head with slit pattern, crossed arms, torso dissolving into drifting dots. Outland is blunt about this era: "The figures in De Giuli's Phantoms (2021) were awkwardly mannequin-like; his most exciting work tends to be his most abstract." Caption: "Each piece will be generated on mint." Mechanism: particles aggregate into a body, hold, then disperse; the body is a statistical event, never modeled geometry.
5. **The unknown creator's hand (Mazinaw, 2022).** f16 (Art Blocks release, April 7 2022): dark churning vortex of fine feathery strokes, a storm of grey filaments on black, hint of violet at the edge. His own text: Mazinaw "suggested the hand of an unknown creator". f18 ("Between order and chaos", Jan 2022): the same dark swirl grammar. The watchable behavior is turbulence resolving toward vortices: strokes that look brushed into being by something large and unseen. This is the "violence in the motion" he later says he removed.
6. **The quantum ambiguity sheet (Glass, 2022).** f10 (Glass Study #12): large translucent blue circles overlapping on pale ground, each circle filled with fine diagonal line-hatching that breaks into short colored segments where shapes overlap, pale blue, peach, cream, luminous. f11 (exhibition print at The Boiler, Brooklyn): the same grammar as a physical print. f12 (release announcement, Aug 22 2022, Art Blocks): the canonical mint look. His own text: Glass "visualized the intrinsic ambiguity of our quantum mechanical universe". Watchable behavior: overlaps resolve and unresolve; the lines read as both solid and ghostly at once. Part of the Tribute to Herbert W. Franke auction (12 studies, "made from code"). First exhibition at The Boiler, Brooklyn (opening June 20, 2022).
7. **The zoomable ecosystem (Calian, 2023, Art Blocks Curated).** His own text (Le Random collection page): "Calian is an infinitely zoomable and scrollable exploration of lifelike behaviour. It exhibits ambiguous natural-digital forms in a dynamic rendering. Species grow and compete; they get carried by the elements; they face boom, bust, and echo." Built from "the fruits of my research into the origin of life"; the Verse interview names its engine: "Calian started from a model of chemical reactions." Watchable behavior: species colonize a field, boom, get carried by currents, collapse, and echo in new territory; the viewer can dive infinitely deeper into the same ecosystem. Outland: "dynamic abstractions resemble a time-lapsed video of bacteria growing in a Petri dish."
8. **Powers-of-ten structure fields (Atlas, 2023, GrailersDAO).** Outland: "a bit like dynamic Powers of Ten paintings... Parts of the piece that interact so strongly at the most infinitesimal level connect to form structures only visible at other scales... the starting point of each Atlas piece is a shimmering, abstract mosaic that moves." The Verse interview: Atlas asks "how macroscopic patterns emerge from small scale chaos"; unlike Calian it was "driven more by aesthetic directions". Watchable behavior: zoom out and the mosaic resolves into structure; zoom in and it shimmers apart. Atlas #8 is the interview's figure.
9. **The infinite material piece (2026).** Caption on f0: "stills from an infinite piece, as usual". f0 shows molten dripping forms: vertical wax-like drips and folds in peach, pink, amber with hot red-orange veins, like lava or wax flowing down a wall. f6 (same era): salmon-orange fields with deep purple-black fluid shapes pouring vertically, eroded-stone texture. The palette is the newest: warm molten color replacing a decade of black and pale blue. The piece runs forever; the stills are samples. (Caption continues past the redacted words; full text unavailable in the pull.)
10. **Pax and the collectible systems.** f38 (Pax, Art Blocks Studio, 2025 reel poster): dark teardrop pebble shapes scattered over a mottled oil-slick field of teal, orange, violet. Caption: "Collectors can customize their hash & vantage point" (text truncated in pull). The Studio format makes the collector a co-explorer of the parameter space. f39 (late 2023 reel poster, cursor visible in frame): a grid of soft glowing pastel dots on deep blue-purple, like a screen recording of interactive points, motion from captions only.

## Evolution: early vs recent

- 2021: the dark monochrome years. Alien Tongues and Codex (spectral scratches, geometric syntax, sold 1/200 on OpenSea); Spectral beings and Phantoms (smoke humanoids, "generated on mint", mannequin-like per Outland); WIP p5js posts. Everything black ground, white hairline, single-stroke calligraphy, radial fans.
- 2022: the Art Blocks years and the light turn. Mazinaw (April 7, the dark storm, "the hand of an unknown creator"); first exhibition at The Boiler, Brooklyn (June 20); Glass (August 22, pale translucent circles, "the intrinsic ambiguity of our quantum mechanical universe"); Glass Study #12 in the Tribute to Herbert W. Franke auction; a talk on the interface between art and science and Franke's legacy (2024).
- 2023: the ecosystem years. Calian (Art Blocks Curated, chemical-reaction model, infinitely zoomable species); Atlas (GrailersDAO, Powers-of-Ten structure fields, "small scale chaos" to macroscopic pattern). The Outland criticism (Nov 2023) lands here: "his most exciting work tends to be his most abstract."
- 2025: Pax (Art Blocks Studio, collectible vantage points); Zero (Broken Symmetry) (SOLOS, September 2025, 256 artist-curated mints, fluid-flow model, micro-error randomness from the viewer's hardware, "removed the violence" of earlier motion). Verse interview (Sept 2025) and Verse Talks podcast episode "breaking symmetry" document the philosophy: "creating order from nothing", "avoiding noise as a structural element", "amplifying and controlling unpredictability", "the work as an unfolding performance", "emergent complexity from simple building blocks".
- 2026: the infinite piece (molten wax/lava drips in peach and red-orange, stills from an endless run); travel photos (Paris dawn, Spinoza statue in Amsterdam, stone relief facade). The palette has gone fully warm.
