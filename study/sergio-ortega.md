# Sergio Ortega: Study Notes

**Site:** Sergio Ernesto Haroldo Ortega Alvarado (Antofagasta, Wednesday 2 February 1938, Paris, Monday 15 September 2003), the Chilean composer, pianist, poet, teacher and politician, the man who wrote the two anthems of the Unidad Popular and the forge that sang the history of Chile, the composer who was popular for the learned and learned for the popular, the soundman of the Antonio Varas theatre who became the musical voice of the Chilean left, the friend of Neruda who musicalised the poet's only play and his book of secret love poems, the teacher of Horacio Salinas and Guillermo Rifo, the director of Canal 9 television, the exile who built a workshop in Nanterre and directed the music school of Pantin for nineteen years, the father whose eldest son joined the French faction of Quilapayun and finished his last film score with him. He composed "Venceremos" in the first half of the 1960s with the lyricist Claudio Iturra and "El pueblo unido jamas sera vencido" on a night of May or June 1973 with Quilapayun collaborating on the versification, and he wrote "La fragua, cantos para chilenos" alone, all twenty-four parts of the cantata, and he set Allende's government programme to music with Luis Advis as "Canto al programa" in 1970, and he died of pancreatic cancer four days after the thirtieth anniversary of the coup, and his remains came home on Saturday 27 September 2003 and were buried on Sunday 28 September 2003 in the Cementerio General while Eduardo Carrasco and Jose Seves and Max Berru and Horacio Duran sang "El pueblo unido" over them. ## M1. The self-taught ear

Ortega learns the piano alone at fifteen, and the alone is the mechanism. He teaches himself by ear, pulling out "Blue Moon" and "Night and Day" and a profuse catalogue of boleros and even the beginning of the "Tannhauser" overture, and the pulling-out is the mechanism, because the ear comes before the score, and the ear is the composer, and the composer is the mechanism. He has no significant musical background in his family, and the none is the mechanism, because the music arrives from outside the house, and the outside is the ear, and the ear is the piano, and the piano is the mechanism. He plays in youth orchestras without thinking of a professional dedication, and the not-thinking is the mechanism, because the playing is a pleasure before it is a profession, and the pleasure is the ear, and the ear is the work. From the Colegio San Ignacio he goes to the Universidad Catolica to study architecture for a time and then literature, and the going is the mechanism, because he tries two other lives before the music, and the trying is the ear, and the ear is the decision. He begins to study music at eighteen, while still in the architecture faculty, and the late is the mechanism, because he systematises a natural vocation rather than being formed by it from childhood, and the systematising is the conservatory, and the conservatory is the ear made formal, and the formal is the mechanism. He enters the Conservatorio Nacional of the Universidad de Chile and studies composition with Roberto Falabella and then with Gustavo Becerra-Schmidt, and the studying is the mechanism, because the autodidact takes two masters and keeps the ear, and the keeping is the composer, and the composer is the mechanism. **Takeaway:** let the ear come first and the score second, try two other lives before the music, and systematise a natural vocation late, so the conservatory formalises an ear that already knows what it wants. Against the chain: Violeta Parra (#369) also learned by ear and by collecting, but hers is a woman who learns the guitar in the countryside and writes down what she hears, and Ortega's is a city boy who pulls Wagner out by ear and then takes the conservatory, which is why hers is a collecting and this is a systematising.

## M2. The soundman in the theatre

Ortega spends six years as a sound engineer, and the six years are the mechanism. After graduating he works at the Instituto de Extension Musical and then six years as a soundman at the Teatro Experimental of the University, the Teatro Antonio Varas, and the working is the mechanism, because he learns the theatre from the booth before he writes for the stage, and the booth is the school, and the school is the mechanism. He is hired in 1963 as soundman and composer at the Antonio Varas on the proposal of Celso Garrido-Lecca, who leaves the post that year, and the proposing is the mechanism, because one soundman hands the post to another, and the handing is the theatre, and the theatre is the mechanism. In some stagings he does the sound of the works, launching pre-existing music or simply ambient sounds, and the launching is the mechanism, because the recordings show horses and thunder and rain and crickets that Ortega himself had to obtain to include on the tape, and the obtaining is the sound, and the sound is the theatre, and the theatre is the mechanism. In other cases he composes the music or makes arrangements and musical direction, and the composing is the mechanism, because the same hands that run the tape write the score, and the writing is the sound, and the sound is the music, and the music is the mechanism. The theatre is his first school of the popular, and the school is the mechanism, because the work for the stage puts him among actors and directors and popular song, and the putting is the learning, and the learning is the theatre, and the theatre is the composer. **Takeaway:** spend six years in the booth before writing for the stage, learn to obtain a horse and a thunderclap on tape, and let the same hands that run the sound write the score, so the theatre teaches the composer before the composer teaches the theatre. Against the chain: Jara (#370 M1) also staged the song, but his is a director who stages his own songs and steps forward to sing them, and Ortega's is a soundman who learns the stage from behind the curtain and then writes for it, which is why his is a staging of the self and this is a schooling from the booth.

## M3. The bridge between docta and popular

Ortega builds a bridge between the learned and the popular, and the bridge is the mechanism. MusicaPopular states it plainly: he was popular for the learned and learned for the popular, and the plainly is the mechanism, because the two worlds meet in one man and neither claims him whole, and the neither is the bridge, and the bridge is the mechanism. His biography crosses many times with Luis Advis, and the crossing is the mechanism, because both men had the disposition to build bridges between the classical and popular worlds, and the building is the bridge, and the bridge is the two men, and the two men are the mechanism. The musicologist Silvia Herrera says tracing Ortega means standing on two paths of musical craft, the academic and the popular, and the standing is the mechanism, because the profile cannot be drawn on one path alone, and the two paths are the composer, and the composer is the mechanism. Ortega himself, asked in the 1980s about his place in Europe, answers that in his music there come out elements infrequent in contemporary European composers, because there the popular art is not practised by people who leave the conservatory, there is a very deep separation, and he has dedicated his whole life to finding this link, "Yo he dedicado toda mi vida a encontrar esta ligazon", and the dedicating is the mechanism, because the link is the life's work, and the life's work is the bridge, and the bridge is the mechanism. The early chamber works already show it, and the showing is the mechanism, because the "Vals peruano" of 1962 and the "Bossa nova" of 1963 are prophetic in their mixture of the learned and the popular, and the mixture is the bridge, and the bridge is the prophecy, and the prophecy is the mechanism. **Takeaway:** build the bridge and spend a life on it, be popular for the learned and learned for the popular, and let the mixture be there from the first chamber works, so the bridge is the career and not a phase. Against the chain: Advis (in #377 M5) also built the bridge, but his is a cantata that brings the conservatory into the nitrate pampa, and Ortega's is a life spent finding the link itself, which is why his is a work and this is a dedication.

## M4. The dramatic sense

The theatre teaches Ortega dramaturgy, and the dramaturgy is the mechanism. One of the most interesting learnings of his relation with the theatre is the possibility of establishing a sense of dramaticity in his music, both in the music composed for the stage and in the so-called autonomous music, and the establishing is the mechanism, because the composer develops a consciousness of dramaturgy that he applies in his compositions, building characters, elaborating dialogues and dramatic counterpoints, developing narrations, and the building is the drama, and the drama is the music, and the music is the mechanism. He also establishes close links between text and music, and the linking is the mechanism, because "El pueblo unido jamas sera vencido" is a clear example of dramaticity and dialogue between music and text, and the dialogue is the drama, and the drama is the song, and the song is the mechanism. The connection leads him to Brecht and Hanns Eisler, and the leading is the mechanism, because in Brecht there is a conjunction between popular music, vanguard and ideological commitment, and the conjunction is the model, and the model is Ortega, and Ortega is the mechanism. Eisler's theoretical and ideological developments become the axes of his creative work, and the becoming is the mechanism, because the German playwright and the Austrian composer give him a method for making the song a political act without losing the craft, and the method is the drama, and the drama is the song. Ortega explains "El pueblo unido" in his own words, and the explaining is the mechanism, because the song, of simple appearance, is of extreme complexity, its structure resting on an alternation sometimes brusque, sometimes with transition, of terms from the lyric with others from the epic, the epic being the shouts of the multitude, which the singers describe as absent, that is, the singers describe its action and narrate that multitude, but in reality it is present, and the mass understands it so, participating as it does, and the participating is the drama, and the drama is the crowd, and the crowd is the mechanism. **Takeaway:** let the theatre teach the song its dramaturgy, build characters and dialogues and dramatic counterpoints inside the music, and let the crowd be described as absent while it is present, so the song is a play the audience acts in. Against the chain: Quilapayun (#377 M2) also staged the song as theatre, but theirs is a director who stages the group and leaves them staged, and Ortega's is a composer who learns dramaturgy in the booth and writes it into the notes, which is why theirs is a staging and this is a writing.

## M5. The Neruda partnership

Neruda entrusts Ortega with his words, and the entrusting is the mechanism. He is not yet thirty when Neruda asks him to musicalise the staging of his translation of "Romeo y Julieta", and the asking is the mechanism, because the poet chooses a young composer for his Shakespeare, and the choosing is the partnership, and the partnership is the mechanism. Then Neruda asks him for the music of his only play, "Fulgor y muerte de Joaquin Murieta", premiered in 1967 at the Antonio Varas under Pedro Orthous, and the asking is the mechanism again, because the poet writes the play and the composer makes it sing, and the making is the partnership, and the partnership is the work. The work tours Europe with success, and the touring is the mechanism, because a Chilean bandit in California becomes a European stage, and the becoming is the opera, and the opera is the mechanism. Ortega defines the work as a combination of oral tradition, popular tradition and the "experimental trades" Neruda spoke of, and the defining is the mechanism, because the opera refuses the solemn reading many wanted to give to the music of great stagings, and the refusing is the fiesta, and the fiesta is the opera, and the opera is the mechanism. "La opera debe ser una fiesta", the opera must be a party, is his point of view, and the party is the mechanism, because the opera is a celebration and not a ceremony, and the celebration is the work, and the work is the mechanism. The partnership has a third and last coincidence in the stage version of "Canto general" in 1970, with Gustavo Becerra and the group Aparcoa, and the third is the mechanism, because the poet's great book becomes a stage with two composers and a popular group, and the becoming is the partnership, and the partnership is the book. In exile the partnership returns as love, and the returning is the mechanism, because "Los Cantos del Capitan" of 1982 sets Neruda's "Versos del Capitan", the secret love book for Matilde Urrutia, as Lieder for voice and piano, dedicated to Ortega's own wife Sophie Geoffroy-Dechaume, and the dedicating is the love, and the love is the partnership, and the partnership is the exile. Bulnes calls Neruda the "cronista poeta" and Ortega the "cronista musical" of the century's great events, and the calling is the mechanism, because the poet writes the history and the musician sings it, and the singing is the history, and the history is the partnership. **Takeaway:** let the poet entrust you with his only play and his secret love book, refuse the solemn reading, and make the opera a party, so the partnership runs from Shakespeare to a wedding song. Against the chain: Quilapayun (#377 M11) also waited fifty-five years on a Neruda play, but theirs is a cantata carried across a lifetime and finished by old men, and Ortega's is a partnership that runs from the first commission to the last love song, which is why theirs is a waiting and this is a working.

## M6. The commissioned hymn

The organisations commission the hymn, and the commissioning is the mechanism. Ortega composes the anthems of the Central Unica de Trabajadores, of the Federacion de Estudiantes de la Universidad de Chile, of the Juventudes Comunistas and of the Partido Radical, and the composing is the mechanism, because each organisation asks for its own song and the composer gives each one a different hymn, and the giving is the commission, and the commission is the mechanism. The CUT asks through its president, and the asking is the mechanism, because the commission illustrates how the action is valued and what resonance is attributed to it inside the organisations of the people, and the valuing is the hymn, and the hymn is the organisation, and the organisation is the mechanism. "Venceremos" is the summit of the line, and the summit is the mechanism, because it is composed with Claudio Iturra for Allende's campaign and becomes the hymn of the Unidad Popular, and the becoming is the commission, and the commission is the anthem, and the anthem is the mechanism. The hymn is a job, not an inspiration, and the job is the mechanism, because the composer works to order for a movement, and the working is the hymn, and the hymn is the movement, and the movement is the mechanism. The commissions keep coming in exile, and the coming is the mechanism, because the same hand that wrote for the CUT writes for the resistance, and the writing is the commission, and the commission is the exile, and the exile is the mechanism. **Takeaway:** take the commission from the organisation, give each one its own hymn, and let the hymn be a job done well, so the movement has a song because it asked for one. Against the chain: Jara (#370) also wrote the campaign song, but his is a singer who writes his own anthem and sings it himself, and Ortega's is a composer who takes the commission from the union president, which is why his is a singing and this is a commissioning.

## M7. The moving bass

"Venceremos" is built on a bass that never stops, and the never-stopping is the mechanism. Ortega explains it in his own manuscript: in the 1970 campaign he composed "Venceremos" in continuous dialogue around the text with Victor Jara and other comrades, all at the request of the UP command, and the dialogue is the mechanism, because the song is made in conversation and not alone, and the conversation is the bass, and the bass is the mechanism. Here is a complicated construction and an example of his eagerness to work the bass in a structural way, and the eagerness is the mechanism, because during the whole song, even in the chorus, the bass stays in incessant gradual movement, adding to the shout of "Venceremos" a harmonic activity that gives that slogan a sensation of unexpected sonorous depth that takes it away from the pamphlet, and the taking-away is the mechanism, because the craft lifts the slogan out of propaganda, and the lifting is the bass, and the bass is the song. The technique is the descending chromatic bass, and the descending is the mechanism, because the chromatic descent gives the shout its depth, and the depth is the bass, and the bass is the shout, and the shout is the mechanism. The dissertation notes the same care elsewhere, and the noting is the mechanism, because the permanent contrast in movement and direction between the continuo bass and the upper voices is his model, taken from the baroque masters, and the contrast is the craft, and the craft is the bass, and the bass is the mechanism. The song was first recorded by Quilapayun and a small orchestra, then by the Orquesta Sinfonica del Pueblo, and the recording is the mechanism, because the same moving bass carries both versions, and the carrying is the bass, and the bass is the anthem, and the anthem is the mechanism. **Takeaway:** give the shout a bass that never stops moving, let the chromatic descent give the slogan its depth, and let the craft lift the song out of the pamphlet, so the anthem is a construction and not a cry. Against the chain: Advis (in #377 M5) also wrote with a conservatory hand, but his is a cantata that stacks voices course by course, and Ortega's is a bass that walks under the shout, which is why his is a stacking and this is a walking.

## M8. The absent crowd that sings

"El pueblo unido" puts the crowd at the centre, and the centre is the mechanism. Ortega says he set out to make a mobilising song with the participation of the mass, not as an accompaniment to a rhythm, but as the centre and objective of the structure of the song, and the setting-out is the mechanism, because the mass is the structure and not the decoration, and the structure is the crowd, and the crowd is the mechanism. The song begins with a recitado, "El pueblo unido, jamas sera vencido", four times, first by a solo and then by the chorus, to which the whole assembly is added, and the beginning is the mechanism, because the opening is a cappella and then harmonised by the instrument, and the harmonising is the assembly, and the assembly is the crowd, and the crowd is the mechanism. The chorus is a shout with percussion alone, and the shout is the mechanism, because it travels into any language, and the travelling is the chorus, and the chorus is the shout, and the shout is the crowd. The structure alternates the lyric and the epic, and the alternating is the mechanism, because the strophes sing of flags of unity and a red dawn and the life to come, and the chorus shouts the slogan, and the shouting is the lyric, and the lyric is the epic, and the epic is the crowd. The singers describe the multitude as absent, and the describing is the mechanism, because they narrate a crowd that is in reality present, and the mass understands it so, participating as it does, and the participating is the crowd, and the crowd is the song, and the song is the mechanism. The song was composed on a night of May or June 1973, three months before the coup, and the composing is the mechanism, because the shout is written before the disaster it will answer, and the before is the crowd, and the crowd is the anthem, and the anthem is the mechanism. **Takeaway:** put the mass at the centre of the structure and not as an accompaniment, open with a solo that becomes a chorus that becomes the whole assembly, and let the singers describe a crowd that is present, so the song is the crowd singing itself. Against the chain: Quilapayun (#377 M6) also closes with the same anthem, but theirs is the anthem written with its own hands and closed with at the festival, and Ortega's is the anthem composed alone at night with the crowd as its structure, which is why theirs is a closing and this is a composing.

## M9. The workshop at Lo Canas

The house at Lo Can~as is the workshop, and the workshop is the mechanism. Carrasco remembers it: "Trabajabamos en su casa de Lo Can~as", we worked in his house at Lo Can~as, and the remembering is the mechanism, because the house is where the songs were made, and the making is the house, and the house is the workshop, and the workshop is the mechanism. Farias describes the method, and the describing is the mechanism, because Ortega would come out of his meetings of the Communist Party culture commission and call them urgently to go to his house to work on some themes suggested to them, and between wines and jokes the seriousness of the task was never lost sight of, and the wines are the work, and the work is the house, and the house is the mechanism. The list grew as they worked, and the growing is the mechanism, because one song to celebrate the triumph, another to help the economic measures of the government, another to stigmatise the coup-plotting right, another to call for the unity of the people, and the listing is the workshop, and the workshop is the list, and the list is the mechanism. The songs that resulted from these workshop jobs were marked by the seal of his relation with popular music and with oral music, and the marking is the mechanism, because the house work is oral work, and the oral is the workshop, and the workshop is the song. The 1970 "Venceremos" was recorded between Carrasco's house and Ortega's Lo Can~as plot, and the between is the mechanism, because the anthem is made in two houses and not in a studio, and the two houses are the workshop, and the workshop is the anthem. **Takeaway:** make the house the workshop, work between wines and jokes with the seriousness never lost, and let the list grow song by song, so the anthem is made at a table and not in a studio. Against the chain: Quilapayun (#377 M9) also ran a workshop album in exile, but theirs is a group of interpreters forced to become authors, and Ortega's is a composer who calls his friends to his house to work, which is why theirs is a forcing and this is a calling.

## M10. The contingent song

The contingent song answers the news in two days, and the two days are the mechanism. Ortega calls them "canciones contingentes", and the calling is the mechanism, because the song enters the political struggle as a carrier of a counter-attack response to the enemy, and the carrying is the song, and the song is the news, and the news is the mechanism. "Las ollitas" is the proof, and the proof is the mechanism, because it was launched as a response against the right-wing women who went out incited by the fascists to beat pots in the street as a sign of hunger against the popular government, and Ortega composed it in a while, after discussing the adequate rhythm and style with Eduardo Carrasco, made the arrangement at night, rehearsed it the next day and recorded it the following night, and in two days it was on the radios, on magnetic tapes, played incessantly at the hour the distinguished ladies went out to the corners with the pots, and the two days are the song, and the song is the answer, and the answer is the mechanism. The song explains that the right has two little pots, one big one full of black-market products and one empty and new because she has just bought it and uses it to beat, and the explaining is the mechanism, because the satire names the trick, and the naming is the song, and the song is the mockery, and the mockery is the mechanism. "No se para la cuestion" is composed just as fast for the truck owners' strike, the "paro patronal" called on 26 July 1973, and the fast is the mechanism, because the song must reach the scene of the contingency at once, and the at-once is the song, and the song is the strike, and the strike is the mechanism. "El enano maldito" is a satire against the opponents who use the press to discredit the government, and the satire is the mechanism, because the little dwarf is a comic-strip character of the UP newspaper Puro Chile, and the comic is the song, and the song is the press, and the press is the mechanism. "Vox populis" narrates the poisoned political atmosphere and has the people demand that Allende stop the sedition, and the demanding is the mechanism, because the chorus repeats "Paralo, paralo", stop him, stop him, and the repeating is the demand, and the demand is the song. Ortega's rule for the form is that it supposes deducing a given situation, discovering the most effective style of contact with the mass, composing and verifying very fast and precisely, and above all easy to sing and mount, which does not mean bad, necessarily, and the rule is the mechanism, because speed and precision and singability are the craft, and the craft is the song, and the song is the news. **Takeaway:** answer the news in two days, discuss the rhythm with a friend, arrange at night, rehearse next day, record the following night, and let the song be on the radio while the pots are still beating, so the song is the answer and not the commentary. Against the chain: Yupanqui (#371) also reused the collected form, but his is a form collected on muleback and refilled once with his own words, and Ortega's is a song written in an afternoon to answer the pots on the street, which is why his is a refilling and this is a reply.

## M11. The forge

"La fragua" hammers the history of Chile, and the hammering is the mechanism. The work is a collection of "cronicas populares", popular chronicles, presented for the first time at the Teatro Municipal in 1972, with narration by Roberto Parada and singing by Quilapayun and musicians of the Orquesta Sinfonica de Chile under Eduardo Moubarak, and the presenting is the mechanism, because the elegant hall hears the "chimuchina", the hubbub, enter it, and a newspaper complains about it, and the complaining is the forge, and the forge is the hall, and the hall is the mechanism. It is conceived as a greeting to the fifty years of the Chilean Communist Party, founded in 1922, and the greeting is the mechanism, because the party's anniversary is the occasion and the history is the gift, and the gift is the forge, and the forge is the mechanism. Ortega explains it himself: "La fragua" is a homage to the social history of Chile, to the workers' struggles, to the set of processes that have been adding up and have led to the present government, and he adds that he would not know whether it is a cantata or what, in reality he could not define it musically, and the not-knowing is the mechanism, because the work refuses its own genre, and the refusing is the forge, and the forge is the history, and the history is the mechanism. The structure is twenty-four parts in four sections, "Las claves", "Las luchas", "La herencia", "El pun~o del pueblo", twelve songs and eleven recitados and a pregon, and the counting is the mechanism, because the history runs from the conquest to the triumph of the Unidad Popular, and the running is the forge, and the forge is the chronicle, and the chronicle is the mechanism. The final song, "El pun~o del pueblo", rises to build the road to socialism, and the rising is the mechanism, because the timbales and horns open it and the coda in imitation ends it with a sustained pedal, and the ending is the fist, and the fist is the people, and the people are the forge. The double album adds six contingent songs at the end, and the adding is the mechanism, because the forge closes with the news of the day, and the news is the history, and the history is the forge. The 2023 revival, fifty years on, opens the Foro de las Artes at the Universidad de Chile on Thursday 12 October 2023, and the opening is the mechanism, because the original score survived in the university archive and the work returns to the hall it first entered, and the returning is the forge, and the forge is the memory, and the memory is the mechanism. **Takeaway:** hammer the history from the conquest to the present government, refuse to say whether it is a cantata, and close the forge with the news of the day, so the work is a chronicle and not a genre. Against the chain: Advis (in #377 M5) also wrote a cantata that tells a massacre, but his is eighteen parts with five spoken relatos about one day in 1907, and Ortega's is twenty-four parts about four centuries, which is why his is a telling and this is a hammering.

## M12. The programme set to music

"Canto al programa" musicalises a government programme, and the musicalising is the mechanism. In November 1970 Ortega, as part of the Communist Party culture commission, proposes to Luis Advis, creator of the Cantata Santa Maria de Iquique, that they write together the music of a work in the same line as that cantata to spread the main measures of the new government, and the proposing is the mechanism, because the programme becomes a cantata, and the cantata is the programme, and the programme is the mechanism. The texts are by Julio Rojas, and the texts are the mechanism, because each song is one measure of the government, the deepening of democracy, the armed forces and carabineros, the agrarian reform, the new culture, international relations, and the measuring is the song, and the song is the programme, and the programme is the mechanism. Ortega writes six of the songs and Advis the rest, and the six is the mechanism, because two composers share one programme, and the sharing is the cantata, and the cantata is the programme, and the programme is the mechanism. Inti-Illimani sings it, and the singing is the mechanism, because the group that will carry the programme into exile first learns it at home, and the learning is the singing, and the singing is the programme. A narrator speaks between the songs, and the speaking is the mechanism, because the character is Peyuco Pueblo, the voice of the programme of the "poder popular", played by Alberto Sendra, and the voice is the people, and the people are the programme, and the programme is the mechanism. The deadline is told two ways, and the two ways are the mechanism, because one account says the work had to be written, rehearsed and recorded in a week, and another says there was little more than a month, and the week is the urgency, and the urgency is the programme, and the programme is the mechanism. The premiere is held at the end of January 1971 in the Sala La Reforma with Allende and his ministers in the first row, and the first row is the mechanism, because the government hears its own programme sung back to it, and the hearing is the cantata, and the cantata is the programme. **Takeaway:** take the government programme and set it to music, give each measure its own song, put a narrator called Peyuco Pueblo between them, and sing it to the ministers in the first row, so the programme is a cantata and not a document. Against the chain: Quilapayun (#377 M3) also made an anthology the argument, but theirs is twelve songs from seven countries with the programme written on the sleeve, and Ortega's is twelve songs that are the programme itself, which is why theirs is a map and this is a musicalisation.

## M13. The chronicle of real people

Ortega writes songs about real people, and the real is the mechanism. "Marta Ugarte se queda" is composed in exile, and the composing is the mechanism, because Marta Ugarte, teacher, dressmaker, member of the Central Committee of the Communist Party, was one of the victims thrown into the sea by order of the military government, her body in a sack with a rail tied to one arm so it would sink, and the rail came loose, and on Thursday 9 September 1976 her body rose from the ocean and washed up at Playa La Ballena near Los Molles, the only testimony of a way of extermination, and the rising is the song, and the song is the body, and the body is the evidence, and the evidence is the mechanism. The song says Marta waters the rosebushes with her blood and they do not dry, and the saying is the mechanism, because the metaphor makes the murdered woman into a red spring, and the spring is the patria, and the patria is the woman, and the woman is the mechanism. "Hijo de amor de una noche de invierno" is part of the cantata "Bernardo O'Higgins Riquelme", and the part is the mechanism, because it tells the condition of the bastard son of Bernardo O'Higgins, born of a winter night's love, who has no father but the fatherland, and the telling is the son, and the son is the fatherland, and the fatherland is the mechanism. The cantata was composed and mounted between April and June 1978 in the old house at Nanterre, and the mounting is the mechanism, because Ortega says none of its features could have been imagined without the deep presence of the Taller Recabarren and each of its members beside him, and the presence is the cantata, and the cantata is the exile, and the exile is the mechanism. It premiered on Thursday 26 October 1978 in Graz, Austria, at the Steirischer Herbst, and the premiering is the mechanism, because the father of the fatherland is first sung in Austria by exiles, and the Austria is the exile, and the exile is the fatherland. "Lautaro y Valdivia" is the eighth song of "La fragua", and the eighth is the mechanism, because it narrates two men, the Mapuche Lautaro and the conquistador Valdivia, two cultures in struggle personified in two beings, each carrying his own destiny, and the narrating is the duel, and the duel is the history, and the history is the mechanism. The song is built as a composed song close to the Schubert Lied and fused with the classical sonata form, with strophes and recitados and a fugato and a tutti, and the building is the mechanism, because each part contradicts the one before, and the contradicting is the duel, and the duel is the music, and the music is the mechanism. **Takeaway:** name the real person, the real date and the real place, let the body that rose from the sea be the evidence, and let the song be the history that was not written, so the chronicle is the work. Against the chain: Violeta Parra (#369) also sang the real Chile, but hers is a woman who collects the songs of the countryside and sings them alone, and Ortega's is a composer who writes the murdered woman and the bastard liberator into the score, which is why hers is a collecting and this is a chronicling.

## M14. The exile workshop

Exile makes Ortega build a workshop, and the workshop is the mechanism. After the coup he takes refuge in the Panama embassy and then leaves for France, and the leaving is the mechanism, because his destination is the same as his friends of Quilapayun, France, and the same is the exile, and the exile is the workshop. He settles first in Nanterre, where he works with the Chilean-French musical collective Taller Recabarren, and the settling is the mechanism, because the workshop is French and Chilean musicians together, and the together is the workshop, and the workshop is the exile, and the exile is the mechanism. He takes the musical direction and plays the piano in it, and the taking is the mechanism, because the composer directs and plays, and the playing is the directing, and the directing is the workshop. With the Taller he records the LP "Venceremos" in 1978 on Movieplay, and the recording is the mechanism, because the old anthem is re-recorded by exiles, and the re-recording is the resistance, and the resistance is the workshop. He writes the cantata "Bernardo O'Higgins Riquelme" for the Taller, and the writing is the mechanism, because the workshop is the instrument the cantata is written for, and the instrument is the exile, and the exile is the cantata. He works with the Studio H&S of Walter Heynowski and Gerhard Scheumann in East Berlin, musicalising documentaries, and the working is the mechanism, because "J'etais, je suis, je serai" of 1975 shows the prisoners of Chacabuco and Pisagua with a lugubrious music that recalls the Cantata Santa Maria de Iquique, and the recalling is the desert, and the desert is the documentary, and the documentary is the mechanism. He directs the Ecole Nationale de Musique of Pantin from 1983 to 2002, and the directing is the mechanism, because the exile becomes a school, and the school is the workshop, and the workshop is the nineteen years, and the nineteen years are the mechanism. He writes the French Revolution operatic trilogy with texts by the French poet Francis Combes, premiered for the Bicentennial in 1989, and the writing is the mechanism, because the Chilean exile writes the French Revolution, and the French is the Chilean, and the Chilean is the trilogy. **Takeaway:** build the workshop in the exile, direct it and play the piano in it, re-record the old anthem with new hands, and write the French Revolution as a Chilean, so the exile is the work and not the interruption. Against the chain: Inti-Illimani (#373) also worked in exile, but theirs is a group that tours nine countries and learns the music at first hand, and Ortega's is a workshop built in one suburb of Paris, which is why theirs is a travelling and this is a building.

## M15. The travelling song

"El pueblo unido" travels, and the travelling is the mechanism. The song is the travelling song in the fullest sense, and the fullest is the mechanism, because it leaves its native soil and crosses other places, adapting its message according to where it finds itself, and the adapting is the travelling, and the travelling is the song, and the song is the mechanism. Frederic Rzewski writes 36 piano variations on it in 1975, and the writing is the mechanism, because the American pianist counts Ortega among his close friends and turns the march into a concert work, and the turning is the travelling, and the travelling is the variation, and the variation is the mechanism. In Iran the melody carries a revolutionary song in Persian, "Barpakhiz", during the revolution of 1979, and the carrying is the mechanism, because the same notes lift a different people, and the lifting is the travelling, and the travelling is the melody. In Ukraine the song is paraphrased as "Razom nas bahato", sung in the Orange Revolution of 2004 and taken to the Eurovision Song Contest of 2005, and the paraphrasing is the mechanism, because the march becomes a rap and the rap becomes a national entry, and the becoming is the travelling, and the travelling is the song. In Tunisia the protesters sing the same rhythm to the phrase "the people want the fall of the regime" in 2010 and 2011, and in Tahrir Square in Cairo they sing it on 25 January 2011, and the singing is the mechanism, because the rhythm carries new words in a new language, and the carrying is the travelling, and the travelling is the rhythm. The documentary "Himno" by Martin Farias and Eileen Karmy collects 150 recordings from all over the world, and the collecting is the mechanism, because most songs are recorded once and a successful one a couple of times, and a Chilean song with more than a hundred versions and so many translations is a phenomenon almost without comparison, and the comparison is the travelling, and the travelling is the song. **Takeaway:** write a chorus that is a shout over percussion alone, let it travel and adapt its message wherever it lands, and count the versions, so the song is a traveller and not a record. Against the chain: Quilapayun (#377 M6) also carried the same anthem, but theirs is the anthem closed with at every concert, and Ortega's is the anthem that leaves the concert and becomes a Ukrainian rap and an Iranian rising, which is why theirs is a closing and this is a travelling.

## M16. The unfinished return

Ortega comes home and does not stay, and the not-staying is the mechanism. In June 2003 he returns to Santiago to supervise a new staging of "Fulgor y muerte de Joaquin Murieta" at the Teatro Municipal, and the returning is the mechanism, because the opera he wrote at twenty-nine comes back to the Municipal at sixty-five, and the coming-back is the work, and the work is the return, and the return is the mechanism. A forum organised by the SCD records his opinions in a round table with his friends Fernando Garcia, Luis Advis and Horacio Salinas, and the recording is the mechanism, because the four composers sit together and the old man speaks, and the speaking is the return, and the return is the table, and the table is the mechanism. He speaks of the work he is developing with his son Chan~aral, an opera on the Mexican novel "Pedro Paramo", and the speaking is the mechanism, because the last work is a collaboration with his eldest son, and the son is the father, and the father is the opera, and the opera is the mechanism. He accepts to start conversations about temporary composition courses at the SCD School of Popular Music, and the accepting is the mechanism, because the teacher comes home to teach, and the teaching is the return, and the return is the school. He leaves for Finland, where "Joaquin Murieta" will be presented at the Savonlinna Opera Festival, and the leaving is the mechanism, because the opera travels and the composer follows it, and the following is the work, and the work is the travelling, and the travelling is the mechanism. Only weeks later he is diagnosed with advanced pancreatic cancer, and the diagnosing is the mechanism, because the return is cut short by the body, and the body is the return, and the return is the diagnosis. In September 2003 he is already in a coma at the Saint Louis hospital in Paris, and the coma is the mechanism, because the man who wrote the shout falls silent, and the silence is the composer, and the composer is the mechanism. He dies on Monday 15 September 2003, four days after the thirtieth anniversary of the coup, and the dying is the mechanism, because the date is the wound, and the wound is the death, and the death is the mechanism. His remains are waked at the Pantin town hall and return to Chile on Saturday 27 September 2003, and the returning is the mechanism, because the exile ends in a caravan with stops at the CUT and the Antonio Varas theatre and the Facultad de Artes and the Teatro Municipal, and the caravan is the return, and the return is the exile ended, and the exile ended is the mechanism. He is buried on Sunday 28 September 2003 in the Cementerio General, and the burying is the mechanism, because the composer who left in 1973 comes home to stay, and the staying is the burial, and the burial is the return. The posthumous editions of his work and reflections are still pending, and the pending is the mechanism, because the work waits for its editor, and the waiting is the return, and the return is the work. **Takeaway:** come home to stage the old opera, start the new one with your son, and let the return be cut short, so the burial is the last return and the work waits for its editor. Against the chain: Quilapayun (#377) also returned, but theirs is a group that comes back for the No campaign and stays to sing, and Ortega's is a man who comes back to stage an opera and dies four days after the anniversary, which is why theirs is a return and this is an ending.

## Evolution

The evolution is the soundman who becomes the anthem writer and then the exile workshop master, and the becoming has eight stages, and each stage keeps the one before it. From 1938 to 1960 he is the Antofagasta boy who learns the piano by ear at fifteen and tries architecture and literature before entering the Conservatory at eighteen, and the trying is the ear. From 1961 to 1964 he is the theatre soundman, composing for a dozen shows, working with CADIP and Skarmeta and Jaime Silva, hired at the Antonio Varas in 1963, writing the music for "Romeo y Julieta" in Neruda's translation in 1964 and for "El husar de la muerte" the same year, and the hiring is the booth. From 1965 to 1967 he is the theatre composer, writing "La dama del canasto" with Isidora Aguirre in 1965, the "Sexteto" in 1966, "Fulgor y muerte de Joaquin Murieta" in 1967, and arranging Jara's self-titled album in 1967, and the writing is the stage. From 1968 to 1970 he is the bridge, writing the "Sexteto" and "El viejo gallego trueba" and "Primeras noticias de mi muerte", scoring "El chacal de Nahueltoro" in 1969, teaching composition at the Conservatory in 1969 with Salinas and Rifo among his pupils, and writing "Canto al programa" with Advis and "Venceremos" in its hit version in 1970, and the teaching is the bridge. From 1970 to 1973 he is the most productive man in Chile, directing Canal 9 from 1970, composing at a frightening speed, writing the hymns of the CUT and the FECH and the Juventudes Comunistas, writing "La fragua" in 1972 and "El pueblo unido" on a night of May or June 1973, and the speed is the anthem. From 1973 to 1978 he is the exile, taking refuge in the Panama embassy, settling in Nanterre, building the Taller Recabarren, scoring the Studio H&S documentaries, writing "Chile resistencia" in 1976, and the building is the workshop. From 1978 to 1989 he is the workshop master and the school director, premiering "Bernardo O'Higgins Riquelme" at Graz on Thursday 26 October 1978, recording the "Venceremos" LP in 1978, writing "Los Cantos del Capitan" in 1982, directing the Ecole Nationale de Musique of Pantin from 1983, and writing the French Revolution trilogy for the Bicentennial in 1989, and the directing is the school. From 1990 to 2003 he is the opera composer and the returning father, premiering the two-act "Fulgor y muerte" opera on Monday 14 December 1998, writing a song for "Taxi para tres" in 2001, beginning "Pedro Paramo" with Chan~aral in 2003, returning in June 2003 to stage "Murieta" at the Municipal, and dying on Monday 15 September 2003, and the dying is the return.

## Aesthetic logic

The aesthetic logic is the bridge, the drama and the chronicle. The bridge is the learned and the popular in one hand, the "Vals peruano" of 1962 and the "Bossa nova" of 1963, the life spent finding the link, and the bridge is the ear that learned by itself and then took the conservatory, and the bridge is the soundman who writes the score, and the bridge is the song. The drama is the theatre that teaches the music its characters and dialogues and counterpoints, the Brecht and Eisler model, the lyric and the epic alternating in "El pueblo unido", the crowd described as absent while it is present, and the drama is the bass that walks under the shout, and the drama is the recitado that stops the song to tell what happened, and the drama is the song. The chronicle is the history hammered in "La fragua", the programme sung in "Canto al programa", the murdered woman whose body rose from the sea, the bastard liberator, the two enemies Lautaro and Valdivia, and the chronicle is the contingent song that answers the news in two days, and the chronicle is the travelling song that adapts its message in every country, and the chronicle is the work. The three hold together because the bridge lets the drama be popular and the chronicle be learned, and the drama lets the chronicle be sung, and the chronicle lets the bridge carry the history, and the carrying is the composer, and the composer is the song.
