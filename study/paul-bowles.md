## M1. Climaxless, hypnotic music

What it is: Bowles's own doctrine of incidental music, quoted in Wikipedia and PCMS Concerts, it allowed him to present "climaxless music, hypnotic music in one of the exact senses of the word, in that it makes its effect without the spectator being made aware of it", and the mechanism is fixed in the New York theatre years, 1937 to 1946, some twenty plays, Welles, Saroyan, Hellman, Williams, and the music never swells to a climax, and never announces itself, and yet changes the temperature of the room, and the audience feels the scene differently without knowing the music did it, and the watchable behaviour is a field that changes temperature without ever announcing itself, and the spectator affected without knowing why.

**Takeaway:** the music must change the temperature without announcing itself, and a score that swells to prove it is working has broken the hypnosis.

## M2. Economy of means, "simple but not minimalist"
