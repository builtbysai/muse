# Che-Yu Wu (吳哲宇) — study #209

**Identity:** Taiwanese new-media artist, musician and educator (b. 1995, Taipei;
NYU Integrated Digital Media). Instagram @cheyuwu345 (14,065 followers, 780
posts; his real handle was found via cheyuwu.com's footer after the bare
`cheyuwu` handle proved to be a private namesake — never guess handles). His
bio: "Creating Eternal Digital Life and Emotions". Was in the TAIPEI lineup of
NEORT's dialog() 2024 Asian generative art exhibition (niewmedia artist list:
Natalie.J / Yi-Wen LIN / Newyellow / Che-Yu Wu / Alvis Lee / Jinyao Lin /
PING SHENG WU / Chih-Yu Chen / Aluan Wang / eziraros) — i.e. he shared that
lineup with study-target #1 Chih-Yu Chen (CYC), whose IG handle could NOT be
verified (private/dead namesakes only) and who is therefore NOT the study
subject; this study is the network-adjacent win the expansion series is for.
This is a WEB+IG hybrid study: 33 works downloaded from cheyuwu.com/work/
(curatorial essay + full index), 19 visually inspected in depth, plus 43
stratified IG frames (ig_sample.json) with 4 more inspected.

**Core engine: THE SYSTEM IS THE WORK, AND THE WORK IS ALIVE.** Che-Yu Wu
treats every piece as a living rule-set that grows and *answers to people* —
his own quote: "I treat the system itself as the work. The images it produces
are just one kind of output" (May 2025 podcast). A fish is grown from a sine
wave and a hash; orchids regrow from parameters every 60 seconds; carbon data
weaves itself into a cloth; a pianist's notes grow a city while a virus
unmakes it; visitors' answers become frequencies in a twelve-metre wall of
water; thirty-five years of Taiwan's carbon data become cloth on an LED ring.
The world is the input — the day's weather, the people sitting in front of
the wall, the piano he plays. The image on the wall is a readout, never the
point.

**Signature devices (observed):**
1. **Generative impressionism — the algorithmic brush.** The Orchid program
   (p5.js): flower shape, buds, colour, stem curves set by parameters; the
   picture grows anew every 60 seconds. Exhibited as four upright AUO digital
   frames standing among real orchids and driftwood in a 321 Alley Art
   Settlement machiya room (frame w17: luminous impressionist orchid
   paintings in a dark wooden room with live plants). The Flower of the Other
   Shore (frame w14): spider lilies grown in about five seconds, each time in
   a new colour — hair-fine red radial petals on black. Urban Contour (frame
   w15): a night city painted in code — diagonal light-ribbons, a crescent
   moon, traffic-streaks like impasto — a different city each opening.
   Van Gogh's brush, he said in a 2021 talk, was generative art carried out
   by a person; his brush is a 3D landscape + lighting computed, then turned
   into stroke texture.
2. **Digital life from minimal math.** SoulFish (frame w26): "a fish grown
   from a sine wave and a hash" — a translucent cyan/blue creature with
   rippling body waves and fin trails, swimming alone on black; also
   sound-making, swam from fxhash into Taipei 101, across a touchscreen in
   Yilan, onto a wall in Venice (2024 Biennale parallel show). SoulSea: a sea
   of sine waves and noise "born of lonely subway rides to the New York
   shore", 300 editions minted in 30 seconds, projected at Taipei 101 and
   printed 10,000 pixels wide. The Soul (frame w30): an eye computed pixel by
   pixel in a shader, C-minor triad falling at random, written in a Lisbon
   conference hall in 2021 — projected across the floor of Taipei 101,
   minted as 100 copies in 2023.
3. **The world as input.** The Great Equation (frame w19): thirteen works
   computed LIVE, visitors located by millimetre-wave radar so the image
   unfolded around them; one wall projects the running p5.js source itself
   (readable lines: `whirls.map(b=>(b.p.x,b.p.y,b.distortForce,b.cl))`,
   `setUniform('u_time',millis()/1000)`, "Entropy" in giant type, firework
   particle bursts, marble-swirls on the floor). The Coffee Dreamscape: a
   live generative painting on a Starbucks Reserve wall — an imagined coffee
   estate that changes colour with Taipei's time and weather, trees growing
   mostly where people sit, a sea of bubbles in the colours of recent ORDERS
   sweeping over every twenty minutes (he refused Starbucks' offer to put an
   explanation of the algorithm beside it: "I don't want that place to
   become a high-tech venue"). Cymatics of Life (frame w03): a twelve-metre
   projected wall of water driven by visitors' answers — each response
   becomes a frequency, revealing a ripple mandala on the phone AND the wall
   at once (frame: deep-blue water with ~9 crisp concentric cymatic rings).
   Woven Land, Woven River (frame w02): thirty-five years of Taiwan's carbon
   data, Taoyuan's ponds and rivers, and visitors' daily choices become one
   continuously growing cloth on a ring of LED — dense red/green/blue
   thread-fields with floating palette swatches and a scroll of white cards.
4. **Music grows worlds.** The Last Input (frame w00): a 49.4-channel spatial
   sound performance, premiere July 2026 at C-LAB — the pianist feeds a
   living digital world where a City and a Virus grow, fight and evolve in
   real time, until the last input is given (frame: him at a keyboard,
   screen-filling projection of glowing amber wireframe towers on dark).
   The Eternal Garden in a Bottle (frame w07): a digital terrarium that
   breathes on its own — an ecosystem writes a live score, violinist and
   cellist improvise from it, their sound feeds the garden back (frame:
   grand piano in a dark room, walls and floor flooded with blue line-art:
   floating bubble-rings, plant forms, radiating root patterns).
5. **The daily practice as infrastructure.** April 2020, Brooklyn lockdown:
   he began writing a p5.js work every day — grew past 800 works,
   most published under CC on OpenProcessing. Many exhibition pieces began
   as one of those days. 240507: Reality Lens (frame w06): a daily sketch
   from 7 May 2024 — "fifteen invisible points twist every pixel in turn" —
   a psychedelic pixel-twisted marbling in orange, green, blue, magenta;
   FILE São Paulo 2026 section invite. The archive on his site: 347 records
   and counting.
6. **Cloud-chamber physics.** Electriz (frame w32): "a digital cloud chamber"
   on Art Blocks — 910 editions of particles tracing, splitting and
   vanishing across regions of different physics, minted out in 55 minutes
   (frame: three panels — gold streamers around a white disc on dark; red
   sun + navy/orange streaks; yellow-white sparks on black).
7. **Chaos you walk through.** Chaos Lab (frame w25): first solo exhibition
   (May 2022, AMBI SPACE ONE, Taipei 101) — a mad scientist's basement,
   projected on FIVE walls, performed live with p5.js and a drum machine.
   Frame: room flooded with overlapping giant type — "EXPERIMENT / CHAOS"
   repeated in white on dark, floor projection mirrored, a human silhouette
   at the center. Shape of Mind (frame w13): six ink-plotter drawings of
   spiral vortex forms (white-on-black and black-on-white) on a gallery wall
   with a pen plotter drawing one live on a plinth.
8. **Squishy on-chain creatures.** CryptoPochi (frame w33): 732 interactive
   NFTs on his own contract — soft squishy blocky characters (blue, coral,
   yellow, orange) on polka-dot red — draggable, told by on-chain events to
   jump or speak TOGETHER. Strange Robots → The Hamily: a 2021 daily work
   turned into an Art Blocks series selling 600+ pieces; when another artist
   released it, he negotiated a share, then hand-drew Hamily characters and
   gave them to the community (IG frame ig572: ~90 wonky-eared robot faces
   in a grid).
9. **Terrain cinema.** Project % · Island (frame w27): Project %, led by
   Mashbean and released by FAB DAO (July 2022), asked five Taiwanese
   generative artists to write ONE mountain each; Newyellow joined their
   ridgelines into one. Che-Yu Wu's is Island — shown on five upright
   screens at Ars Electronica Linz (frame: sunset-gradient mountainscapes,
   each with a small sun/moon); then at TCCF Taipei. Fairy Village: particles
   drift along a spiral force field and leave horizontal lines that read
   like weaving, overlaid with strokes for trees, windows and hills.

**Palette habits:** luminous-dark is the default — black grounds with
glowing bodies (SoulFish cyan, Soul iris violet-pink, Eternal Garden blue
line-work, spider lilies red, Reality Lens full-spectrum); exhibition
white-type on dark for the code-forward works; gold/purple for the physical
alcohol-ink paintings. One work, one palette, materials (glass, cloth,
water) doing the talking.

**Interaction stance:** the gardens, fish, orchids and cloth are watch-only
living systems — they live, you witness. The world-inputs (piano, visitors,
weather, orders, answers) EARN pointer input: in a browser piece the cursor
is the "visitor" — drag to disturb/answer, and the system responds as a
living thing would, not as a button. Tap-to-reseed belongs to the
seed-based mints (Electriz's regions, BioCode's seeds). Never
click-to-reload; never hidden controls; he shows the source code on the
wall, so fly pieces should show their mechanism openly.
