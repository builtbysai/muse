# Ayumu Nagamatsu (永松 歩) — study #208

**Identity:** Japanese creative coder / 3D proceduralist. Instagram @ayumu_naga
(1,770 followers, website ayumu-nagamatsu.com). Co-founded Processing Community
Day Tokyo with Naoto Hieda and Yasuto Nakanishi (confirmed in Hieda's PCD Tokyo
2020 report; he presented "how he became a creative-code freelancer" and is a
core PCD member). Works across Houdini (procedural 3D, rendered in Karma and
Octane), openFrameworks (live/math studies), TouchDesigner, and Unreal Engine
for VJ performance systems. This is an IG-ROUTE study: 101 posts pulled via
instagram-cli, 21 works visually inspected from stratified og:image extraction.

**Core engine: THE PROCEDURAL INSTRUMENT + MATH MADE TANGIBLE.** Nagamatsu
treats abstract mathematical structures — polyhedra and their duals, 4D
polytopes, fractals, Lenia, origami crease patterns, easing functions — as
instruments: named, legible systems he plays across media. The same geometry
appears as a render, a VJ loop, a 3D print, a cast silver ring, a projected
shadow, an exhibition key visual. Two signature moves recur: (1) the *modifier
stack* — one base polyhedron run through Noise / Voronoi / Triangulate / Woven
/ Voronoi Cell / Regular Cell / Recursive Subdiv, each step visible and named;
(2) the *projection game* — 4D→3D wireframe stereographic projections, 3D
objects whose 2D cast shadows are the real content, slit-scans turning a live
city into form. He builds the tool and the artwork is the tool being played.

**Signature devices (observed):**
1. **The modifier stack as composition.** "Modifiers for regular polyhedra"
   (Noise / Voronoi Surface / Triangulate Surface / Woven Surface / Voronoi
   Cell / Regular Cell / Recursive Subdiv): one polyhedron, deformations
   applied like a mixing desk. "Easing Catalogue": easing functions (Sine,
   Quad, Cubic, Quint, Expo × In/In-Out/Out) extruded as ribbons in glass
   boxes with labeled swatch bars — motion math as physical furniture.
2. **Duals and decomposition.** "Procedural geometry rendered in silver"
   (cast by @hamadayaro_): shapes from combining dual polyhedra as a net
   pattern, realized as a physical silver ring. "Decompose into a 3D Venn
   diagram": multiple sphere intersections separated into glass-and-matte
   petals on green. Octahedra decomposed by octahedra; parallelepipeds
   merging into rhombic hexahedra — boolean operations as choreography.
3. **4D wireframes.** Stereoscopic projections of the 600-Cell and 120-Cell,
   tesseract and 16-cell wireframes floating over grey — the nested-box
   projection as sculpture, dense white arcs on dark.
4. **Fractal solidity.** Mandelbox rendered as white architectural mass;
   "div(div(div(hexa)))": recursively subdivided pearlescent hexagonal quilt;
   "Platonic Coral": white recursive growth inside a glass wireframe shell —
   growth contained by symmetry.
5. **Frozen simulation.** "Smoke Sim to geometry": one frame of a blue fluid
   sim held still as glass with caustic refractions. Metaballs in lime and
   teal on pale ground — organic cellular forms, porcelain-still.
6. **Time-axis imaging.** Masked slit-scan of the Shibuya live cam (oF):
   segmented frames accumulated in time direction — a city rebuilt as
   streaks. Object detection masks over the same feed. "Motion Un-blur":
   extracting only the between-frames part.
7. **Live systems.** VJ setup automating Resolume via MaxMSP real-time audio
   analysis while launching his own apps; UE5 VJ rig (60 FPS on a laptop)
   with neon orbit rings on stage; "Follow the Dance" (Houdini + mocap) —
   directing dancers as a VFX artist. Physarum, GPU particles, strange
   attractors, Lenia ("from simple fractal noise, something that behaves
   autonomously, like a CA glider") in openFrameworks.

**Evolution (early vs recent):**
- **2024 (C8xxx shortcodes):** dialog() 2024 key visual (black/white particle
  field); openFrameworks math-and-live-data studies — Shibuya live-cam
  object detection + slit-scan, slit-scan studies sourced from Kabukicho
  livestreams, spidron origami sims, twisted rings, mocap dance, self-avoiding
  pathfinding walks, vertex-locus rotations. Raw experiment energy, code on
  the table.
- **Late 2024–early 2025 (C4xxx–C7xxx):** the polyhedra workshop era —
  wireframe 4D polytopes, Mandelbox, surface-fractal folding, the modifier
  stack posts "rendered for my workshop", dual-net geometries, 3D-print
  shadow studies ("printed wireframe sphere... fun to actually print"),
  "Casat objects", caustics studies, stylized MapBox maps. Teaching becomes
  part of the practice.
- **2025–2026 (Dxxx):** the catalogue era — Easing Catalogue, Metaball, Smoke
  Sim to geometry, Digital Minimalism, Earth as torus, Crystallization,
  Platonic Coral, all in production Houdini/Karma/Octane renders; then
  outward: "Re: Play" virtual city at CCBT (interactive, built from real
  dialogue logs that drive the world — "dialogue log... woven and circulated
  endlessly"), "Transcription" for Toda/ENCOUNTERS (real-time urban rhythms
  into light and sound, curated by Shunsuke Takawo), Silver Trail spin-off
  with Chih-Yu Chen (GitHub-collaborated, "loose rule-based" joint method),
  Yamaguchi hospital installation, Toda Building long-vision projection,
  hiragana rigging, UE5 VJing. The instrument becomes civic infrastructure.

**Palette habits:** monochrome restraint for the math studies — white/chrome
wireframes on neutral grey, black grounds for the wireframe era; then
production color for the catalogue era: deep blue smoke, lime/teal metaballs,
iridescent Lenia glitter, neon orange/white VJ loops on black, the green
Venn field. One idea, one palette per post; materials (glass, chrome, matte)
do the talking, not hue variety.

**Interaction stance:** renders are watch-only loops; the LIVE SYSTEMS earn
interaction — audio-driven VJ (the music is the input), slit-scan city feeds
(the city is the input), Re: Play's dialogue-log world (the visitors'
dialogues are the input), Transcription's urban rhythms. For fly pieces:
tap-to-reseed where a new mathematical seed belongs (new polytope, new
decomposition); drag only where the projection or the modifier stack is the
instrument (rotate the solid to steer its shadow; scrub the deformation
stack).
