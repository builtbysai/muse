# Syd Nuku: Study Notes

Study date: 2026-11-01. ig-artist-study #260.

**Site:** @sydnuku (1,103 followers, public, no verified badge; bio "I make recordings / @snorkelstudios"). Luke Kulukundis, artist name Syd Nuku (also signed "Syd Nukuluk" in his own captions), is a London film director, producer, composer, and the frontman of the experimental hip-hop five-piece Nukuluk. He is a producer-director inside the foreignbodyproductions / @foreignbodyfilm collective, an engineer/producer at Snorkel Studios in Forest Hill, and the connective tissue of the crew this study series has been circling: Mateo Villanueva Brandt's bandmate and co-director, Ciara Reddy's fellow production-design credit, Matty Mancey-Jones's repeat collaborator.

His practice has one through-line and it is the homemade stage. Every phase of his work builds a small, deliberate world and then watches it behave: a turquoise kitchen of arranged bodies, a child's bedroom with a cracked ceiling built so adults barely fit, an overhead pink tray where a flower arrangement smuggles in a joke, a point-and-shoot diary of vernal Britain. He co-directs, co-writes, co-produces, and composes what he films; the credit lists always run long, and he is always inside them. Where his closest collaborators isolate one discipline, he keeps the band, the set, and the studio in the same hand. The camera is deadpan, the room is small, and the event is arranged.

**Doorway:** Hop from the Mateo Villanueva Brandt study (#259): the two are repeat collaborators and bandmates, "my main comrade" in his own Ride Around caption, and the correction matters, they are not repeat co-directors. The one confirmed co-direction is Goat Girl's Ride Around (2024); on I Just Wanna Luv U (2023) Mateo was editor and 1st AD while Luke co-directed with Iso Attrill. Villanueva Brandt is the study of a cinematographer who constructs the event so the camera has something to be inside of. This study is about the collaborator who stages what goes inside the construction: the arranged tableau, the built miniature room, the homemade universe. Same crews, same sets, the complementary instrument. It also closes a causal loop in the evidence: the 2022 caption "Mateo gave me my first point and shoot for my birthday" is the origin of the diary register that runs the entire second half of this study.

**Depth:** the full own-post feed was pulled cleanly (101 posts, 2014-07-08 to 2025-12-05, captions included; a same-session control on @foreignbodyproductions confirmed the feed is genuine). 19 frames were stratified across all eleven years, JPEG-verified, and visually inspected frame by frame. Web leg: four search rounds; the Still Listening Magazine band interview (2023-04-21) was read end to end; Goat Girl press (Far Out, The Upcoming, Clash, live4ever, Stereoboard) and the Athens Short Film Festival synopsis read in full. ## M1. Locked deadpan tableau, the Andersson grammar

The signature mechanism, and it was named explicitly by the maker. Goat Girl's Ride Around (2024, f04) was "built around Roy Andersson references" in his own caption; Goat Girl arrived with "a Pinterest board featuring the work of Roy Andersson" (Far Out); the video trades "lonely benches, group therapy sessions and ballet classes" for a disco-ball idea that never survived. The directors' own statement: the film explores "outsiderdom via both desire and fear of connection. The strangeness of togetherness in the context of a drab city, mired in capitalist claustrophobia and absurd mundanity" (live4ever). The still (f04): a static turquoise kitchen, the singer seated at a table with eggs, a bald suited man arranged at the right like furniture. But the grammar predates the commission by years. f15 (2019, Data X Change): a deadpan infomercial tableau, smiling salesman in tan suit and red tie against a red curtain, a table of knives, direct camera address. And the citation is older still: his 2018-09-21 post (f16) is a still from Songs From The Second Floor, quoted with admiration, roughly five and a half years before the Goat Girl video. The mechanism is exact: lock the camera, arrange bodies inside a drab room as if they were furniture, let the absurdity be quiet, and never move. The joke and the melancholy are the same shot.

**Takeaway:** lock the camera, arrange people like furniture in a drab room, let the absurdity stay quiet.

## M2. Miniature, wrong-scale room universes

The mechanism behind his most quoted production story. Nukuluk's I Just Wanna Luv U (2023, f06): the video was built around a scary crack in the ceiling of his childhood bedroom, and the crew, led by production designer Matty Mancey-Jones, built a miniature child's bedroom "which all the band members could barely fit inside of", stocked with 90s toys (his own words, Still Listening). The frame: the full band crammed into the tiny blue room, pyjamas on, feet up on the door, a Korg on the bed, walls papered with posters and photos. The cited reference points, from the liner notes and interview, are Gondry, Kaufman, and Lynch: the lineage of the homemade miniature as psychological space. The watchable behavior is physical comedy at true scale: adult bodies failing to fit a child's world, forced perspective gags, the room visibly too small for the performance inside it. This is the build register at its most literal, and it is the one mechanism in the study that visibly earns the viewer's hand: a wrong-scale room wants to be tilted, squeezed, made smaller.

**Takeaway:** build the room too small for the bodies, then let the misfit be the whole show.

## M3. Point-and-shoot diary, the post-2022 register

The second signature, and it has a documented origin story. 2022-04-28 (f08): "Mateo gave me my first point and shoot for my birthday and now I am young and sentimental forever." The cover: black-and-white film, a woman dancing mid-air in a crowded park. It became an annual series with travel dates as titles: 2023's summer run ("cities all summer that I pointed and shot at whilst hunting Bransoms, Yaxleys and Boiled Eggs"), 2024's "Britain in colour, 2023 + 24" (f03, beached boat in dune grass, West Norwood allotments, Tebay services, Vauxhall bridge), 2025's Lisbon park frame (f01, taken on the first phone call about the Sa Baile project) and the latest post, a ferry deck at golden hour (f00, 2025-12-05). The subjects are vernacular: hikers, services stations, beaches, boats. The mechanism: the cheap camera is the style, not a limitation of it. Grain, flash, plain film are welcomed as the finish. Eleven years of archive obsession (the gramophone in f18, 2014) compressed into one disciplined habit: shoot the ordinary on film, date it, keep going.

**Takeaway:** one cheap camera, vernacular subjects, travel dates as titles; the grain is the finish.

## M4. Welcomed film accident

The corollary of M3 and the most economical mechanism in the study. f07 (2022): "leaking light all over adirondack mountains and my grandfather in august." The cover is a film fault kept as the work: red-orange fog, bare tree silhouettes, a blown-out left edge where the light leak eats the frame. f12 (2021, Nukuluk formation): a warm red double-exposure group portrait, layered faces melting into each other. In both, the camera's failure is the composition. He does not correct the leak or the double exposure; he captions it and posts it. The distinction from M3 matters: the diary is a discipline, the accident is a surrender. One says the cheap camera is the style; the other says the broken camera is the image.

**Takeaway:** keep the light leak, caption the fault, let the camera's failure compose the frame.

## M5. Sterile-space service comedy

The short-film register, and the study's purest gag structure. All Of A Sudden I Began To Melt (2022, f09), co-written, produced, and composed by him, directed by Ruth Waters: "a short art-comedy film exploring luxury wellness, anxiety and the class politics of wotsits... the entirety of the film takes place over a massage treatment that is flipped on its head by an unwanted visitor floating in a flower arrangement" (Athens Short Film Festival synopsis). His own framing: "power dynamics of luxury service are flipped on its head by an empty packet of Wotsits." The still is the exact gag object: an overhead pink tray of water, floating dahlia heads, a small yellow toy, shot deadpan-overhead like a product photo. The mechanism: take a sterile service space (the wellness suite), hold it in the deadpan tableau grammar of M1, then admit one low-status intruder object (the crisp packet, the floating toy) that detonates the whole register. The comedy is class-coded, but the visual rule is plain: the room stays immaculate, the object stays trashy, the camera stays still, the flip lands.

**Takeaway:** hold a sterile room perfectly still, then admit one low-status object and let it detonate the register.

## M6. Intimate documentary portraiture

The serious register, and the award validates it. Zagon (2019-2021, f11): a year-long documentary of Vincent, "a 78-year-old shaped by his early life experiences of Nazi occupation and Soviet communism", made with Mateo and the foreignbodyproductions graph; it won an RTS award (his 2021-06-25 post). The watchable behavior is portraiture of endurance: an undiscovered artist "running out of time", filmed patiently. The cover is a laughing selfie with Vincent in an office, which is telling: the mechanism is presence, not style. No trick, no tableau, no wash. After five mechanisms of staged image-making, this one earns its place by subtraction: point the camera at one person for a year and stay.

**Takeaway:** one person, one year, no tricks; let endurance be the whole portrait.

## M7. Sonic-materialist short film

The through-line that makes the other six cohere. He composes what he directs: co-writer/producer/composer on All Of A Sudden, producer and mixer on the Nukuluk singles, co-producer/mixer of Sa Baile's debut (2025). The two short-film cases: If At Last, A Happy Man (2018, f17), a figure in a red poncho walking down a lush green hillside garden, "written and shot in the days leading up to" a gathering in Shipton Gorge, painterly fairytale register; and Stones In Hand (2021, f13), made with Mo'min Swaitat and Amy Douglas-Morris, "getting to remix Mo'min's incredible cassette archive of music from the Palestinian intifada... creating our own magic realist universe in Leigh Marshes." The mechanism: the archive is the material. A cassette collection, a box of phonograph recordings (his father's wax cylinders sampled on Hand On The Hilt, f02), an old tape becomes the score, and the image is built to house the sound. The gramophone post from 2014 (f18, "Found a big fucking gramophone") turns out to be the thesis statement of the whole eleven-year feed: the sound object comes first, the film follows.

**Takeaway:** start from a found sound object, build the image to house it, let the archive score the film.

## Evolution

- **2014: the archive teen.** Earliest post (f18, 2014-07-08): a big gramophone in a book-lined room. The sound-object fixation is present from day one.
- **2017-2018: travel diary and the first short.** Kiev, Brixton, hikes; If At Last, A Happy Man (2018-01-11); the Roy Andersson citation (2018-09-21); the Soccer96 video (2018-05-15).
- **2019: the theatre turn.** Voice In The Crowd with Tyreke Leslie; the Data X Change EP on Slowfoot (Jan 2020) with first co-directed videos (with @arthurstuds, f15); Zagon shooting; community workshops.
- **2020-2021: the generalist peak.** Zagon streams and the RTS award (2021-06-25); Nukuluk formed (2021-03-01); Stones In Hand (Jan 2021); Open Source Collaborations; DISASTER POP (Nov 2021).
- **2022: the point-and-shoot era.** Mateo's birthday gift (Apr 2022) starts the diary register that runs to the latest post; All Of A Sudden I Began To Melt opens (Feb 2022); the Adirondacks light leak (Dec 2022).
- **2023: the video peak.** I Just Wanna Luv U (Mar 2023, co-directed with Iso Attrill); SUPERGLUE EP (Apr 2023); his father's death (Dec 2023).
- **2024: the comrade credit.** Ride Around (Feb 2024), the one confirmed co-direction with Mateo, Rough Trade single; Hand On The Hilt (Jul 2024), produced around his father's Roland JDXA; the monastery remembrance (Oct 2024).
- **2025: the studio elder.** Co-producer/mixer of Sa Baile's debut (Mar 2025); Snorkel residency; the diary continues to the ferry-deck latest post (2025-12-05).

The arc: archive-obsessed teen, theatre and documentary generalist (2018-2021), band-leader and co-director (2021-2024, the Nukuluk and Foreign Body video peak), producer-engineer elder (2024-2025, studio residency, album co-production, the diarist's habit). The through-line never moves: build the small world, arrange the bodies, welcome the accident, score it from the archive.

## Aesthetic logic

Everything Luke Kulukundis makes is a small built world with the camera held still inside it. The room is too small or too drab to contain the performance, and that is the performance. He locks the camera in the Andersson grammar and arranges bodies like furniture; he builds a child's bedroom for adults; he holds a wellness suite immaculate until a crisp packet detonates it; he points a cheap film camera at the ordinary and dates the frame. When the camera fails, he keeps the failure. When the archive offers itself, a gramophone, a ceiling crack, a box of Palestinian cassettes, his father's synthesizer, he builds the image to house the sound. He is the study series' counterpart to Villanueva Brandt's constructive eye and Reddy's positional eye: where the cinematographer builds the event and the photographer stays inside it, the director stages what happens inside the build. The long credit lists are not a lack of authorship; they are the authorship. The homemade universe only works if everybody is in the room.
