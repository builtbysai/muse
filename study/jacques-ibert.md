## M1. Orchestral colour as the argument

What it is: timbre before theme, and Escales opens with shimmering tremolo muted violins and a solo flute over harp dashes, and the Divertissement juggles a 15-player chamber orchestra, "an infallible juggler of timbres", per Le Figaro, so that a whistle, a wood block and a piano and celesta carry jokes a full orchestra could not, and the watchable behaviour is colour changes doing the work that melody does in other composers, and a new timbre arriving is the event, not the accompaniment to one.

**Takeaway:** the colour must do the arguing, and a piece whose timbres only dress its tunes has not made colour the argument.
