## Core engine

**Turning poetry into embodied vocal sound.** A literary text is the compositional seed, and her voice operates on a parola-canto continuum of speech, song, breath and noise, delivered through small-group chamber improvisation with a staged, theatrical presentation per project.

Three sub-mechanisms:

1. **Poem-as-score.** The text seeds the piece, not a lead sheet. Delivery alternates speech, song and noise across the text; syllable density becomes note density. Mechanism: a text-to-phoneme engine where the literary object dictates the rhythmic and timbral budget.
2. **The chamber swap.** In her small groups the voice leads, then dissolves into texture, then leads again. Mechanism: rotating leadership in a multi-agent improvising system, leader and follower roles handed off without a stop.
3. **Site as constraint.** Nuraghi, mountain parks, "lunch with the artists" formats, theatrical venues with the Raminghe company. Mechanism: place-derived constraints (acoustics, ritual timing, itinerant routes) shape the piece before a note is sung.
