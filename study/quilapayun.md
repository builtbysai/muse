# Quilapayun: Study Notes

**Site:** Quilapayun (from the Mapudungun words quila, three, and payun, beards, the three beards, taken by three bearded university students), the Chilean vocal and instrumental ensemble of the Nueva Cancion Chilena, formed on Monday 26 July 1965 in Santiago by Numhauser, Julio Carrasco and Eduardo Carrasco, the last of the three a "temporary" member who stayed, all of them students at the Universidad de Chile, the group that won the black ponchos and the first prize at the Primer Festival Nacional del Folklore "Chile Multiple" in 1966 and that Victor Jara took as his artistic direction from 1966 to 1969, the group that recorded the Cantata Santa Maria de Iquique in 1970 and "El pueblo unido jamas sera vencido" live in June 1973, the group that was on tour in Europe when the coup of Tuesday 11 September 1973 happened and sang at the Olympia in Paris on Saturday 15 September 1973, four days later, the group that lived fifteen years in exile in Colombes outside Paris and that returned for the No campaign and the plebiscite of Wednesday 5 October 1988, the group that broke with the Communist Party in the late 1970s under the influence of the painter Roberto Matta and that split into two groups under one name in 2002 to 2004 and fought over the name in the courts of France and Chile, the group whose Cantata was sung with women's voices in December 2021 and whose Neruda cantata "Fulgor y muerte de Joaquin Murieta" was finished in 2022, fifty-five years after the project began, the group that is still singing in 2026, sixty-one years on, with the sons of its dead singers on stage. ## M1. The three beards

The name is the uniform, and the uniform is the mechanism. Quila is three and payun is beards in Mapudungun, and three bearded university students take the name in 1965, and the taking is the mechanism, because the name says the group before the group sings, and the saying is the image, and the image is the three beards, and the beards are the group. The black ponchos complete the uniform, and the completing is the mechanism, because the ponchos are won, not bought, at the Primer Festival Nacional del Folklore "Chile Multiple" in 1966, where the group takes the first prize and the "La Guitarra de Oro", and the winning is the uniform, and the uniform is the prize, and the prize is the poncho. A row of identical dark figures, beards and ponchos, reads at a distance, and the reading is the mechanism, because the group is the image and no single face leads, and the not-leading is the group, and the group is the row, and the row is the mechanism. The uniform loosens with time, and the loosening is the mechanism too, because in q-01, the 2017 group photograph, six men stand in dark suits at microphones against a blue backdrop, two holding quenas and two playing classical guitars, and there are no ponchos, and the no-ponchos is the late form, and the late form is still a row, and the row is the group, and the group is the mechanism; and in q-02, the Victor Jara stage of 27 January 2018, the band is small and distant in black ponchos at sunset, and the ponchos return for the festival, and the returning is the uniform, and the uniform is the occasion, and the occasion is the poncho. **Takeaway:** take a name that is a picture, win a uniform to wear with it, and let the row be the image so no single face leads, and let the uniform loosen but never let the row break. Against the chain: Inti-Illimani (#373 M13) also wears the poncho and trades the instruments inside it, but theirs is a workshop where the instruments pass from hand to hand, and Quilapayun's is a row where the voices stand in formation, which is why theirs is a swap and this is a rank.

## M2. The theatre director

Jara runs the show as a stage production, and the staging is the mechanism. Quilapayun meets Victor Jara at a pen~a in Valparaiso in 1966 and asks him to become their artistic director, and the asking is the mechanism, because he holds the post until 1969, and the holding is three years, and the three years are the discipline, and the discipline is the staging, and the staging is the group. He stages the shows as theatre, and the theatre is the mechanism, because he decides the lighting and the entrances and who speaks and how it is said and the silence and the timing, and the deciding is the show, and the show is the play, and the play is the concert, and the concert is the mechanism. Carrasco remembers it as a studied mechanism: "Era un mecanismo estudiado en que se abria el telon y comenzaba a funcionar", the curtain opens and the mechanism starts, and the opening is the mechanism, and the mechanism is the curtain, and the curtain is the show. He presents them to Odeon, and the presenting is the mechanism, because they record five LPs there, and the five are the discipline made audible, and the audible is the staging, and the staging is the record. The discipline does not leave when Jara leaves, and the not-leaving is the mechanism, because in exile the French theatre director Daniel Mesguich works with the group and deepens the same discipline, and the deepening is the staging, and the staging is the exile, and the exile is the theatre. A concert that behaves like a play is the watchable behaviour: blackouts between numbers, spoken introductions placed like scenes, the group entering as a cast, and the entering is the mechanism, because the cast is the group, and the group is the play, and the play is the concert. **Takeaway:** let a theatre director stage the song, let the curtain be the mechanism, and let the discipline outlive the director, so the concert is a play even when the director is gone. Against the chain: Jara (#370 M1) also staged the song, but his is a director who stages his own songs and then steps forward to sing them, and Quilapayun's is a director who stages other men and then leaves them staged, which is why his is a staging of the self and theirs is a staging that stays.

## M3. The internationalist LP

"Basta" (1969) is an anthology, and the anthology is the argument, and the argument is the mechanism. It is the fifth album and the first without Jara as musical director, and the first-without is the mechanism, because the group must now state its own programme, and the stating is the twelve tracks, and the twelve are the map, and the map is the argument. The twelve come from Colombia and Cuba and Italy and Argentina and the USSR and Mexico and Chile, and the coming is the mechanism, because "A la mina no voy" is Colombian popular song and "La muralla" is Guillen and "La gaviota" is Huasi and Carrasco and "Bella ciao" is Italian popular song and "Coplas de baguala" and "Cueca de Balmaceda" and "Por montan~as y praderas" is a Soviet guerrilla hymn and "La carta" and "Por que los pobres no tienen" are Violeta Parra and "Carabina 30-30" is the Mexican Revolution and "Patron" is Sampayo and "Basta ya!" is Yupanqui, and the listing is the mechanism, because each song arrives from a different country and the set accumulates into a map, and the map is the internationalism, and the internationalism is the album. The sleeve carries a long written statement, and the statement is the mechanism, because it says bourgeois society wants art to be another factor contributing to social alienation, and the saying is the programme, and the programme is the sleeve, and the sleeve is the album, and the album is the mechanism. The watchable behaviour is the set that accumulates: each song arrives from a different country, and the arriving is the map, and the map is the concert, and the concert is the world. **Takeaway:** make the anthology the argument, put the programme on the sleeve in writing, and let twelve songs from seven countries be the map, so the album is the world before a single note is played. Against the chain: Inti-Illimani (#373 M3) also toured nine countries and learned the music at first hand, but theirs is a census that collects the continent by travelling it, and Quilapayun's is an anthology that collects it by pressing it, which is why theirs is a tour and this is a record.

## M4. The wall that sings

"La muralla" sets Guillen's poem as a choral wall, and the wall is the mechanism. The poem says the wall must be built of rose and jasmine and song, against racial difference, and the saying is the mechanism, because the wall is built of fraternity and not of stone, and the fraternity is the singing, and the singing is the wall, and the wall is the mechanism. The music is Quilapayun's and the text is Nicolas Guillen's, and the pairing is the mechanism, because a Cuban poem about race becomes a Chilean chorus about the same wall, and the becoming is the wall, and the wall is the chorus, and the chorus is the mechanism. The song is on "Basta" in 1969, and the placing is the mechanism, because the wall stands in the middle of the internationalist anthology, and the standing is the argument, and the argument is the wall. The voices stack course by course, and the stacking is the mechanism, because the wall rises in sound while the words refuse a wall of exclusion, and the refusing is the singing, and the singing is the wall, and the wall is the mechanism. The song travels, and the travelling is the mechanism, because it was already known in Spain through Ana Belen's version before the exile, and a French television version was recorded on Wednesday 28 September 2005, the eve of the Trianon concert in Paris, and the recording is the wall, and the wall is the travelling, and the travelling is the song. **Takeaway:** build the wall out of voices, course by course, and let the words refuse the wall of exclusion while the singing builds the wall of fraternity, so the wall is the song. Against the chain: Violeta Parra (#369) also built with her hands, but hers is an arpillera stitched of cloth and a song collected in the countryside, and Quilapayun's is a wall built of many voices at once, which is why hers is a stitching and this is a stacking.

## M5. The narrator inside the song

The Cantata's eighteen parts alternate five spoken relatos with songs and interludes, and the alternating is the mechanism. The Cantata Santa Maria de Iquique is composed in late 1969 by Luis Advis, recorded in 1969 by the infobox and released in 1970 on DICAP JJL-08, thirty-seven minutes and fifteen seconds, and the eighteen parts are two pregones and a prelude and three instrumental interludes and five spoken relatos and seven songs, and the counting is the mechanism, because the relatos carry the history and the songs carry the feeling, and the carrying is the work, and the work is the cantata, and the cantata is the mechanism. The relatos tell the massacre of Saturday 21 December 1907, when General Silva Renard's troops fired on the striking nitrate workers at the Santa Maria school in Iquique, and the telling is the mechanism, because the audience is told the history before it is sung, and the telling is the song, and the song is the history, and the history is the mechanism. The narrator is a role, not an introduction, and the role is the mechanism, because Hector Duvauchelle speaks on the record and Marcelo Romo narrated the premiere and Roberto Parada later and Hector Noguera at Santa Laura on Saturday 1 November 1997, and the changing of the voice is the role, and the role is the narrator, and the narrator is the mechanism. The first presentation is July 1970 at the Teatro La Reforma, and the official premiere is August 1970 at the Second Festival of the Nueva Cancion at the Estadio Chile, and the two premieres are both true, and the both-true is the mechanism, because a work this large needs two births, and the births are the cantata, and the cantata is the mechanism. The songs carry the feeling inside the told history: "Vamos mujer" and "Soy obrero pampino" and the letania "Murieron tres mil seiscientos" and the final "Ustedes que ya escucharon", and the carrying is the mechanism, because a speaking voice stops the music, tells what happened, and the singing resumes inside the story, and the resuming is the history, and the history is the song. Rolling Stone Chile ranked it fourth among the 50 best Chilean albums in April 2008, and the ranking is the afterlife, and the afterlife is the mechanism, because the cantata outlives its occasion, and the outliving is the work. **Takeaway:** put a speaking voice inside the sung work, let the narrator stop the music and tell what happened, and let the singing resume inside the story, so the audience hears the history before it sings it. Against the chain: Jara (#370 M8) also put recorded testimony inside the album, but his is the pobladores speaking for themselves on "La Poblacion", and Quilapayun's is an actor speaking the history for the singers, which is why his is a testimony and this is a narration.

## M6. The anthem that closes

"El pueblo unido jamas sera vencido" is a march, and the march is the mechanism, and the closing is the mechanism, because it is placed last in every concert, and the last is the ritual, and the ritual is the anthem, and the anthem is the mechanism. The music is Sergio Ortega's and the text was written jointly with Quilapayun, and the joint writing is the mechanism, because the division of the labour is disputed, the Spanish Wikipedia giving the music to Ortega and the text to the joint writing, and another account giving Ortega the music and the lyric with Quilapayun's text and Carrasco as co-author and musical director, and the disputing is carried and not settled, and the carrying is the honesty, and the honesty is the anthem. The chorus is a shout with percussion only, and the shout is the mechanism, because it travels into any language, and the travelling is the chorus, and the chorus is the shout, and the shout is the anthem. The first live recording is June 1973 at the Primer Festival Internacional de la Cancion Popular at the Estadio Chile, closing the DICAP live album, and the closing is the mechanism, because the festival ends with the shout, and the shout is the festival, and the festival is the anthem. The closing repeats everywhere, and the repeating is the ritual: Quilapayun closes the 1974 Finnish live album "Yhtenais ta kansaa ei voi koskaan voittaa", recorded in Helsinki on Friday 31 August and Sunday 2 September 1973 with Agit-Prop; and the Essen concert on Friday 31 May 1974 with Inti-Illimani; and the Royal Albert Hall tribute to Jara on Tuesday 30 September 1975; and the Luna Park concerts in Buenos Aires from Wednesday 23 November 1983; and the return concerts in Chile in 1989, and the closing is the same every time, and the same is the ritual, and the ritual is the concert. The afterlife is the mechanism too, because Frederic Rzewski wrote 36 piano variations on it in 1975, and more than 150 versions have been recorded worldwide, and the 150 are the anthem, and the anthem is the world, and the world is the shout. **Takeaway:** write a chorus that is a shout over percussion alone, put it last in every concert, and never vary the ending, so the concert ends the same way every time and the ending is a ritual, not a song. Against the chain: Inti-Illimani (#373 M6) also closes with the same anthem, but theirs is the anthem carried into Italy and sung by a million people in the Plaza Dignidad in November 2019, and Quilapayun's is the anthem written with its own hands and closed with at the festival three months before the coup, which is why theirs is a carrying and this is a writing.

## M7. The reusable tune

"La batea" is a rocking trough, and the rocking is the mechanism, because the same notes carry new lyrics for each occasion, and the carrying is the vessel, and the vessel is the tune, and the tune is the mechanism. "Mira la batea, como se menea, como se menea el agua en la batea", and the rocking is the water, and the water is the trough, and the trough is the song, and the song is the mechanism. The single is 1971, DICAP JJS-109, with "Soy del pueblo" by Carlos Puebla, and the first version sings "El gobierno va marchando, que felicidad... Por el paso de Uspallata, que barbaridad", and the later version sings "Por los cinco continentes... Para Chile hay que ponerse a trabajar", and the changing is the mechanism, because the melody stays and the verse is rewritten for the occasion, and the rewriting is the tune, and the tune is the vessel, and the vessel is the news. The song also appears on "Adelante" in 1975, in the single "La batea / Tio Caiman", and the reappearing is the mechanism, because a familiar melody returns with different words and the audience recognises the tune before it hears the news, and the recognising is the tune, and the tune is the news, and the news is the song. The two lyric variants are both attested and are not merged, and the not-merging is the honesty, and the honesty is the tune. **Takeaway:** keep the melody fixed and rewrite the verse for the occasion, so the audience recognises the tune before it hears the news, and let the tune be a vessel that any verse can be poured into. Against the chain: Yupanqui (#371) also reused the collected form, but his is a form collected on muleback and refilled once with his own words, and Quilapayun's is a tune refilled again and again for each occasion, which is why his is a refilling and this is a reusing.

## M8. The waltz of gratitude

"El vals de Colombes" is a waltz for the town that housed them, and the waltz is the mechanism, because the thanks are set as a dance rather than a speech, and the dance is the thanks, and the thanks are the town, and the town is the waltz. The zampen~as imitate the barrel organs of the Paris street musicians, and the imitating is the mechanism, because Andean flutes play a Paris waltz, and the playing is two places in one tune, and the two places are the exile, and the exile is the waltz, and the waltz is the mechanism. The group arrives in Colombes outside Paris in mid-October 1973, and the arriving is the mechanism, because the mayor Dominique Frelaut offers them a flat in a new building after their Colombes concert, and the offering is the housing, and the housing is the town, and the town is the exile, and the exile is the waltz. They live in Colombes more than fifteen years, and the living is the mechanism, because the waltz is composed from mid-October 1973 onward, in the first months of the exile, and the composing is the thanks, and the thanks are the years, and the years are the waltz. A photograph of the waltz exists in the official site gallery, Punta Arenas, 25 January 1989, and the photograph is the return, and the return is the waltz, and the waltz is the thanks. **Takeaway:** thank a town with a dance, not a speech, and let the flutes of one place play the waltz of another, so two places live in one tune. Against the chain: Inti-Illimani (#373 M10) also thanked Italy, but theirs is a joke about the belly of the monster and a pension paid back within a year, and Quilapayun's is a waltz for the mayor's flat, which is why theirs is a debt and this is a dance.

## M9. The workshop album

"Adelante" (1975) is the product of an internal workshop, and the workshop is the mechanism. Exile forces the group to stop being interpreters of other people's songs and become authors, and the forcing is the mechanism, because the songs they used to sing are now behind a border, and the border is the exile, and the exile is the authorship, and the authorship is the workshop. Most members compose on the album, and the most is the mechanism, because the workshop is run by Carrasco and Oddo and Parada and Gomez and Lagos are credited, and the crediting is the workshop, and the workshop is the album, and the album is the mechanism. The same hands that play now write, and the writing is the mechanism, because the set is signed from inside, and the inside is the group, and the group is the authorship, and the authorship is the set. The turn is stated in the group's own account of "Con el alma llena de banderas": "pasamos a ser los creadores de nuestras canciones", we passed to being the creators of our songs, and the passing is the mechanism, because the interpreters become the authors, and the authors are the exiles, and the exiles are the creators, and the creators are the group. **Takeaway:** let exile force the interpreters to become authors, run the album as a workshop where most members compose, and let the set be signed from inside.
