## M1. The sacralization of politics: the sacred moves house

The transfer of sacredness from traditional religion to a worldly political entity, so that the nation, the state, the party or the leader is surrounded with an aura of sacredness and becomes an object of worship. Il culto del littorio. La sacralizzazione della politica nell'Italia fascista, Laterza, 1993, English The Sacralization of Politics in Fascist Italy, Harvard University Press, 1996, translated by Keith Botsford, is the core book, and Le religioni della politica. Fra democrazie e totalitarismi, Laterza, 2001, English Politics as Religion, Princeton University Press, 2006, translated by George Staunton, is the general theory, and the mechanism is that modernisation and secularisation did not kill the sacred, they displaced it, and the displacement is the work, because the sacred migrates, it does not vanish, and when the church empties, the square fills, and the square fills with flags treated as relics, oaths as prayers, a political rally staged as a liturgy with processions, silence and acclamation, and the transfer is visible, because the gestures are the same gestures, only the object has changed, and the object is now the nation, and the nation is now sacred, and the sacredness is real, because the crowd feels it, and the feeling is the proof, and the proof is the kneeling, because a crowd that does not debate but kneels has already changed its religion, and the religion is the politics, and the politics is the religion. The watchable behaviour is a crowd that does not debate but kneels, flags treated as relics, oaths as prayers, a rally staged as a liturgy.

**Takeaway:** let the sacred move house, and the piece should show the crowd kneeling to a flag as if it were a relic.

## M2. Political religion: a church with no god

Gentile's definition, quoted in the review essay of Politics as Religion: "a more or less developed system of beliefs, myths, rituals and symbols that creates an aura of sacredness around an entity belonging to the world and turns it into a cult or object of worship or devotion". A political religion is totalitarian when it rests on monopoly of power, ideological monism and unconditional subordination, and the mechanism is that the system has everything a religion has, dogmas, commandments, heretics and excommunications, but no god, and the no-god is the point, because the sacred object is the nation or the leader, and the nation or the leader is of this world, and the worldliness is the danger, because a god can be argued with only through scripture, and a leader can be argued with not at all, and the system is more or less developed, and the development is the history, because a movement begins with a myth and ends with a catechism, and the catechism is the dogma, and the dogma is the commandment, and the commandment is the heresy trial, and the trial is the excommunication, and the excommunication is the proof that the system has become a religion, and the religion has no god, and the no-god is the leader, and the leader is the god. The watchable behaviour is a movement with dogmas, commandments, heretics and excommunications, but no god, the sacred object being the nation or the leader.

**Takeaway:** build the church with no god, and the piece should let the heresy trial prove the religion.
