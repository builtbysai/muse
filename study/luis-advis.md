# Luis Advis: Study Notes

**Site:** Luis Archibaldo de Lourdes Advis Vitaglich (Iquique, Sunday 10 February 1935, Santiago, Thursday 9 September 2004), the Chilean composer and professor of philosophy and aesthetics, the conservatory man who brought classical form into the Nueva Cancion, the inventor of the "cantata popular", the man who wrote the Cantata Santa Maria de Iquique in 1970 and then spent the rest of his life refusing to be only the man who wrote it, the teacher of aesthetics who never lived from music and never belonged to a party and never left Chile, the composer who was annoyed by a bolero until he was thirty and then learned the mambo from a novel, the fast hand who wrote a film score in two nights and a symphony in sixteen years, the man who disowned the one work he made with his twin and called his most famous work dramatically valuable and musically normal. He composed the Cantata Santa Maria de Iquique in the winter of 1969 to 1970 and Quilapayun sang it, and he set Violeta Parra's Decimas as Canto para una semilla in 1972 and Inti-Illimani sang it with Isabel Parra singing her mother's life, and he wrote the government programme to music with Sergio Ortega as Canto al programa in 1970 and then said the music of it was very bad and very mediocre and he did not consider it a nameable work at all, and he finished Los tres tiempos de America in 1986 and it was premiered on Friday 5 August 1988 in the Roman theatre of Merida with Paloma San Basilio and Quilapayun before more than three thousand five hundred people, and he wrote about ninety theatre scores of which most were never archived and are lost, and he wrote his last oratorio, La pampa del Tamarugal, a few weeks before he died of cancer at sixty-nine, and at his death he was preparing two books, one on the music of Violeta Parra and one on the Nueva Ola, and five songs for a Quilapayun album that came out in 2007 as Siempre, and Gabriela Mistral's poetry was recited at his funeral. The permission is the second thread, because Ortega came to Advis afterwards and asked, ironically, "Maestro, me da permiso para hacer una cantata?", and Advis answered, "hagala no mas, maestro", and then Ortega made La fragua, and the asking is the doorway, and the answering is the doorway, and the permission is the model and the answer. The ensembles are the fourth thread, because both men wrote their large works for the same two groups, Advis's Cantata and Los tres tiempos for Quilapayun and Canto para una semilla and Canto al programa for Inti-Illimani, Ortega's La fragua and El pueblo unido for Quilapayun and Canto al programa for Inti, and the same two ensembles are the doorway. The table is the fifth thread, because both men sat at the June 2003 SCD round table with Fernando Garcia and Horacio Salinas, and the sitting is the doorway. The contrast is the sixth thread, because Ortega went into exile in France and built the Taller Recabarren, and Advis stayed in Chile, kept his university chair with a gap from 1981 to 1990, and never belonged to a party, and the staying is the doorway, and the leaving is the doorway, and the bridge was built by two men standing on opposite sides of the same history. Qualifications, stated before the hold is used: the "twin" label is about the bridge, not about equal output, because Ortega wrote the anthems and the forge and Advis wrote the cantatas and the symphony, and Advis disowned the one work they made together, "la musica de esa obra es muy mala, es muy mediocre. Yo no la considero para nada una obra nombrable", so the doorway carries a disowning inside it; Advis was not a party militant and Ortega was a Communist militant to his death; and the doorway is composer-to-composer, through a shared teacher, a shared commission and a permission asked and given, not member-to-member and not performer-to-performer.

## M1. The late conversion

Advis converts late, and the late is the mechanism. Until he is twenty-eight or thirty he does not like American music and he does not like folklore, and the not-liking is the mechanism, because the true music for him is Wagner and Strauss and Mozart and Beethoven, and a bolero annoys him, and the annoyance is the conversion waiting to happen, and the waiting is the mechanism. He says it himself in his last interview: "Hasta los 28 o 30 an~os no me gustaba la musica americana, no me gustaba el folklore... me gustaba Wagner, me gustaba Strauss, Mozart y Beethoven", and the saying is the mechanism, because the confession is the conversion, and the conversion is the late, and the late is the composer. The door into the music he refused is not a record and not a teacher, it is a novel, and the novel is the mechanism, because he reads Vargas Llosa and Garcia Marquez and Cortazar in the early 1960s, and the characters in those novels dance the mambo and the tango and the milonga, and the dancing is the door, and the door is the literature, and the literature is the music. He says, "cuando advirtio que los personajes de estos escritores bailaban mambo y otras musicas populares, comenzo a escuchar lo que hasta entonces habia excluido", and the noticing is the mechanism, because the fiction teaches the ear what the conservatory never taught it, and the teaching is the novel, and the novel is the conversion. The second door is a record, and the record is the mechanism, because in 1966 a Margot Loyola record introduces him to Violeta Parra and the cantoras, and the introducing is the conversion, and the conversion is the record, and the record is the ear opening. He never becomes a folklorist, and the never is the mechanism, because he says, "Nunca he sido un tipo que domine el folklore, y no me considero un musico folklorico. He tomado ciertos elementos de la musica que se llama folklorica... pero eso ha sido algo eventual, no una receta que yo me he dado", and the taking is the mechanism, because he borrows elements and never takes the recipe, and the borrowing is the bridge, and the bridge is the conversion. He finds Chilean folklore itself limited, and the finding is the mechanism, because "A mi nunca me ha interesado demasiado el folklore chileno porque lo encuentro muy limitado. Me interesa el folklore latinoamericano en general", and the general is the mechanism, because the continent is wider than the country, and the wider is the symphony to come, and the symphony is the conversion. The conversion is late and it is a reader's conversion, and the reader's is the mechanism, because he arrives at the popular as a man who read his way in, and the reading is the music, and the music is the conversion. **Takeaway:** let the conversion come late and let it come through a novel, read the characters dancing before you listen to the dance, borrow the elements and never take the recipe, and let the continent be wider than the country, so the ear opens at thirty and the opening is the career. Against the chain: Ortega (#378 M1) also learned by ear and systematised late, but his is a city boy who pulls Wagner out by ear at fifteen and then takes the conservatory at eighteen, and Advis's is a man who keeps Wagner until thirty and is converted by a novel and a Margot Loyola record, which is why his is a systematising and this is a converting.

## M2. The cantata popular as a named form

Advis names the form, and the naming is the mechanism. He calls it "cantata popular" because it had to be called something, and the having-to-be-called is the mechanism, because without a name nobody would understand what the work was, and the understanding is the name, and the name is the form, and the form is the mechanism. He says it himself: "Yo le puse asi. Habia que darle un nombre, sino no se iba a entender de lo que se trataba. Le puse 'cantata', porque en cierto modo es como las cantatas de Bach del siglo XVII o XVIII y como iba dirigida a un tipo de gente, le puse 'popular'", and the putting is the mechanism, because the name is half Bach and half the people, and the half-and-half is the form, and the form is the naming. The frame is the baroque cantata, and the frame is the mechanism, because the European model gives him arias and choruses and instrumental pieces and recitatives, and the giving is the frame, and the frame is the cantata, and the cantata is the mechanism. He keeps the relation of soloist and chorus, and the keeping is the mechanism, because the baroque relation survives the crossing into the pampa, and the surviving is the form, and the form is the keeping. He replaces the recitative with a spoken narration, and the replacing is the mechanism, because the relato stops the music and tells what happened, and the telling is the narration, and the narration is the cantata, and the cantata is the speaking. He keeps the instrumental interludes, and the keeping is the mechanism, because the interludes develop or comment on what was just sung, and the commenting is the interlude, and the interlude is the music thinking aloud, and the thinking is the form. He pours a national tragedy into the frame, and the pouring is the mechanism, because the massacre of the nitrate workers of Santa Maria de Iquique on Saturday 21 December 1907 becomes eighteen parts, two pregones, a prelude, three instrumental interludes, five spoken relatos and seven songs, and the counting is the form, and the form is the tragedy, and the tragedy is the cantata. The orchestra is the folk ensemble, and the ensemble is the mechanism, because two guitars and two quenas and a charango and a bombo carry the work, enlarged only by a cello and a double bass, and the only is the mechanism, because the enlargement is small and deliberate, and the smallness is the popular, and the popular is the orchestra. The form is a genre because he named it, and the naming is the mechanism, because others then used the term, and the using is the genre, and the genre is the name, and the name is the cantata. **Takeaway:** name the form because it has to be called something, take half the name from Bach and half from the people, keep the soloist and the chorus, replace the recitative with a spoken narration, and let two guitars and two quenas and a charango and a bombo carry the orchestra with only a cello and a double bass added, so the tragedy fits the frame and the frame fits the people. Against the chain: Ortega (#378 M11) also wrote a cantata that tells a history, but his is twenty-four parts about four centuries with the forge as the frame, and Advis's is eighteen parts about one day in 1907 with a name he invented for it, which is why his is a hammering and this is a naming.

## M3. The narrator inside the song

The narrator stands inside the song, and the inside is the mechanism. Five spoken relatos stop the music in the Cantata and tell what happened before the singing resumes inside the story, and the stopping is the mechanism, because the story is told in prose and then sung in verse, and the telling is the singing, and the singing is the story, and the story is the narrator. Eight relatos do the same in Canto para una semilla, and the same is the mechanism, because the life of Violeta Parra is told in eight stations and sung in nine parts, and the stations are the relatos, and the relatos are the life, and the life is the narrator. The relatos of Los tres tiempos carry the history of a continent, and the carrying is the mechanism, because the narrator speaks the birth of America and the pre-Columbian history and the birth of the nations, and the speaking is the continent, and the continent is the narrator. The narrator is a role and not an introduction, and the role is the mechanism, because the voice that speaks is part of the work and not outside it, and the part is the role, and the role is the narration, and the narration is the song. The role outlives the voice, and the outliving is the mechanism, because the Cantata's relatos were spoken by Marcelo Romo at the August 1970 festival premiere and by Hector Duvauchelle on the DICAP record and by Roberto Parada later and by Hector Noguera at Santa Laura on Saturday 1 November 1997, and the changing is the role, and the role is the work, and the work is the narrator. The pregon opens the Cantata, "Sen~oras y sen~ores", and the opening is the mechanism, because the town crier calls the audience to listen before any music is played, and the calling is the narration, and the narration is the pregon, and the pregon is the song. The final song answers the audience directly, "Ustedes que ya escucharon", and the answering is the mechanism, because the narrator's last word is a question put to the listeners, and the question is the song, and the song is the audience, and the audience is the narrator. The narration is the hinge of the form, and the hinge is the mechanism, because without the relatos the songs would be a suite and with them they are a story, and the story is the hinge, and the hinge is the cantata. **Takeaway:** put the narrator inside the song and not in front of it, let the spoken relato stop the music and tell what happened, let the role outlive the voice that first spoke it, and let the last song answer the audience directly, so the story is the hinge and the hinge is the work. Against the chain: Ortega (#378 M4) also built drama into the music, but his is a dramaturgy learned in the theatre booth and written into the notes, with characters and dialogues and counterpoints, and Advis's is a narrator who stops the music to speak, which is why his is a writing and this is a speaking.

## M4. The crescendo machine

The Cantata climbs, and the climbing is the mechanism. The alternation of recitation and song and instrumental interlude runs in a continuous crescendo to a climatic point and then resolves, and the running is the mechanism, because the short blocks stack one on another and the stacking is the climb, and the climb is the machine, and the machine is the cantata. Gallegos says the work is "mucho mas cerca de Wagner que de Bach", and the saying is the mechanism, because the model is not the baroque alternation but the Wagnerian accumulation, and the accumulation is the crescendo, and the crescendo is the machine. The climax is earned by accumulation and not by volume, and the earning is the mechanism, because the letania comes after seventeen parts of climbing, and the coming-after is the earning, and the earning is the climax, and the climax is the machine. The letania sings "Murieron tres mil seiscientos", and the singing is the mechanism, because the number is the work's text and not a historical count, and the text is the climax, and the climax is the number, and the number is the machine. Advis's own verdict on the work is that its value is dramatic and not musical, and the verdict is the mechanism, because he says, "La cantata tiene un valor dramatico, no musical. Musicalmente es normal, no es ni bueno ni malo. Tiene impacto por el desarrollo dramatico, el cual llega a un climax", and the impact is the development, and the development is the climax, and the climax is the value. The machine is the form's engine, and the engine is the mechanism, because the same climb runs in Canto para una semilla from a Schubert-like melancholy to an ecstasy worthy of Richard Strauss, and the running is the engine, and the engine is the elegy, and the elegy is the machine. The crescendo is slow and it is built from short blocks, and the slowness is the mechanism, because each relato and each song adds one step and no step is skipped, and the adding is the climb, and the climb is the work. **Takeaway:** build the climax from short alternating blocks, let the recitation and the song and the interlude stack one on another, earn the letania by seventeen parts of climbing, and let the value be dramatic and not musical, so the machine is the climb and the climb is the work. Against the chain: Quilapayun (#377) also closed with an anthem, but theirs is a group that sings the climax together at the festival, and Advis's is a machine that climbs to the climax by accumulation, which is why theirs is a closing and this is a climbing.

## M5. The elegy, not the cantata

For the second work of the type he refuses the word cantata, and the refusing is the mechanism. He is tired of the word, "hasta la 'tusa' de la cantata", fed up to the crown of his head with the cantata, and the fed-up is the mechanism, because the name he invented has become a cage, and the cage is the refusing, and the refusing is the elegy, and the elegy is the mechanism. He calls Canto para una semilla an elegy, and the calling is the mechanism, because the elegy is a mourning and not a history, and the mourning is the form, and the form is the name, and the name is the refusing. The text is Violeta Parra's autobiographical Decimas, reordered and adapted by Advis, and the reordering is the mechanism, because a life is told as eight stations, Los parientes and La infancia and El amor and El compromiso and La denuncia and La esperanza and La muerte and Epilogo, and the stations are the life, and the life is the seed, and the seed is the elegy. The seed grows, and the growing is the mechanism, because the personal replaces the tragic, and the replacing is the elegy, and the elegy is the growth, and the growth is the song. The daughter sings the mother's words, and the singing is the mechanism, because Isabel Parra is the soloist and the Decimas are her mother's autobiography, and the mother is the daughter, and the daughter is the voice, and the voice is the elegy. The final song is built from "Gracias a la vida", and the building is the mechanism, because the thanksgiving that Violeta sang becomes the closing of her own elegy, and the closing is the thanks, and the thanks is the life, and the life is the seed. The form is expanded, and the expanding is the mechanism, because the timbres multiply and a female soloist joins Inti-Illimani, and the joining is the colour, and the colour is the expressive contrast, and the contrast replaces the dramatic tension of the Cantata, and the replacing is the elegy. The work was rehearsed in February 1972 in Advis's own house in Iquique, and the rehearsing is the mechanism, because the elegy is learned at home before it is sung at the Teatro Antonio Varas in December 1972, and the home is the rehearsal, and the rehearsal is the elegy. The dramatic-tragic tension of the Cantata is replaced by the emotional world of inner human feelings, and the replacing is the mechanism, because the elegy mourns a person and not a massacre, and the mourning is the person, and the person is the seed. **Takeaway:** refuse the word you invented when it becomes a cage, call the second work an elegy, tell a life as eight stations of a seed's growth, let the daughter sing the mother's words, and close with the thanksgiving she sang, so the mourning is the form and the form is the person. Against the chain: Violeta Parra (#369) also told her own life, but hers is a woman who writes her decimas alone in her carpa and sings them herself, and Advis's is a man who reorders her decimas and gives them to her daughter and a group, which is why hers is a writing and this is a reordering.

## M6. The programme set to music, and disowned

Canto al programa is written in a week, and the week is the mechanism. Advis says it himself, "Esa obra se escribio y se grabo en una semana, asi es que ni me acuerdo", and the saying is the mechanism, because the work was written and rehearsed and recorded in seven days, and the seven days are the programme, and the programme is the week, and the week is the mechanism. Each government measure is its own song, and the each is the mechanism, because the deepening of democracy and the agrarian reform and the new culture and international relations each get a song, and the getting is the measure, and the measure is the song, and the song is the programme. A narrator speaks between the songs, and the speaking is the mechanism, because the character is Peyuco Pueblo, the voice of the programme of the "poder popular", and the voice is the people, and the people are the programme, and the programme is the narrator. The premiere is held at the end of January 1971 in the Sala La Reforma with Allende and his ministers in the first row, and the first row is the mechanism, because the government hears its own programme sung back to it, and the hearing is the cantata, and the cantata is the programme. The speed is a compositional condition, and the condition is the mechanism, because the work had to be written and rehearsed and recorded in a week, and the having-to-be is the speed, and the speed is the work, and the work is the week. And then the composer disowns it, and the disowning is the mechanism, because Advis says, "La musica de esa obra es muy mala, es muy mediocre. Yo no la considero para nada una obra nombrable", and the saying is the disowning, and the disowning is the work, and the work is the mistake. A work can be a mechanism and a mistake at once, and the at-once is the mechanism, because the programme set to music works as a commission and fails as a composition in the composer's own judgement, and the failing is the honesty, and the honesty is the disowning. The disowning updates the record, and the updating is the mechanism, because #378 carried the deadline as a week against little more than a month, and Advis's own testimony settles this leg toward the week, and the settling is the interview, and the interview is the evidence. **Takeaway:** take the programme and set it to music in a week, give each measure its own song, put a narrator called Peyuco Pueblo between them, sing it to the ministers in the first row, and then say plainly that the music is very bad and very mediocre and not a nameable work, so the commission is the mechanism and the disowning is the honesty. Against the chain: Ortega (#378 M12) also set the programme to music, but his is the man who proposed the work in November 1970 and wrote six of its songs, and Advis's is the man who wrote the rest in a week and then disowned it, which is why his is a proposing and this is a disowning.

## M7. The symphony that stops being a cantata

Los tres tiempos de America takes sixteen years, and the sixteen years are the mechanism. Advis begins it in late 1970, retakes it in 1977, and finishes it between October and December 1986, altering it as Chilean politics change, and the altering is the mechanism, because the work grows with the history it tells, and the growing is the gestation, and the gestation is the symphony. He says it himself: "Comence esta obra, algunas de sus canciones y la idea general, a fines de 1970. Algo olvidada, la retome en 1977, cuando compuse algunos trozos mas. Finalmente, entre octubre y diciembre de 1986, despues de revisar lo hecho y escribir los relatos que la integran, considere que estaba terminada", and the saying is the mechanism, because the finishing is a decision and not a deadline, and the deciding is the sixteen years, and the sixteen years are the work. When the orchestra joins the folk ensemble the name has to change, and the changing is the mechanism, because the folk instruments dissolve into the total orchestral sound, and the dissolving is the symphony, and the symphony is the name, and the name is the orchestra. The form is three times of America, the distant past and the present and the future, a prelude and two interludes and eight songs, and the three is the mechanism, because the origin of American man and the hopes and sorrows of the present and the open paths of humanity each get a movement, and the getting is the time, and the time is the symphony. The premiere fails in part, and the failing is the mechanism, because the ensemble and the soloists stand in front of the stage in the Roman theatre of Merida and cannot see the conductor, nor he them, and the not-seeing is the failure, and the failure is the staging, and the staging is the premiere. ## M8. The theatre mine

Against the chain: Ortega (#378 M2) also learned in the theatre, but his is six years in the booth as a soundman, obtaining horses and thunder and rain and crickets on tape, and Advis's is ninety scores as a composer, digging the old music out and resetting it, which is why his is a schooling and this is a mining.

## M9. The fast hand

Against the chain: Ortega (#378 M10) also answered the news in two days, but his is a contingent song written in an afternoon to answer the pots beating in the street, and Advis's is a film score in two nights and a symphony in sixteen years, which is why his is a reply and this is a hand that is fast and slow at once.

## M10. The oratorio bookends

Against the chain: Yupanqui (#371) also framed a life with a form, but his is the collected form refilled once with his own words on muleback, and Advis's is the oratorio taken up at the beginning and at the end, which is why his is a refilling and this is a framing.

## M11. The superposed song

In "America tiene amores" two different songs run at once, and the at-once is the mechanism. A tonada sung by the soloist runs over a joropo sung by the male chorus, and the running-over is the mechanism, because two rhythms and two melodies occupy the same bar without cancelling each other, and the occupying is the superposition, and the superposition is the song, and the song is the two. Gonzalez calls it "un gesto de maxima complejidad en un genero popular y mas bien propio de un compositor como Bach", and the calling is the mechanism, because the complexity is Bach-like inside a popular genre, and the Bach-like is the superposition, and the superposition is the complexity. Elsewhere there are polyphonic passages for two quenas, and the passages are the mechanism, because two Andean flutes carry two lines at once, and the carrying is the polyphony, and the polyphony is the folk instrument doing the learned work. The tiple and the charango take the place of the baroque harpsichord, and the taking-the-place is the mechanism, because the Latin American instruments arise from the baroque ones, and the arising is the timbre, and the timbre is the superposition, and the superposition is the bridge. The folk ensemble dissolves into the total orchestral sound in the symphony, and the dissolving is the mechanism, because the superposition is not a collage but a melting, and the melting is the orchestra, and the orchestra is the song. Two songs at once is the proof of the bridge, and the proof is the mechanism, because the learned and the popular do not take turns, they sound together, and the together is the proof, and the proof is the song. **Takeaway:** run two different songs at once, a tonada over a joropo, two quenas in polyphony, let the tiple and the charango take the harpsichord's place, and let the folk ensemble dissolve into the orchestra, so the learned and the popular sound together and the together is the proof. Against the chain: Ortega (#378 M7) also stacked voices, but his is a moving bass that walks under the shout and lifts it out of the pamphlet, and Advis's is two songs superposed in the same bar, which is why his is a walking and this is a superposition.

## M12. The arranger-teacher

Advis teaches by scoring, and the scoring is the mechanism. He teaches Quilapayun and Inti-Illimani by writing for them and arranging for them, and the writing-for is the mechanism, because the composer is the ensembles' school, and the school is a set of parts handed over in rehearsal, and the handing-over is the teaching, and the teaching is the score. Against the chain: Ortega (#378) also taught, but his is a Conservatory teacher with Salinas and Rifo among his pupils, and Advis's is a teacher whose classroom is the rehearsal room of Quilapayun and Inti-Illimani, which is why his is a teaching and this is a scoring.

## Evolution

The evolution is the Wagnerian who becomes the cantata man and then the symphony man, and the becoming has seven stages, and each stage keeps the one before it. From 1935 to 1958 he is the Iquique boy in a house with a piano and his father's RCA records of Wagner and Mahler and Chopin and Tchaikovsky, studying law to the fourth year and then philosophy, composing innately from childhood, taking his first systematic lessons only at twenty-three with Gustavo Becerra and Alberto Spikin, and writing his first theatre score, La princesa Panchita, with Jaime Silva in 1958 while still a law student, and the house is the piano. From 1958 to 1966 he is the docta chamber composer and the theatre man, writing the Sexteto of 1957 and the Cinco preludios of 1960 and the Quinteto para vientos of 1962 and Cosas of 1963 on Mistral, writing fifteen theatre works with Jaime Silva, and refusing the popular, annoyed by a bolero, and the refusing is the Wagnerian. From 1966 to 1970 he is the convert, reading the Latin American novelists and hearing the Margot Loyola record of 1966, meeting Violeta Parra through the cantoras, writing the music for Isidora Aguirre's massacre play Los que van quedando en el camino in 1968, and composing the Cantata from November 1969 to March 1970, first presented in July 1970 at the Teatro La Reforma and officially premiered in August 1970 at the Segundo Festival, and the converting is the Cantata. From 1970 to 1973 he is the most listened-to composer in Chile, co-writing Canto al programa with Ortega in one week in 1970, producing Autores chilenos for Inti in 1971, writing Canto para una semilla in 1971 and 1972 and premiering it in December 1972 at the Teatro Antonio Varas, writing for the television serial La sal del desierto and the film La tierra prometida, and beginning Los tres tiempos in late 1970, and the listening is the cantata. From 1973 to 1986 he is the man who stays, never exiled and never a party member, returning to traditional writing after the coup, writing the Oratorio 1850 in 1974, the Suite Latinoamericana chamber version in 1976, Rin y cueca in 1976, the film Julio comienza en julio in 1978, leaving the university in 1981, and retaking Los tres tiempos in 1977, and the staying is the oratorio. From 1986 to 1994 he is the symphony man, finishing Los tres tiempos between October and December 1986, seeing it premiered on Friday 5 August 1988 in the Roman theatre of Merida before more than three thousand five hundred people, writing Murales extremenos for the Extremadura government and seeing it premiered in July 1993 in Merida, returning to the university in 1990, becoming president of the SCD in 1993, and seeing the orchestral Suite Latinoamericana premiered in September 1994 and the Chilean premiere of Los tres tiempos on Thursday 20 October 1994 in Vina del Mar, and the finishing is the symphony. From 1994 to 2004 he is the late master, writing Cinco danzas breves in a week in 1998, the Coronacion score in two nights in 2000, the sixteen Preludios para piano in 2003 with four of them the Cosas songs of 1963 with the vocal line removed, receiving the Figura Fundamental and the Premio Presidente de la Republica in 2003, seeing the Ley de Fomento a la Musica Nacional approved in September 2003, giving his last interview on Thursday 17 June 2004, finishing the Oratorio de la Pampa del Tamarugal a few weeks before his death, and dying of cancer on Thursday 9 September 2004, and the dying is the oratorio.

## Aesthetic logic

The aesthetic logic is the conversion, the name, the climb and the speed. The conversion is the Wagnerian who learns the mambo from a novel and the cantoras from a Margot Loyola record, and the conversion is the late ear opening at thirty, and the opening is the bridge, and the bridge is the composer. The name is the "cantata popular", invented because the work had to be called something, half Bach and half the people, and the name is the form, and the form is the tragedy, and the tragedy is the naming. The climb is the crescendo machine, the short blocks stacking one on another until the letania sings "Murieron tres mil seiscientos", and the climb is the drama, and the drama is the value, and the value is the machine. The speed is the lightning hand, the film score in two nights and the quartet in a week and the programme in a week, and the speed is the symphony in sixteen years, and the sixteen years are the waiting, and the waiting is the speed at the other end. The four hold together because the conversion makes the name necessary, and the name makes the climb possible, and the climb makes the speed meaningful, and the speed makes the waiting bearable, and the bearing is the composer, and the composer is the work. He writes his music for ten or twenty people, for his friends, and the ten-or-twenty is the mechanism, because the Cantata becomes one of the best-selling Chilean records while the composer doubts it is for more than his friends, and the doubting is the humility, and the humility is the work.
