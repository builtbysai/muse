## M16. Slickness against gesture (the cold surface cooling the hot mark)

From the early 1960s Rivers mixes the hot gestural mark with cold, impersonal techniques, airbrush, stencils, projections, Plexiglas, so the picture feels detached rather than expressive, a slick, manufactured surface, plastic, airbrushed tone, photographic transfer, set against bravura brushwork in the same work, and the watchable behaviour is a painting that pulls away from the viewer just as it invites them in, the emotion reading as quoted rather than felt, and the mechanism is the slickness against the gesture, the cold doing the work the hot cannot, and the slick is the gesture, and the gesture is the slick. ## Evolution

Eight phases. 1) Learning to draw, 1945 to 1948: Bonnard-inflected interiors after the 1948 MoMA Bonnard retrospective, Interior, Woman at a Table of about 1948, paints at night after sax gigs, draws eight hours a day, the figure intimate, domestic, borrowed. 2) Breakthrough of the personal, 1949 to 1952: first solo show 1949, The Burial of 1951 takes Courbet's peasant funeral and makes it a large-scale statement, Greenberg praises him, draftsmanship already the point, drawing has always served as the most important component of painting. 3) Declaration of independence, 1953 to 1955: Washington Crossing the Delaware of 1953 is a deliberate insult to Abstract Expressionism and a claim on history painting, O'Hara Nude with Boots of 1954 and Double Portrait of Berdie of 1955 break the taboo on the frank nude and the double view, and the Art Story says the idea of Larry Rivers was born with Washington Crossing the Delaware. 4) Collaboration and the news photo, 1957 to 1960: Stones of 1957 to 1959, Cedar Bar Menu of 1959, the Life-magazine veteran paintings of 1959 to 1961, the source of imagery moving from art history to the mass media while the paint stays gestural. 5) Paris and the series, 1961 to 1965: Vocabulary Lessons, French Money, Camels, then the consumer-packaging paintings and the monumental assemblage of the Russian Revolution, the work becoming systematic, templates, series, stencils, projections, international recognition, Tate purchase May 1962, first UK solo, Gimpel Fils, 1962. 6) Expansion into other media, 1966 to 1977: sculpture, Lampman Loves It of 1966, Plexiglas, neon, video, film, Africa and I, teaching at UC Santa Barbara 1972, documenta 6 in 1977, the painting thinning out and the practice spreading across media, and the 1971 Rice curation "Some American History" drawing criticism for centering his own experience over the Black artists shown, a real reputational wound, worth one line, not a section. 7) Self-recycling, 1978 to 1988: Golden Oldies turns his own fame into material, the three-dimensional foam-board relief paintings begin, Art and the Artist from 1988 repaints David, Manet, Cezanne and himself, the magpie now eating its own work. 8) Late histories and show business, 1984 to 2002: History of Matzah of 1984 and 1985, History of Hollywood of 1999 and 2000, Fashion Show of 1997 to 2001, large commissioned narrative machines built with researchers and assistants, in the same spirit as the Russian Revolution thirty years earlier, and he painted until three months before his death.

## Aesthetic logic

The logic is the copy as the original, the figure kept, and the source moved. The copy is the original: he never paints from the thing, he paints from the reproduction, the schoolbook, the cigar box, the magazine photograph, the lesson book, and the distance from the original is the subject, and the sabotage is the picture. The figure is kept: he never gives it up after 1953, and it is what lets him use Abstract Expressionist gesture without becoming an abstractionist, and the body, wobbling at the edges, carries the picture. The source is moved: from art history to the mass media, from the Old Master to the news photograph, from the museum to the cigar box, and the painting follows the source, and the source is the picture. Hot against cold: the gestural mark kept, and the stencil, the airbrush, the Plexiglas set against it, so the emotion reads as quoted, and the quotation is the exorcism. The series as a standard: one template run through variations like a jazz tune, the rehearsal doing the work the single picture cannot, and late the template is himself, his own fame recycled into the next work, and the recycling is the picture, and the picture is the copy, and the copy is the figure, and the figure is the source, and the source is the media, and the media is the history.
