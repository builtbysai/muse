## Core engine

**DO NOT DOCUMENT THE WORLD; TRANSMIT YOUR LISTENING OF IT: put a fixed
architecture (space) in front of a wandering ear (place), and make the
work the overlap of the two horizons of listening.**

The named mechanisms:
1. **The two ears.** His PhD theory, relational listening: the organic ear
   (interior, psychological, preoccupied) and the prosthetic ear of the
   microphone (apolitical, conditioned by the gear) have different horizons
   of listening, and the work succeeds when the two horizons overlap so the
   recordist's listening is transmitted to the listener. "The horizon of
   listening is not fixed in a linear fashion like that of the horizon of
   vision. It is dynamic and always in flux, it is promiscuous and not
   governed by the same conditions as the spectrum of light" (Ableton).
   Watchable behavior: he states the mic setup per project (DPA 4060s in
   Antarctica, Telinga parabolic for the sleeping seal, omni atmos for
   disorientation), and captions name what the prosthetic ear revealed.
2. **Recording the activated, not the force.** Viento (Patagonia and
   Antarctica, 2010, via the Argentine Antarctic Division): stuck in Rio
   Gallegos during a wind storm, he spent three days recording outside the
   military shelters. "You're never recording wind, you're recording the
   objects being activated by wind." Mechanism: sound as the trace of a
   force on materials, never the force itself.
3. **Space versus place.** "Space is the kind of physical environment, if
   you like, the hard fixed architecture of the world. But what happens in
   that architecture is the stuff that I guess I've been talking to so far,
   this idea of the incidental things" (Tonearm 2025). Watchable behavior:
   every major work pairs a fixed field (the pipe organ at Brisbane's Old
   Museum on Observation of Breath; the 712kg bell on Proximities; the
   Naala Badu building entrance on Even The Horizon Knows Its Bounds) with
   wandering incident events.
4. **Acoustic audition architecture.** Installations that change what a
   listener CAN hear, not what they hear. Audition (2015, APT8, f03): two
   2.4-metre parabolic acoustic mirrors, the listener stands in the focal
   field and their own listening is amplified. Proximities (2024, f18):
   bronze bell forged with Anton Hasell with the minor third eliminated
   for equal temperament tuning, rung daily as companion timekeeping at
   AGSA. Wavefields: 10-hour durational performance for sleepers at
   Burleigh Heads (his site; reported as 12 hours in the 2025 podcast).
5. **The photograph as listening note.** room40shoots is a dated audition
   log. The #musicforairports series documents flights as numbered transits
   ("53. ASP BNE", born 2014 when @kingchowoz challenged him to document
   flights). Annual birthday posts count "rotations" ("44th rotation",
   2020; "another rotation", 2024, with lava and 1000 singing frogs).
   Captions routinely record what he heard, where, and what the mic caught
   (the vocal crows of 2015-11-20, f06; the eerie siren before the crow
   photograph, 2017). The photo is evidence of having listened there.
6. **Imagined field recordings; the ghosts of the piece.** ShellType
   (2024, f19): "a piece of imagined field recordings and notional
   atmospheres." "There was a great deal of listening that happened without
   recording taking place... Some sounds, some experiences are just for
   you. They are the ghosts of the piece, experiences that resolutely
   impact on your sense in a given time and place but only persist in
   memory" (Ableton, on Vela Luka). Mechanism: the unrecorded and the
   remembered are legitimate compositional material.
7. **Portraits of fellow listeners.** A recurring portrait series of the
   listeners he listens with: Chris Abrahams' hands on the Yamaha keys
   (f10, 2017), Annea Lockwood at Caramoor (f14, 2020), Anton Hasell
   (f18, 2024), the exhibition collaborator (f17, 2023). "I love to listen
   to a recording in which I sense the audition of the artist making them"
   (on Chris Watson, Jana Winderen, Aki Onda, Douglas Quin, Ableton).
   Listenership as social portraiture.
8. **Patience as structure.** Duration is a compositional parameter, not a
   container. "These recordings only make sense in time, be patient and let
   them reveal themselves as you focus on them. The idea of immediacy that
   so fully dominates our daily lives does not apply" (Ableton). Hundreds
   of hours archived per trip, a fraction published; 50+ hours of Amazon
   recordings compressed into A Mirror Holds The Sky (2021). The Radical
   Listener masterclasses (Brown, UCSB, Salzburg, Austin, St Paul, Tbilisi)
   teach listening as a practice, not a skill.

## Evolution

- **1990s: bands, cassettes, tape trading.** Industrial and alternative
  rock bands; teenage cassette releases with covers printed at the public
  library. Late 90s: organizing Brisbane concerts for artists he admired
  (David Toop, Scanner, David Shea, DJ Olive). Room40 founded 2000.
- **2002-2008: aliases to his own name.** Calm as I/O (2002), the I/O3
  trio (with Toop, Scanner, DJ Olive), Pandemic as Object (2003,
  experimental hip-hop), first solo albums Transit and Happiness Will
  Befall (2005). Kiri No Oto (Touch, 2008): dense, subtly shifting; the
  breakthrough wider audience.
- **2009-2014: harmonic drone to tectonic.** A Colour for Autumn (12k,
  2009, with Fennesz and Dean Roberts; 15th anniversary edition 2024). The
  Peregrine (2011, after J.A. Baker). Free field editions Songs of the
  Living / And the Lived In (2012). For/Not for John Cage on Richard
  Chartier's LINE (2012). Antarctica 2010 feeds Viento. Wilderness of
  Mirrors (2014): "most tectonic auditory offering," inspired by Earth,
  Swans, My Bloody Valentine live; Paul Clipson Super 8 film (f04).
- **2015-2019: HEXA, PhD, politics.** HEXA with Jamie Stewart (Factory
  Photographs 2015, sonic response to David Lynch's factory photographs
  at GOMA; they bonded over affective sound, Japanese photography,
  birdwatching). Approaching Nothing (2016): Vela Luka, Croatia, second
  chapter after Luc Ferrari's Presque Rien (1969). Cruel Optimism (2017,
  after Lauren Berlant: power, obsession, fragility). PhD 2017. Selva
  Oscura with William Basinski (2018), Immediate Horizon with Alessandro
  Cortini (2018). Photographic turn: from 2015 the feed locks into the
  B&W square diary (crows, dead animals, sirens, portraits).
- **2020-2026: organs, bells, imagined fields.** Observation of Breath
  (2021, the Brisbane pipe organ, Hallow Ground). Colours of Air (2023,
  with Loscil). Even The Horizon Knows Its Bounds (2025): AGNSW Naala Badu
  commission, eight movements, cast including Jim O'Rourke, Claire Rousay,
  Stephen Vitiello, Dean Hurley, Chuck Johnson, Norman Westberg. Trinity
  (2025): third Vitiello duet. Proximities bell (2024). The Rest Is My
  Ghost (2026): "Acid Nostalgia," the flattening of the past into empty
  visual signals.

## What to take for prompts

English is overwhelmingly sonic, so every prompt is an honest translation
of a mechanism into a visual system, never a claim about his visuals. The
translatable moves: two synchronized but different sensors whose overlap
is the work (organic vs prosthetic), incident events wandering across a
fixed architecture (space vs place), forces made visible only through what
they activate (wind through objects), audition architecture (mirrors and
bells that amplify the listener, not the sound), the dated listening
note (numbered transits, rotation birthdays, B&W square diary),
composition from remembered or imagined recordings (the ghosts of the
piece), portraits of fellow listeners, and duration as structure (nothing
arrives on demand). His photography's actual grammar, B&W square,
high-contrast grain, crows, towers, boats, empty interiors, is decoration;
the mechanisms are the drivers.
