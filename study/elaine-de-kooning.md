## M1. The face painted, then wiped out (facelessness as erasure, not omission)

On Frank O'Hara (1962): "First I painted the whole structure of his face; then I wiped out the face, and when the face was gone, it was more Frank than when the face was there", her words in the 27 August 1981 Phyllis Tuchman oral history, and the face is built in full and then removed, so the portrait keeps the structure of a face without the face, and the watchable behaviour is a head that reads as a smudge, an oval, a black mark where brow and nose were, Willem de Kooning of about 1952, seated, barely any face, no mouth or eyes, just an oval smudge with a mop of gray hair and a black mark for brow and nose, and the likeness getting stronger as the features disappear, and the mechanism is erasure, not omission, because the face was there and was taken away, and the taking away is the portrait, and the portrait is the face that is gone. **Takeaway:** paint the whole structure of the face, then wipe it out, so the likeness survives as stance and smudge and the face that is gone is more the sitter than the face that was there. Against the chain: Willem (#387 M1) scraped the whole canvas to a ghost and repainted, and Elaine scrapes only the face and keeps the body, which is why his picture is a painting that lost itself and hers is a body that lost its face, and the body is the portrait, and the portrait is the erasure, and the erasure is the face.

## Aesthetic logic

The logic is the figure as armature, the face as the removable part, and the critic as the second pair of eyes. The figure is the armature: she never abandons it, and it is what lets her use Abstract Expressionist gesture without becoming an abstractionist, and the pose, not the face, carries the person. The face is the removable part: painted in full, then wiped out, so the likeness survives as stance, splay, weight, silhouette, and the erasure is the portrait. The critic is the second pair of eyes: she wrote the movement from inside it, and her paintings are written the same way, from inside the sitter's company, the salon and the sitting being the same event. Late, the armature widens: a bull, a basketball player, a statue, a cave wall, each a body in motion that the gesture can chase, and the chase is the picture, and the picture is the body in motion, and the body in motion is the figure, and the figure is the armature.
