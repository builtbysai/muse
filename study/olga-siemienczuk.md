## Core engine

Two interlocking mechanisms, stated as what they ARE:

1. **Practice made public as serialized play.** The most private labor of a singer — daily technical practice — is posted as a steady stream of practice reels, each naming one concrete technical target: microtones practiced "in her natural habitat" (2021-06-16), hitting the C4, Berio's Sequenza III coached by Donatienne Michel-Dansac, Nono's "Djamila Boupacha" ("the most challenging piece I've ever sung. It takes a lot of courage and trust in myself", 2024-08-05), and "queen of the night a 4th higher, because... why not" with a clown-face emoji sticker (2024-10-17). Humor is the container that makes extreme difficulty watchable: stickers, clown emoji, laughing selfies around the hardest repertoire in existence. The mechanism: private labor becomes legible to non-specialists through play framing; the cadence is the message (regular posts across 2023-2025, each with one named target).

2. **Improvisation as a social game with rules about having no rules.** The 2024-05-19 impro-session with Beatrice Arrigoni is captioned "à la @philippinval": "I love atonal improvisation, I love the no-rule..." — improvisation treated as a two-player game that is more fun with a partner; the thumbnail shows the afterglow embrace in a bright room, not the session itself. She then institutionalizes the game: teaching improvisation at @stimme_voice ("huuuuuuge pleasure to teach improvisation... I am always amazed", 2023-07-22), "instant composition" gigs ("The unknown matter(s)", Utrecht 2023), and "slow sound exploration" duos at impuls 2025 with Lisa Newill-Smith. The mechanism: no-rule constraints generate vocal behavior; the practice scales from duo sessions to festival workshops without changing its rules.

Supporting mechanism: **the coloratura as theatrical athlete.** In Ondrej Adamek's world she is Maid/Storyteller (Seven Stones, Konzerthaus Dortmund 28.1.2022), plays an overtone flute built from white PVC pipes taped into a bundle (with Shigeko Hata), is lowered from 17 meters ("my one and only stuntwoman/opera singer experience"), and has her voice mapped to audiovisual systems ("FISH DANCE WHEN THEY ARE NOT SLEEPING", with Tatiana Rosa and Brand Kracht, Feb 2024). Opera re-staged as total-body virtuosity.

- **2024: opera-house scale.** World premiere of Adamek's INES at Oper Köln (16.6.2024, Staatenhaus Saal 3; her role E2: "a sneak peek at my role in E2"). Impro-session à la Philippin with Arrigoni. - **2025: festival dozentin and her own room.** Dozentin at Stimme-VOICE Festival (July 2025; 4 tagged posts from @stimme_voice). Slow sound exploration with Lisa Newill-Smith at impuls. Organizing and performing "my very own concert" in Berlin-Lichtenberg. ARCHE rehearsals begin.
- **2026: world-premiere circuit.** ARCHE, immersive installation by Eugene Birman, libretto/direction Katharina Schmitt, at the Munchener Biennale (May 8-20, 2026). Then six weeks in Aix-en-Provence as chorus in the world premiere of Francesco Filidei's ACCABADORA (Festival d'Aix, 2-21 July 2026, conducted by Lucie Leguay, directed by Valentina Carrasco). She posts a Filidei excerpt from Aix: "variazioni sull'aria della forforetta".
