## Aesthetic logic

The logic is the figure kept, the word admitted, and the manner abandoned. The figure is kept: she never gives it up after 1952, and it is what lets her use Abstract Expressionist gesture without becoming an abstractionist, and the body, half dissolved in the field, carries the picture. The word is admitted: the poem painted onto the canvas, the name doing the identifying work, and the painting has to be read as well as seen, and the word is what lets a blonde smear be Ophelia. The manner is abandoned: as soon as she knows how to do something she leaves it, and the career is a series of departures, abstraction, Old Masters, street life, collage, mythic queens, floor painting, each left the moment it is mastered. Late, the body returns as a tool: the canvas on the floor, the paint poured and walked on, and the Pollock conversion of 1948 is reclaimed on her own terms, and the reclaiming is the picture, and the picture is the body in motion, and the body in motion is the figure, and the figure is the event.
