## Core engine

The guitar as a box of indeterminacy, run through two interlocking mechanisms. (1) Two faces, never blended: Breaking News (recorded 2005, released 2007) alternates avant-garde skronk and traditional blues in hard sequence, and the Free Jazz Collective review names the mechanism exactly ("Blending or mixing genres are maybe the wrong words, because it's rather alternation"); Kreis is "composition and improvisation, sound and noise" as the negative and positive of one photograph. (2) The sonic box: one body forced through every conjugation (electric, fretless, lap steel, baritone, banjo, pump organ, prepared acoustic, loops) until the guitar stops being an instrument and becomes a box of voices, wood, strings and objects, with John Cage lingering as the project's "protective spirit". The engine's vehicle shrinks as the ambition sharpens: the maximal electric arsenal and bandleader stance of 2005 to 2009, then the rigorous acoustic reduction of Kreis (one archtop, preparations, no pedals), then the pedagogical and documentary register (teaching post at the Conservatorio Luca Marenzio in Brescia, graphic scores, B&W street photography as a parallel notation practice).

## Evolution timeline

- **2005-2009, the maximal electric years (pre-IG, web-evidenced):** Breaking News recorded September 2005 (Long Song Records, 2007). Personnel: Massaron on electric guitar, fretless electric guitar, electric lap steel and loops; Daniele Cavallanti (tenor sax), Steve Piccolo (Lounge Lizards, bass and vocals), Elliott Sharp (electric guitar, electric lap steel), Tiziano Tononi (drums). Tracklist: Breaking News, Albert Meets Nels, Louisiana Rain, Plick Plick, The Kid, Run Through The Jungle (a furious Creedence Clearwater Revival cover), Bumper To Bumper (a Massaron/Sharp duet the press called "math jazz"), Brain Washed, Ruth's Tale. The record runs from wide-open guitar experimentation to stripper-beat blues to haunting balladry without ever crossfading. Dandelions On Fire recorded June 2007 (released 2009): Massaron with Carla Bozulich (voice), Xabier Iriondo (mahai metak, 1920s gramophone), Andrea Viti, Zeno De Rossi, Davide Tedesco, Riccardo Di Paola, Francesco Guerri. His instrument list for the session reads like the engine in one line: baritone electric guitar, electric guitar, pump organ, tenor banjo, electric lap steel, acoustic guitar, loops, slide guitar, guitar noise. Blues, ballads, folk and improvised moments; the press heard Tom Waits territory ("Never Saw Your Face" opens as a lowering two-chord drama with bowed bass and harmonium). Long Song's copy names his circle of acquaintances as Nels Cline, Marc Ribot and Steve Piccolo, and names his speciality: fretless guitar.
- **2015-2017, the B&W Milan years (IG opens):** the account opens 2015-07-27. ## Corrections and honest gaps

- **Correction:** the corpus leg's draft described Kreis as his electric-guitar improv duo. The project page states the duo is rigorously acoustic: classical guitar for Naibo, archtop for Massaron, no pedals. The electric side of his practice lives in Breaking News and Dandelions On Fire, not in Kreis.
- **Correction:** the Kreis project page spells the influence "Derek Baley". The intended figure is Derek Bailey (evident from the Lachenmann and Cage company and the free-improvisation context); the site's spelling is preserved here as found.
