## Core engine

**Reduction as instrument: isolate one sonic or visual variable at the threshold of perception and repeat
it until the space it occupies becomes the content. Silence is the material, monochrome is the field,
typography is the image.**

The named mechanisms:
1. **The threshold test.** Sound placed at the edge of audibility; the listening room and its background
   noise finish the piece. The mechanism is the same as Deupree's zone test (#228) but pitched one step
   lower: where Deupree keeps a loop until nothing sticks out, Chartier keeps reducing until the silence
   around the sound becomes audible content.
2. **File-exchange composition.** SPEC. (12k1007, 1999/2000 with Deupree): exchange of source files, each
   remixing the other's material. The collab protocol is the compositional method.
3. **The cover system as score.** The LINE visual identity is codified like music: white header band,
   black sans ARTIST, thin rule, italic serif title; below it, one monochrome field with one object or
   gradient (f11, f15, f18, f19). Twenty-five years without breaking the system; evolution happens inside
   it (Basinski's icy paintings, the Koenig tonometer rods, Strecker's blurred pink gradient).
4. **Monochrome + small object.** The Pinkcourtesyphone cover grammar (f01): one flat color field, one
   small thing on it, minimal lowercase typography. Reduction made commercial: the cover is the hook.
5. **Atmosphere as document.** Personal photography: pale grey skies with faint double rainbows (f05),
   bare branches against cobalt (f06), overcast seascapes (f08). His eye photographs the same near-nothing
   his ears compose; the Sugimoto-adjacent taste is explicit (Specification.Fifteen was built against
   Sugimoto's Seascapes, Hirshhorn 2006).
6. **The curator's ledger.** The Dublab show "between two points" (running since 2017, f17: black ground,
   thin white lowercase sans, orange date) is a monthly spine; the feed's recent era is the label's archive
   (LINE 25th anniversary monument f14, Transparency 15 years, CoH anniversaries). The system catalogs
   itself.

## What to take for prompts

Chartier's grammar is the purest reduction vocabulary of the whole 12k strand: threshold-of-perception
material, monochrome fields, one small object, typography as image. For generative pieces the translatable
systems are: a field that holds near-nothing so the near-nothing becomes content; a codified cover system
where evolution happens inside fixed rules (the fly equivalent of his header band); monochrome gradients
with one accent (the Basinski ice + single red line); slow accumulation toward an anniversary monument.
Interaction is never earned in this work: watch-only. The pink/camp register is the decoration that
proves the minimalism is a choice, not a limitation.
