# Roberto Matta: Study Notes

**Site:** Roberto Sebastian Antonio Matta Echaurren (Santiago, Saturday 11 November 1911 - Civitavecchia, Saturday 23 November 2002), the Chilean painter who drew the inside of the mind as if it were a landscape, the architect who left Le Corbusier's studio to join the Surrealists in 1937 and invented the inscape, the psychological morphology, the painting that is a head you walk inside, the Chilean who lived abroad for sixty-seven years and still painted the volcano as his body and the people as his subject, the friend of Neruda and Lorca and Breton and Duchamp and Pollock, the teacher of automatism to the New York School, the expelled Surrealist, the man who presided over the Havana Cultural Congress in January 1968 and named the guerrilla interior, the painter of the first goal of the Chilean people on a swimming-pool wall in La Granja in 1971, the older Chilean already in Paris when the exiles arrived, the one who taught Quilapayun late in the 1970s that the revolution was at the service of culture and not the other way round, the maker of the open cube, the painter of cosmic machines and social morphologies and murals and arpilleras, the father of Gordon Matta-Clark, the Chilean who died in Italy aged ninety-one and was buried in a crypt under his house in Tarquinia, after three days of national mourning in Chile. He was born on 11.11.11, a date he used as a motif in his work, the son of a well-off Santiago family, and he studied architecture at the Universidad Catolica, graduating in 1935, and he sailed for Europe in 1935 and worked for Le Corbusier in Paris, and he met Federico Garcia Lorca and Pablo Neruda in Spain in 1936, and he joined the Surrealists in 1937, and he painted his first oils in Brittany in the summer of 1938 with Gordon Onslow Ford, and he fled to New York in the autumn of 1939, and he held weekly automatist sessions at his apartment in the fall of 1942 with Jackson Pollock and William Baziotes and Peter Busa and Gerome Kamrowski and Robert Motherwell, and he was expelled from the Surrealists in 1947 or 1948 over the Gorky affair, and he returned to Europe in 1948, and he painted the UNESCO mural in Paris in 1956 and had a MoMA retrospective in 1957, and he presided over the Havana congress in January 1968, and he returned to Chile in 1970 and 1971 at Allende's invitation and painted the La Granja mural, finished Thursday 25 November 1971, and he painted "La vida allende la muerte" after the coup of 1973, and his Chilean passport was annulled in 1974, and he won the Premio Nacional de Arte in 1990 and the Principe de Asturias in 1992 and the Praemium Imperiale in 1995, and his last work, "La Source du Calme", was made in 2002, the year he died. ## M1. Automatism as engine

The hand moves faster than the mind, and the moving is the mechanism. Paint is squeezed from the tube, spread with rags, worked with fingers, one finger per colour, in the Sotheby's account of the summer of 1938, and the squeezing is the mechanism, because the paint arrives before the plan, and the arriving is the squeezing, and the squeezing is the hand, and the hand is the engine. Stains are rubbed on at random, and the rubbing is the mechanism, because the accident comes first and the recognition comes second, and the accident is the rubbing, and the rubbing is the stain, and the stain is the beginning. Then the stains are refined into hovering shapes and filaments of line, and the refining is the mechanism, because the chance mark is slowly recognised into form, and the recognising is the refining, and the refining is the form, and the form is the accident grown up. The earliest drawings prove it, Snail's Trace of 1937, a single looping line coiling into a snail spiral on white paper, and Morphology of 1937, pale washes with thin filaments and a small biomorphic knot, and the proving is the mechanism, because the vocabulary exists before the oil paint, and the before is the proving, and the proving is the line, and the line is the engine. In New York the engine becomes a school, weekly automatist sessions at Matta's apartment in the fall of 1942 and the winter of 1942 to 1943, Pollock and Baziotes and Busa and Kamrowski and Motherwell, six sessions, and the schooling is the mechanism, because one man's fast hand becomes five men's method, and the becoming is the schooling, and the schooling is the apartment, and the apartment is the engine. Matta says it himself on the MoMA page, "We spoke of automatism", and the saying is the mechanism, because the talking is the method, and the talking is the saying, and the saying is the automatism. **Takeaway:** let the hand move before the mind, let the stain come before the shape, let the accident be recognised into form slowly, and let the piece start unplanned and resolve, never start composed, so the beginning is the accident and the accident is the engine. ## M5. The morphology as evolving organism

One form absorbs, swells, emits and becomes another form, and the becoming is the mechanism. Nothing stays itself, and the not-staying is the mechanism, because the object takes energy in and gives energy out, and the taking is the not-staying, and the not-staying is the emitting, and the emitting is the becoming. ## M7. The open cube

A closed form is dismantled to prove it has more than three dimensions, and the dismantling is the mechanism.
