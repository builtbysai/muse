## Mechanisms

M1. **The filmstrip index.** The homepage is not a list or a grid: it is 30 vertical slices, one per project, shown greyscale. Scroll pans the strip sideways; hover colourises a single slice. The index IS the homepage -- there is no separate project list to click into first. The top-centre counter shows the slide number of 30.
M2. **The giant condensed title.** Every slide's title is set in huge condensed capitals (the site's own "jws" font) laid over the project image, letters individually positioned with per-letter spacing from the data, bleeding off the frame. The title is the slide: it is bigger than the image it labels. "MARRY MONDAY" renders as "MARY MONDAY" where the letters overlap -- the overlap is part of the look, not a bug.
M3. **The two-colour re-skin.** Every slide re-skins the whole page in its own two-colour palette: background and type come from the slide's own bg/txt RGB pair. House of Gucci is gold type on white; Rappi Pay is coral on black; Everest is violet on pale blue; Mank is dark red on pale grey. The chrome (counter, links, meta columns) changes colour with the slide. A generative prompt can steal the mechanism without stealing the look: one content item, two colours, whole page.
M4. **The A/B/C/D meta columns.** Every slide carries the same four facts in the same place: A COMPLETED (month year), B TYPE, C ROLE, D CLIENT, plus one description sentence and an EXPLORE link. Thirty slides, one fixed information architecture. The viewer learns where to look once and never re-learns it.
M5. **Fullstack dev and motion as the repeated role.** 24 of 30 slides list the role as full-stack dev and motion (one spells it FULL STACK); the other six are front-end dev and motion (Jacques Marie Mage, TM, Dribbble, CRSA), creative dev (New Company), or full-stack dev with no motion (Rappi Pay). The repetition is the brand: he does not do design, he does the build and the movement, and the designers he works with are named as the design.
M6. **The portfolio as its own client, four times over.** FOLIO V1 (Oct 2017, with Ben Mingo and Michael Novia), FOLIO V4 (Aug 2019, with Ben Mingo), the Malaga Folio on Behance, and the current site itself (2026, designed by Jon Way Studio). The portfolio is a recurring project with its own slide, its own date and its own collaborators -- the shoemaker's children are shod, repeatedly, in public.
M7. **The award engine as the client pipeline.** His own words (Communication Arts): the majority of his clients come from the prize platforms, and one SOTD brought 10,000 visitors in a day. The awards are not a trophy shelf added at the end; they are the distribution channel. CANALS proves it from the other side: a personal project won SOTM December 2019, Developer Award and FWA of the Month.
M8. **The photographer-portfolio run.** A repeat client type: portfolios for image-makers -- Paul & Henriette (photographers duo, Paris), Guillaume Belveze (commissioned and press photographer, Paris), Benjamin Guedj (digital art, product and set design), Jenny Johannesson (digital designer, Sweden), Ben Mingo (graphic and interactive designer, California), Camille Mormal (Behance only, 2022). Six portfolios for people whose work is images, built by a developer whose work is movement.
M9. **The personal project as award magnet.** CANALS (Dec 2019, with Marcus Brown) is listed as Personal, no client, and it took SOTM December 2019, Developer Award and FWA of the Month. Marry Monday (Aug 2019, a wedding site for Alice Veley and Gene Ross) is also Personal. The two personal slides sit among the client work with the same meta columns and the same weight.
M10. **WebGL throughout, zero decoded HTML images.** A text fetch of the homepage returns only "0 0 1"; the page reports 0 decoded HTML images. All imagery is canvas/WebGL textures served from /static/media/<folder>/h/ (home) and /w/ (work pages); the visible text is DOM letters positioned from data. The site is a WebGL app that happens to have a URL, not a document with pictures.
M11. **The work page as the case file.** Each project has its own page (/house-of-gucci etc.) with a media set of 5-8 images and a "visit site" link. The carousel is the index; the work page is the evidence. The same two-colour palette and the same A/B/C/D columns carry over.
M12. **The post-carousel drift.** After April 2022 the site stops updating but the work does not: in_cognita (Feb 2025) is Behance-only, video-driven (10 embedded Vimeo videos, autoplay, loop, muted), and Onnu Jonu Son (2023/24) is an album site built with Robbin Cenijn. The newest work is a music site and a video-led case, not a carousel slide.
M13. **The scrambled monogram.** The About page opens with a giant scrambled monogram, "ESY68 / 33098L" -- 11 glyphs, E S Y 6 8 3 3 0 9 8 L. It did not resolve to readable text. It is reported as observed; see C3.
M14. **The collaboration credit as a system.** The carousel names collaborators inside the client slot: "NETFLIX - WATSON DG", "MGM STUDIOS - WATSON DG". The About page names Ben Mingo and Michael Novia on the portfolios; CANALS names Marcus Brown; Capsulin names Index Studio; in_cognita names Pierre Patrault. The credit line is part of the work record, not a footnote.

- **2016-2017, the breakthrough.** Jenny Johannesson (Nov 2016), then Epicurrence 6 (Aug 2017, Zion), Bear Grylls, Digital Asset, FOLIO V1 (Oct 2017, with Ben Mingo and Michael Novia) and Ben Mingo's own portfolio (Dec 2017).
