## M1. The little review as a battering ram: eight pages against a wall

A tiny, cheap, irregular periodical used to attack the dominant culture rather than to join it. Leonardo, 4 January 1903 to August 1907, was 25 issues, 8 pages in folio, 15 centesimi, with the motto "Non si volge chi a stella e fisso", he who is fixed on a star does not turn, and the cover by Adolfo De Carolis, a star above a flying eagle, and the address Palazzo Davanzati, via Porta Rossa 9, and seven associates at 10 lire each. Its programme was war on positivism and on the academies "fra i muri di un'accademia", between the walls of an academy, and a ferocious individualism against the solidarist-socialist frenzy, and the "risveglio della borghesia", the awakening of the bourgeoisie. The first phase ran 4 January to 10 May 1903, nine issues, shaped by Nietzsche and D'Annunzian aestheticism, then came a break with the artist collaborators in May 1903, five months' suspension, and a resumption in November 1903 with Papini's "Morte e resurrezione della filosofia" and Prezzolini profiling the pragmatists F. C. S. Schiller and William James, and new collaborators Cecchi, Vailati and Calderoni, and Costetti's new logo, and from 1906 a religious-mystical turn, until 1907, when Papini declared the campaign failed and the last issue, August 1907, opened with "La fine", the end. The mechanism is that the smallness is the weapon: eight pages cannot contain a culture, so they do not try, and they strike instead, and each issue is a single hammer-blow, and the blows accumulate, and the wall cracks by accumulation, because the review is too small to be an institution and too sharp to be ignored, and the irregularity is part of it, because a battering ram does not keep office hours. The watchable behaviour is a small white page that keeps hitting a big grey wall, each issue a single blow, the wall cracking by accumulation.

**Takeaway:** let the small page hit the big wall, and the piece should crack it by accumulation, one issue at a time.

## M2. The synthetic program: the whole creed on page one

The whole creed compressed onto the front page of issue one, so the program is a seed that grows into the whole world of the piece. Leonardo's Programma sintetico, first issue, Sunday 4 January 1903: "Nella vita [sono] pagani e individualisti... Nel pensiero [sono] personalisti e idealisti", in life they are pagans and individualists, in thought personalists and idealists. La Voce did the same five years later with "La nostra promessa", the second issue, 27 December 1908: "Non promettiamo di essere dei geni... Ma promettiamo di essere ONESTI e SINCERI... Di LAVORARE abbiamo voglia... di INFORMARE... di PROPORRE riforme... di OCCUPARCI della crisi morale delle universita... di SEGNALARE le opere degne di lettura e di COMMENTARE le vilta della vita contemporanea", we do not promise to be geniuses, but we promise to be honest and sincere, we want to work, to inform, to propose reforms, to occupy ourselves with the moral crisis of the universities, to signal the works worthy of reading and to comment on the cowardices of contemporary life. The mechanism is that the program is not a summary, it is a seed: it states the whole doctrine in a paragraph, and everything the review then does is the unfolding of that paragraph, and the unfolding is visible, because the reader can hold the first page against the fiftieth issue and see the same sentences grown large, and the growth is the argument, because a program that fits on one page cannot hide, and a review that keeps its first page keeps its word. The watchable behaviour is a dense block of text that unfolds, line by line, into the whole world of the piece, the program as a seed that grows.

**Takeaway:** put the whole creed on page one, and the piece should grow it, line by line, into a world.

## M3. The pseudonym as a second self: Giuliano il Sofista and Gian Falco

## M4. The aphorism as a civil code: furbi and fessi

Compressing a whole sociology into one sentence that can be quoted, so the sentence does the work of a book. Codice della vita italiana, 1921, Quaderni della Voce 45, is the furbi-and-fessi code: "L'Italia va avanti perche ci sono i fessi. I fessi lavorano, pagano, crepano. Chi fa la figura di mandare avanti l'Italia sono i furbi, che non fanno nulla, spendono e se la godono", Italy goes forward because there are the suckers. The suckers work, pay, die. Those who make the figure of sending Italy forward are the smart ones, who do nothing, spend and enjoy themselves. Ideario, 1967, is the same mechanism as an archive: fragments alphabetised, 1903 to 1967, the aphorism kept for sixty-four years and filed by letter. The mechanism is that the aphorism is a stone dropped into water: it is short enough to be remembered whole, and sharp enough to cut, and it ripples outward into a scene of Italian life, and the scene is the proof, because the sentence claims a whole country in twelve words, and the reader tests it against his own street, his own office, his own mayor, and finds it true, and the truth travels, because a sentence that can be quoted can be repeated, and a sentence that can be repeated can be believed, and the code is civil because it describes how a country actually works, not how it says it works. The watchable behaviour is short sentences dropping like stones into water, each one rippling outward into a scene of Italian life.

**Takeaway:** drop the sentence like a stone, and the piece should let each ripple become a scene.

## M5. The demolition review: criticism as demolition

Criticism as demolition, the text struck through word by word until only the frame remains. Leonardo polemicised with Benedetto Croce from its columns, the philosopher Prezzolini had discovered through neoidealism and then attacked, and the attack continued into the portrait Benedetto Croce, 1909, and into the quarrel over La Voce's founding, which Prezzolini settled by consulting Croce by letter and then going his own way. The same mechanism runs in reverse in Emilio Gentile's biography, where the verdict on Prezzolini the historian is that "il polemista prese spesso il sopravvento sullo storico", the polemicist often prevailed over the historian, page 970, and that on antifascism he "non si sforzo mai di avere l'atteggiamento da storico che riteneva necessario per studiare il fascismo", he never made the effort to have the historian's attitude he thought necessary to study Fascism, page 957, and that "nel momento stesso in cui esortava a un giudizio storico equanime, Prezzolini rinunciava all'imparzialita, perche nei suoi scritti sull'antifascismo prevalse sempre il polemista", at the very moment he urged an equitable historical judgement, Prezzolini renounced impartiality, because in his writings on antifascism the polemicist always prevailed, page 976. The mechanism is that the demolition is the criticism: the review does not judge the book, it takes it apart, and the taking apart is public, and the public taking apart is the point, because a demolished author cannot answer in the same issue, and the demolition travels faster than the book, and the demolisher is remembered longer than the demolished, and the same blade cuts both ways, because the man who demolished Croce was himself demolished by his biographer, and the demolition is the form, and the form is the same whoever holds the hammer. The watchable behaviour is a text being struck through, word by word, until only the frame remains.

**Takeaway:** strike the text through word by word, and the piece should leave only the frame standing.

## M6. The special issue as an inquiry: one question, one whole number

Devoting a whole number to one question, so the review becomes an inquiry and the inquiry becomes a map. La Voce's special issues: Questione sessuale, 10 February 1910; Irredentismo, 8 December 1910; Questione meridionale, Thursday 16 March 1911, with Einaudi and Fortunato; "Perche non si deve andare a Tripoli", 17 August 1911, with Salvemini; Filosofia contemporanea, 19 December 1912; Albania, 20 February 1913; Canton Ticino, Thursday 18 December 1913, with Francesco Chiesa's "L'anima del cantone Ticino". The mechanism is that the single question focuses the whole review: every contributor writes on the same territory, and the territory is mapped from every side, and the map is the issue, and the issue folds back to a verdict, because a review that spends a whole number on one question has said the question matters more than the week's news, and the reader who opens it knows what he will get, and the getting is the inquiry, and the inquiry is the work, because the special issue is the review thinking in public about one thing until it has thought it through. The watchable behaviour is the review's pages fanning out into a map of one territory, then folding back to a verdict.

**Takeaway:** give the whole number to one question, and the piece should fan out into a map and fold back to a verdict.

## Aesthetic logic

The Prezzolini logic is that the review is the work, and the letter is the life, and the fragment is the late style, and the coat is the man. He began as the self-taught son of a prefect who left the liceo at sixteen and educated himself in his father's library, and the library was the school, and the school was the library, and the man who had no university made two reviews that taught Italy how to think, and the reviews were the university, and the university was the review, because Leonardo and La Voce did not report on a culture, they assembled one, and the assembling was the work, and the work was the review, and the review opened, filled, and closed, and the closing was the movement's death, because Leonardo closed when Papini declared the campaign failed, and La Voce closed when the war took its editor, and the closing was the work, because the review was the act, not the container. The second logic is that the letter is the life: the friendship with Papini was conducted as a fifty-six-year correspondence, three volumes, 1900 to 1956, and the letters outlived every quarrel, the war that distanced them, the conversion that perplexed the friend, the Fascism they both consented to differently, the post-war disgrace they both endured, and the conversation never stopped, even when the writers stopped agreeing, because the disagreement was inside the conversation, and the conversation was the friendship, and the friendship was the letters, and the letters were the life, and the life was the pile, and the pile was the landscape. The third logic is that the fragment is the late style: the aphorism as a civil code, the Ideario as fragments alphabetised over sixty-four years, the bruschetta as a daily crumb that stings, the diary as a line kept for eighty-two years, and the fragment does not need the system, and the system is what failed, and the failure is the point, because the man who wanted a system spent his life writing fragments, and the fragments are complete, and the completeness is the point. The fourth logic is that the coat is the man: he changed coats again and again, nationalist, satanic, Christian, Crocian, interventionist, fascist, and the changing was the work, because the coat was never the man, and the man was never the coat, and the man was the changing, and the changing was public, and the public changing was the reversal, and the reversal was the form, and the form was the same at every age, only the coat changed, and the coat changed because the man kept moving, and the moving was the life, and the life was the century, and the century was the coats, and the coats were the history. The last logic is that the bridge is the work: he carried Bergson and James into Italy and Italy into America, and the carrying changed what was carried, and the change was the import, and the import was the culture, and the culture was the traffic, and the traffic was two-way, and the two-way traffic was the point, because a man who lives in three countries and writes in two languages and founds reviews in one and directs a house in another is himself the bridge, and the bridge is the life, and the life is the crossing.

For August 1907: it.wiki Leonardo, "Fu pubblicata dal 4 gennaio 1903 all'agosto 1907, per 25 fascicoli complessivi", last issue August 1907, opening article "La fine", en.wiki Leonardo, 1903-1907, prabook, editor until 1907 when it ceased publication.
