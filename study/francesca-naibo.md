## Core engine

The guitar is not her instrument, it is her research site: one body forced through every conjugation (classical, electric, fretless, pedal steel, laid flat on a table, prepared with pencils and stones, routed through fuzz and seek-wah and loopers) so that free improvisation becomes the instrument's native language, running from roaring drones down to microscopic vibrations.

Expanded: Naibo's whole visible practice is about refusing the guitar's default posture. Early frames show the conventional classical stance (f00 Bern 2014, f03 Yehudi Menuhin Forum 2015); by 2016 she is photographing the instrument lying horizontal like a dulcimer (f05); by 2020 her hand is on a flat-laid electric with an EBow-style device (f09); by 2021 the studio floor is a pedalboard and cable tangle (f10); by 2022 the recording booth at Niton Lab holds a stool of small objects next to the chair (f12), and her caption names "my beloved objects and preparations". The mechanism is always the same: change the guitar's physical situation, then improvise inside the new constraints. The caption for "So much time" makes the second half explicit: the booth's "no visual distractions and a lot of calmness, stillness, contemplation" is itself a technique, a Deep Listening room built so that tiny sounds (an object on a string, a breath) become events. Two poles hold the engine together: the pedal chain as an external nervous system (ZVex, Mountain etc., MXR, Chase Bliss, loopers named across years of captions) and the prepared tabletop guitar as an acoustic sound box. Solo records document the engine in its pure form ("Namatoulee" 2020, praised by BBC Radio 3's Late Junction; "So much time" 2022, a time-dialogue between her child voice, her adult voice and the guitar). The duo records document it in dialogue: Kreis with Simone Massaron ("the guitar transcends often its renown function and becomes a sonic box"), and Weather Eye with Theresa Wong (all tracks improvised, guitar + cello + two voices). Her 2025 Deep Listening Teaching Certification and her text score in "A Year of Deep Listening. 365 Text Scores for Pauline Oliveros" are the institutional form of what the captions already showed: listening as a scored activity.

## Evolution timeline

- **2014-2015, the classical years:** account opens Dec 2014 in Bern; frames f00-f03 are classical guitar in concert rooms ("Poetry music jam" Bern, Concerto per 4 at L'Orangerie, Yehudi Menuhin Forum Bern). Training visible: she studied in Venice, Milan, Bern, Basel, graduating in classical guitar and free improvisation. f02 (Milan fair, Oct 2015) is the first sighting of the left-handed identity ("Testing for the first time some lefty instruments!"), later codified in the bio "I'm #lefty and I know it".
- **2016-2019, preparation begins:** "Horizontal guitars" (f05, 2016) rotates the instrument into a tabletop; groove lessons and microtonal seminars (f04 caption, 2016); personal images mix in (Cansiglio canopy f06, her 1994 childhood drawing f07, a B&W theater interior f08). The guitar starts appearing treated as an object rather than a worn instrument.
- **2020-2021, the studio decade of pedals:** "Prove (di Resistenza)" (f09, Dec 2020), a documentary-concert with Milanese musicians; the home studio piece (f10, Jan 2021) with pedalboard, loopers, colored lights; first solo album "Namatoulee" (Aut Records, 2020) promoted with free lesson-chats (f11). The gear captions accumulate: fuzz, seek-wah, wah-wah, Bigsby, looper, thomastik strings.
- **2022-2023, the booth and the road:** recording "So much time" at Niton Lab (f12, Oct 2022; released on Ramble Records); "July in music" live month (f13, 2023); workshops at Paganini Guitar Festival Parma, Kitarika Festival Koper, duo with Luca Venitucci at Pisa Ondavideo; ensemble with Eddie Prévost in Rome; Acoustic Trio Kimmig-Studer-Zimmerlin plus Phil Durrant in Zurich and Ljubljana. Mutual Mentorship for Musicians, Fall Equinox 2023 Cohort 6.
- **2024-2026, international circuit and the two records:** first Australian tour, spring 2024 (f14 poster: Melbourne Make It Up, Conservatory workshop, solo); Kreis in Berlin (f15, experimen.tik, June 2025); New York Mutual Mentorship festival trio (Oct 2025); since 2024 PhD student at Siena Jazz researching languages of improvisation. 2026: "Monologo addosso" (Habitable Records, trio with Maddalena Ghezzi and Beatrice Arrigoni) and "Weather Eye" (Kohlhaas KHS 038, duo with Theresa Wong, recorded Feb 13 2023 at Niton Studio Barasso, released May 5 2026).
