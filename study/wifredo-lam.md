## M1. The hybrid figure

The mechanism is that one body is human and animal and vegetal at once, and the being-at-once is the figure. In The Jungle the figures blend human and animal and plant forms to create hybrid creatures, and the blending is the mechanism, because the MoMA label says the faces are reminiscent of African masks, the feet are handlike, the legs are elongated, the bodies carry male and female attributes at once, and the bodies are intertwined with broadleaves and tropical fruit and sugarcane, and the intertwining is the blending, and the blending is the figure, and the figure is the hybrid. Wikipedia calls it polymorphism, the juxtaposition of aspects of humans and animals and plants, creating monstrous hybrid creatures through magical metamorphosis, and the metamorphosis is the mechanism, because nothing in the picture stays one thing, and the not-staying is the metamorphosis, and the metamorphosis is the monster, and the monster is the figure. The hybrid is not a costume and not a mask worn over a body, because the body itself is the mixture, and the mixture is the body, and the body is the mixture, and the mixture is the mechanism, because a leg becomes a stalk and a stalk becomes a leg, and the becoming is the leg, and the leg is the stalk, and the stalk is the body. **Takeaway:** let one body be three things at once, let the leg become the stalk and the stalk become the leg, so the figure is the mixture and the mixture is the figure. Against the chain: Roberto Matta (#382 M5) also let one form absorb and swell and emit and become another, and his is a morphology defined as the graph of transformations due to the absorption and emission of energy, and Lam's is a body that is human and animal and vegetal in the same instant, which is why the father's is a becoming-over-time and Lam's is a being-at-once, and the being-at-once is the becoming made still.

The thicket has no horizon and no exit, and the figures are half-emerged from it, and the half-emerging is the mechanism, because a mind shows itself the way a figure shows itself in the cane, partway, and the partway is the half-emerging, and the half-emerging is the mind, and the mind is the cane. ## M4. The femme cheval (horse-headed woman)

The mechanism is that a woman is ridden by a god, and the riding is the figure. ## M10. Singularisation

## M11. The blank that shines

## M13. Collaborative drawing

Against the chain: Roberto Matta (#382 M1) also let the hand move before the mind, and his is automatism, a stain rubbed on at random and recognised into form, and Lam's is a card drawn at random and answered by the hand, which is why the father's is a recognising and Lam's is an answering, and the answering is the recognising made into a game.

## M14. Fire-transformation (ceramics)

The mechanism is that earth is changed by fire, and the kiln decides the outcome, and the deciding is the work. In the year 1975 alone he produced nearly 300 ceramic works, and the nearly-300 is the mechanism, because the kiln rewards repetition, and the repetition is the nearly-300, and the nearly-300 is the fire, and the fire is the year. Eskil Lam says, "Something might have cracked in the kiln, or the glazes might have run the wrong way or mixed with one another to create an unexpected colour, sometimes welcome, sometimes a disaster... Often I couldn't sleep, waiting for the surprises of those unexpected tones", and the waiting is the mechanism, because the artist cannot sleep while the kiln decides, and the cannot-sleep is the waiting, and the waiting is the crack, and the crack is the colour. A form that is finished by heat, not by the hand, is a form with two authors, and the two-authors is the mechanism, because the hand proposes and the fire disposes, and the disposing is the proposing, and the proposing is the hand, and the hand is the fire. **Takeaway:** make the form, then let the fire finish it, let the glaze run the wrong way and the crack be the colour, so the kiln is the author and the author is the kiln. Against the chain: Gordon Matta-Clark (#383 M12) also put the body in danger and let the film show the labour, and his is a chainsaw in a condemned house, and Lam's is a kiln that cracks the vase while he waits up at night, which is why the son's is a danger and Lam's is a waiting, and the waiting is the danger made of heat.

## M15. The crescent and the breast

The mechanism is that the whole language is two curves, and the two curves are the language. Eskil Lam says in the graphic work after 1958 "his recurring crescent moons and breasts become even sharper, even more angular", and the sharpening is the mechanism, because the crescent and the breast harden into signs, and the hardening is the sharpening, and the sharpening is the sign, and the sign is the curve. The crescent is the moon and the horn and the hoof, and the breast is the body and the fruit and the coconut, and the two-shapes is the mechanism, because a picture built from two curves only is a picture that cannot be anything else, and the cannot-be-anything-else is the two-shapes, and the two-shapes are the crescent, and the crescent is the breast. In printmaking the two curves sharpen because the print demands it, and the demanding is the mechanism, because the etched line cannot blur the way oil can, and the cannot-blur is the demanding, and the demanding is the print, and the print is the curve. A vocabulary of two shapes is a vocabulary that can say everything, and the saying-everything is the mechanism, because the crescent is the sky and the breast is the earth, and the sky is the crescent, and the crescent is the earth, and the earth is the breast. **Takeaway:** build the picture from two curves only, the crescent and the breast, let them sharpen until they are signs, so the two are the language and the language is the two. Against the chain: Roberto Matta (#382 M10) also let the title be a second image, and his is a wordplay that changes what you see, and Lam's is a curve that is a moon and a horn and a hoof at once, which is why the father's is a word and Lam's is a curve, and the curve is the word made into a shape.

## Evolution

The evolution is the apprentice who becomes the exile, and then the returned Cuban, and then the disperser whose crowd thins to a single line, and the becoming has four phases, and each phase keeps the one before it. From 1916 to 1938 he is the apprentice, sent to Havana to study law and studying tropical plants and painting instead, at the Academia de San Alejandro from 1918 to 1923, hating the academic teaching, leaving for Madrid on a grant from Sagua La Grande in the autumn of 1923, studying under Fernando Alvarez de Sotomayor y Zaragoza in the mornings and with young nonconformist painters in the evenings, discovering Bosch and Bruegel at the Prado, painting realist portraits and landscapes and still lifes, mastering texture in Campesina Castellana in 1927, losing the grant in 1925 and surviving on aristocratic portrait commissions, spending months in Cuenca painting mountain people, marrying Eva Piriz in 1929, seeing a Picasso exhibition in Madrid, and in 1931 Eva and their infant son die of tuberculosis, and his work turns dark and the mother-and-child variations begin, and he reads Marx and joins anti-fascist groups and follows the Machado dictatorship in Cuba from afar, and the apprenticing is the hating, because he learns the academic language in order to leave it, and the leaving is the hating, and the hating is the learning, and the learning is the break. From 1936 to 1941 he is the exile, fighting for the Republicans in the Spanish Civil War, making posters and propaganda, drafted to defend Madrid, working six months with explosives at an arms factory and poisoned by chemical contact, sent to the sanatorium at Caldes de Montbui, painting La guerra civil in 1937, meeting Manolo Hugue in Barcelona, who gives him the letter of introduction to Picasso, breaking with academicism definitively in Barcelona in September 1937, moving to Paris in 1938, renting an attic on the Quai Saint-Michel, received warmly by Picasso at Rue des Grands-Augustins, introduced by Picasso to Leger and Matisse and Braque and Miro and Michel Leiris and the dealer Pierre Loeb, first solo show at the Galerie Pierre opening on Friday 30 June 1939, joint show with Picasso at Perls Galleries in New York from 13 November to 2 December 1939, meeting Andre Breton in 1939, a trip to Mexico in 1938 staying with Frida Kahlo and Diego Rivera, fleeing to Marseille in June 1940 and joining the Breton circle at the Villa Air-Bel, making collective drawings with Breton and Lamba and Brauner and Dominguez and Herold and Ernst and Masson, drawing Alice and Lautreamont for the Jeu de Marseille, illustrating Breton's Fata Morgana with Masson, and the exiling is the meeting, because every meeting is a door, and the door is the meeting, and the meeting is the exile, and the exile is the door. From 1941 to 1952 he is the returned Cuban, sailing with Breton in May 1941 and interned about forty days in Martinique, meeting Aime Cesaire at Fort-de-France, returning to Cuba in July 1941 after an eighteen-year absence, and the return is a shock, "What I saw upon my return looked like Hell", Havana flourishing for tourists while the countryside still lives colonialism, and he rejects the folkloric picturesque and sets out to paint "the drama of my country" through the Black spirit, and he paints La jungla between December 1942 and early 1943 in his Buena Vista apartment, and he paints The Eternal Present in 1944, dedicated to the Afro-Cuban composer Alejandro Garcia Caturla, murdered on Tuesday 12 November 1940, and he marries Helena Holzer in 1944, and he is close to Lydia Cabrera and Alejo Carpentier, who coins "lo real maravilloso" in 1949, and he travels to Haiti for four months with Breton, and he goes to New York in 1946 and meets Cage and Duchamp and Matta and Noguchi and Gorky, and Pierre Matisse shows him in 1944 and 1945 and 1947, and Pollock sees the 1947 show and sets out to study Native American art, and he co-founds the Agrupacion de Pintores y Escultores Cubanos, and he makes ceramics with Rene Portocarrero at Santiago de Las Vegas in 1950, and between 1941 and 1949 he paints no fewer than 400 canvases, and the returning is the painting, because the drama of the country is painted through the negro spirit, and the painting is the returning, and the returning is the drama, and the drama is the country. From 1952 to 1982 he is the disperser, settling in Paris in 1952, exhibiting at Havana University in 1955 in support of student protests against Batista, travelling to Mato Grosso in Brazil in 1956 and falling ill, invited by Asger Jorn to the Albissola ceramics meeting in 1954 and arriving about ten days late, meeting the master printer Giorgio Upiglio in Milan in 1958, establishing a studio in Albissola in 1960 and a house in the early 1960s, marrying Lou Laurin in Manhattan on Monday 21 November 1960, honoured as the national painter of Cuba on 1 May 1963, winning the Guggenheim International Award in 1964, painting El Tercer Mundo for the presidential palace in Havana in 1965, retrospectives across Europe in 1966 and 1967, joining the Phases group and loosely affiliated with CoBrA and the Situationist International, producing nearly 300 ceramic works in 1975 alone, a stroke in 1978 leaving him paralysed and in a wheelchair, dying in Paris on Saturday 11 September 1982, and the dispersing is the thinning, because the figures singularise and the backgrounds recede to monochrome wash and everything concentrates in the line, and the thinning is the dispersing, and the dispersing is the line, and the line is the figure. The arc, in one line: from the apprentice who hated the academy, to the exile who met everyone, to the returned Cuban who painted the Jungle, to the disperser whose crowd thinned to a single figure on a wash, with the gods staying the same the whole way through, only fewer of them at a time.

## Aesthetic logic

The aesthetic logic is the hybrid and the jungle and the plantation and the blank. The hybrid is the figure and the mask and the elongation, one body human and animal and vegetal at once, a face that is a carved plane with eye-holes, a limb pulled like warm glass until it is a cylinder, and the hybrid is the figure, and the figure is the mask, and the mask is the limb. The jungle is the psyche and the dense packing and the Trojan horse, a thicket with no horizon and no exit, a field with no empty space where figure and ground trade places, a picture that looks like Cubism until the gods step out of it, and the jungle is the psyche, and the psyche is the packing, and the packing is the horse. The plantation is the shears and the orisha cast and the collaborative drawing, the sugarcane that is also a crop, the oversize shears that are a sign of labour and a necessary cut against foreign imposition, the named gods meeting in the picture, the card drawn by chance and answered by the hand, and the plantation is the shears, and the shears are the gods, and the gods are the card. The blank is the singularisation and the charcoal line and the fire-transformation and the two curves, the crowd thinning to one figure on a wash, the charcoal sketch showing through the thinned oil, the kiln finishing what the hand began, the crescent and the breast sharpened until they are signs, and the blank is the thinning, and the thinning is the line, and the line is the fire, and the fire is the curve. The four hold together because the hybrid needs the jungle to live in, and the jungle needs the plantation to be a workplace, and the plantation needs the blank to be seen clearly, and the blank needs the hybrid to be worth leaving empty, and the empty is the hybrid, and the hybrid is the jungle, and the jungle is the plantation, and the plantation is the blank. He called himself "a mulatto of many worlds, African, Spanish, Chinese", and the calling is the mechanism, because the mixture is the subject, and the subject is the mixture, and the mixture is the picture, and the picture is the man.
