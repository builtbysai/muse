- **Who:** b. June 2, 1971, Lancaster PA. Composer, violinist, vocalist, multi-instrumentalist. Oberlin violin performance; Peabody; San Francisco Conservatory. Cape Cod home studio; faculty NEC (Contemporary Improvisation) and Vermont College of Fine Arts (MFA Composition).

## Core engine
THE STAGE IS BUILT BY HAND. Kihlstedt builds everything as a physical object: instruments are handmade and cut to tune, scores are drawings, stage plots are paper collage, costumes are shredded thrift waste stitched back together, and tours are run off postcards and receipts. The work is assembled cut-and-paste, blacked-out, and stitched, then presented as ceremony. The vehicle keeps growing (kitchen table to transatlantic tour) but the visual language never changes: hand-cut paper, red and black cloth, found instruments.

## Evolution
- 1990s: conservatories; Tin Hat Trio acoustic chamber dissolve of classical/jazz/folk; Charming Hostess.
- 1999-2011: Sleepytime Gorilla Museum (Oakland art-rock/prog/metal theater; invented instruments, corpse makeup, Butoh dancer Shinichi Iova Koga, puppet shows, first performance to a single banana slug). 2 Foot Yard, Book of Knots, Causing a Tiger, Cosa Brava, Minamo, guest work with Tom Waits, Tracy Chapman, John Zorn, Roscoe Mitchell.
- 2012-2020: Rabbit Rabbit with Bossi; song-a-month subscription model (Rabbit Rabbit Radio vols 1-5; rock oratorio Black Inscription, which "takes place underwater" and gives the audience no air until it ends).
- 2021-2022 (corpus start): kitchen-table process world. Necessary Monsters (2021, with poet Rafael Oses, after Borges' Book of Imaginary Beings); graphic notation; postcard mail-art box sets; daily art account @ryaandcarla with Rya (collage and erasure poetry).
- 2023: SGM reunion machinery (kickstarter, costume upcycling, tour packing); solo violin pedagogy.
- 2025: twin peaks. 26 Little Deaths (Present Music commission, Cantaloupe release; 26 songs after Edward Gorey's Gashlycrumb Tinies, one death per letter, 26 daily A-Z album videos) AND SGM summer European tour.
- 2026: large ensembles. X Amendments at Roulette (ensemble with electric guitar orchestra and vocal ensemble, a deconstructed Bill of Rights); SGM US tours; Mewgenics gamescom costumes; upcoming treble-chorus environmental project Long for This World.

## Observed mechanisms (corpus frames)
1. **Staging as paper collage** (f00): the stage plot is a hand-cut object, white sheet, pink cut-paper piano shape, pink sticky labels with performer names, yellow tape, hand lettering. Technical plans are tangible cut-and-paste documents.
2. **Notation is drawing** (f01, f02): handwritten staff scores in dense desk clutter; graphic notation as scribble-filled funnels labeled "DYNAMICS DENSITY". Density is drawn as scribble, not numbers.
3. **Erasure as graphic device** (f06): repeated hand-lettered AMENDMENT overprinted with thick black redaction bars (X Amendments artwork). Deconstruction is physical blacking-out.
4. **Body and scrap as notebook** (f05, f12): string tunings inked in ballpoint on her palm; musical ideas scribbled on a store receipt picked up in the woods. The nearest surface is paper.
5. **Invented instruments as objects** (f11, f09): red whirly tubes tape-labeled, tuned by cutting length with a handsaw ("an instrument you tune with a saw"); a birds-eye case of nested violin/viola family instruments. Instruments are handmade, maintained, and named.
6. **Costumes upcycled from garment waste** (f07, f14): a 13.4 lb heap of red fast-fashion clothing mined for SGM rosettas; deconstructed dyed garments as "upcycled plumage".
7. **Gothic-folk forest ritual** (f08): Burn Into Light triptych, figures in ornate red robes with a book and candles in a winter forest. Costume-forward staged ceremony.
8. **Pen-and-ink album world** (f10): 26 Little Deaths cover, a single Gorey-style black umbrella on kraft paper, the anchor for 26 miniatures built on Gorey's alphabet book.
- Supporting (web): the invented-instrument bench of SGM (Dan Rathbun's log, pedal-action wiggler, electric pancreas, sledge hammer dulcimer; each tour adds one new instrument); the concert as cult ceremony (elaborate lighting, white makeup, spoken-word intros, audience instruction, encores of syncopated snoring); her whisper-singing thread (the "so soft your fur" barely-there voice over strings).
