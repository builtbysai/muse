## M1. The parenthesis

Fascism as a bracket opened and closed inside the long history of liberty: an irruption of irrational and barbaric elements, like the Hyksos invasion of Egypt, which lasted over two hundred years while the Fascist truculence exhausted itself in little more than twenty, and the formula was stated repeatedly after Sunday 25 July 1943, a brief and transitory parenthesis of Italian political life, without any link of continuity with the history of liberal Italy, and Mack Smith called the claim "historically naive and logically absurd", see D2, and the parenthesis is the mechanism, because a long horizontal timeline of Italian history is drawn with a dark segment as a bracket that opens and snaps shut, and the line of liberty runs unbroken underneath it, and the bracket itself wobbles and thins as the years pass, and the snapping shut is the return, and the return is the liberty, and the liberty is the history, and the history is the piece, and the piece must show the bracket opening and snapping shut while the line underneath never breaks. The watchable behaviour is a long horizontal timeline in which a dark segment is drawn as a bracket that opens and snaps shut, the line of liberty running unbroken underneath it.

**Takeaway:** open the bracket and snap it shut, and the piece should let the line underneath never break.

## M2. History as contemporary thought

The watchable behaviour is a document that is grey and inert until a reader's hand touches it, when its ink warms and its figures begin to move.

**Takeaway:** let the hand warm the page, and the piece should let the figures move only while they are thought.

## M3. The fourfold spirit

Mental activity splits into the theoretical and the practical, and the theoretical into aesthetic, intuition, the particular, value beauty, and logic, concept, the universal, value truth, and the practical into economic, will of the particular, value the useful, and ethic, will of the universal, value the good, and each presupposes the one below it, and the aesthetic founds all the rest, and the fourfold is the mechanism, because four panes rotate like the faces of a turning solid, each lighting up with its own colour and its own word, beauty, truth, useful, good, while the other three wait their turn, and nothing is ever seen through one pane alone, and the turning is the circle, and the circle is the spirit, and the spirit is the philosophy, and the philosophy is the piece, and the piece must show the four panes turning and lighting in their order, never one alone. The watchable behaviour is four panes that rotate like the faces of a turning solid, each lighting up with its own colour and its own word, beauty, truth, useful, good, while the other three wait their turn.

## M4. The dialectic of distincts

Against Hegel's dialectic of opposites, which resolves contraries in a synthesis, Croce sets the dialectic of distincts: the four forms of the spirit are distinct, not opposed, and none is cancelled by the others, and the distinction is the mechanism, because four strands braid without merging, each keeping its colour, and beside them a Hegelian pair collides and dissolves into a third, grey strand, and the braiding is the keeping, and the keeping is the distinction, and the distinction is the dialectic, and the dialectic is the piece, and the piece must show the four strands braiding and keeping their colours while the pair beside them dissolves into grey. The watchable behaviour is four strands that braid without merging, each keeping its colour, beside a Hegelian pair that collides and dissolves into a third, grey strand.

**Takeaway:** braid the four strands without merging them, and the piece should let the grey pair dissolve beside them.

## M5. Intuition-expression

## M6. The lyric

The regime is named by its disease: Fascism as malattia morale, moral illness, and its rule as onagrocrazia, government by asses, and the insult is a diagnosis, not a jeer, it says what the thing is, and the naming is the mechanism, because a sick patient is examined while the doctor writes the diagnosis on a card in large letters and pins it to the door, and the card stays there for twenty years, and the pinning is the diagnosis, and the diagnosis is the naming, and the naming is the resistance, and the resistance is the piece, and the piece must show the examination, the card written in large letters, and the card pinned to the door for twenty years. The watchable behaviour is a sick patient being examined while the doctor writes the diagnosis on a card in large letters and pins it to the door, the card staying there for twenty years.

**Takeaway:** write the diagnosis in large letters, and the piece should pin it to the door for twenty years.

## M24. History as the story of liberty

All history is one long story with a single hero: liberty, unfolding new potentialities, trembling in the lines of poets, affirming itself in the pages of thinkers, burning in men who cannot be assimilated, and the historian's task is to discover and emphasise this trend, and the river is the mechanism, because a river runs through centuries of maps, sometimes underground, sometimes in flood, never dry, and a small flame is carried along its bank from 1789 to 1945, and the running is the story, and the story is the liberty, and the liberty is the history, and the history is the piece, and the piece must show the river running through the maps, sometimes underground, sometimes in flood, never dry, and the small flame carried along its bank. The watchable behaviour is a river running through centuries of maps, sometimes underground, sometimes in flood, never dry, a small flame carried along its bank from 1789 to 1945.

**Takeaway:** run the river through the maps and never let it dry, and the piece should carry the small flame along its bank.

## M25. The letter as philosophy

Philosophy conducted as correspondence: the Carteggio with Gentile, 1896-1924, the letters to Vossler, 1899-1949, the Epistolario, and positions are taken, dropped, and retaken in letters before they become books, and the weaving is the mechanism, because two desks stand in two cities, and letters travel between them like shuttles on a loom, and the cloth that comes off the loom is a philosophy, and the travelling is the thinking, and the thinking is the writing, and the writing is the philosophy, and the philosophy is the piece, and the piece must show the two desks, the shuttles travelling, and the cloth coming off the loom. The watchable behaviour is two desks in two cities, letters travelling between them like shuttles on a loom, the cloth that comes off the loom a philosophy.

**Takeaway:** send the shuttles between the two desks, and the piece should weave the cloth into a philosophy.

## M26. The review as tribunal

La Critica as a forty-year court: the editor receives the books of Italy, tries them, and delivers verdicts, the medallion portrait that fixes a writer in one image, the stroncatura that ends a reputation, the patient sorting of poetry from non-poetry, and the trying is the mechanism, because a long table stands in a Neapolitan palazzo, and books arrive in piles and leave in two stacks, kept and condemned, while the editor writes in the margin of each one, and the writing is the verdict, and the verdict is the tribunal, and the tribunal is the review, and the review is the piece, and the piece must show the long table, the piles arriving, the two stacks leaving, and the editor writing in the margin of each one. The watchable behaviour is a long table in a Neapolitan palazzo, books arriving in piles and leaving in two stacks, kept and condemned, while the editor writes in the margin of each one.

**Takeaway:** set the long table and sort the piles, and the piece should write the verdict in the margin of each book.

## Evolution

Phase 1, Erudition and the earthquake, 1866-1895. Born into two wealthy Abruzzi families, father Pasquale Croce of Montenerodomo, mother Luisa Sipari of Pescasseroli, grandfather Benedetto "the Elder", 1794-1854, a Bourbon high magistrate, and schooled at the Collegio della Carita, Naples, then the Liceo Genovesi, and on Saturday 28 July 1883 the Casamicciola earthquake on Ischia kills his parents and his only sister Maria, and Croce himself lies buried under the rubble for hours, and he inherits the family fortune and never needs to earn a living, and he is taken in by his cousin and guardian Silvio Spaventa in Rome, and he meets Antonio Labriola and the political world of the capital, and enrols in law at Naples, never graduates, and works in libraries instead, and the early work is erudite and Neapolitan, the 1799 revolution, the theatres of Naples, 1889-1891, Napoli nobilissima, and the earthquake is the wound behind the optimism: it.wikipedia quotes his own line that those were his most painful and dark years, when "assai volte la sera, posando la testa sul guanciale, abbia fortemente bramato di non svegliarmi al mattino", and that the problem of evil stayed unresolved behind his philosophy of progress.

Phase 2, Marx and the break, 1895-1902. Labriola sends him In memoria del "Manifesto dei comunisti", 1895, and Croce publishes on Marx in Riforma sociale, Critica sociale, and Divenire sociale, and through that work meets Georges Sorel, and the essays become Materialismo storico, 1900, Marx kept as economist, dropped as prophet, and from Marx he climbs back to Hegel, and with Giovanni Gentile, met young, still a student at Pisa, who had reviewed Croce's writings on the theory of history and on Marx, he begins the collaboration that will define Italian philosophy, and the first philosophical memoir, 1893, already said history is art, and the Estetica, 1902, says it as a system.

Phase 3, The system, 1902-1915. The four volumes of the Philosophy of the Spirit: Estetica, 1902, Logica, 1909, Filosofia della pratica, 1909, Teoria e storia della storiografia, 1917, German 1915, and La Critica runs from 1903, Laterza from 1906, the great editorial series, Biblioteca di cultura moderna, Classici della filosofia moderna from 1907, Scrittori d'Italia from 1910, and literary histories pile up, La letteratura della nuova Italia, 1914-15, to 1939-40, the Seicento essays, 1911, the Storia della storiografia italiana nel secolo decimonono, written 1914-15, published 1921, and senator from 1910, and the break with Gentile over actual idealism is fought in La Voce, October and December 1913, collected as Una discussione tra filosofi amici, 1918, and he is neutralist in the Great War, writing Pagine sulla guerra, and the Contributo alla critica di me stesso is dated Thursday 8 April 1915.

Phase 4, Politics and the three histories, 1915-1932. Minister of Public Education, Tuesday 15 June 1920 to Monday 4 July 1921, in Giolitti's last government, and his school reform, the state examination, is rejected by Parliament and he resigns, though Giolitti refuses the resignation, and he applauds Mussolini's Naples speech of Tuesday 24 October 1922 and votes confidence in the Senate on Tuesday 24 June 1924, after the Matteotti murder, hoping to hold Mussolini prisoner to legality, and the murder turns him: by the summer of 1924 he is in opposition, and on Friday 1 May 1925 he writes the Manifesto of the Anti-Fascist Intellectuals, and the three histories follow, Regno di Napoli, 1925, Storia d'Italia, 1928, Storia d'Europa, 1932, with La poesia, 1936, as the late aesthetic, and his house and library in Naples are ransacked by Fascists in November 1926, and he votes against the Lateran Pacts in the Senate in 1929 and attends the Senate from 1929 to 1934 to vote against the liberticide laws, and in 1938 he is the only non-Jewish intellectual who refuses the regime's racial questionnaire.

Phase 5, The parenthesis and the elder statesman, 1932-1952. The Fascist years are lived as a parenthesis: Fascism as moral illness, malattia morale, as government by asses, onagrocrazia, as the Hyksos invasion, and La storia come pensiero e come azione, 1938, states the creed, history is the story of liberty, and after Sunday 25 July 1943 he is the liberal elder statesman: the Bari congress of anti-Fascists, Saturday 29 January 1944, minister without portfolio in the second Badoglio government and the first Bonomi government, Saturday 22 April to Thursday 27 July 1944, president of the reconstituted Italian Liberal Party, 1944-1947, honorary president to 1948, member of the Consulta, deputy to the Constituent Assembly, where he speaks against the peace treaty, 24 July 1947, and against Article 7, and he resigns the party presidency on Sunday 30 November 1947 and founds the Istituto italiano per gli studi storici at Naples in February 1947, with Raffaele Mattioli's help, calling Federico Chabod to direct it, and giving it his library, and he returns to the Senate by right in May 1948 and votes only once more, for NATO accession in July 1949, and president of PEN International 1949-1952, and he dies at Naples on Thursday 20 November 1952, still working, the Taccuini di lavoro running to the end.

Early vs recent contrast: the young Croce writes erudite local history and Marxist polemic from inside libraries, and the old Croce writes the history of liberty from inside a dictatorship, and the philosophy becomes a civic act, and the system does not change, the fourfold spirit of 1902-1909 is still the frame in 1938, but its centre of gravity moves from aesthetics to history, and from history to the defence of liberty, and the early Croce says art is intuition, and the late Croce says liberty trembles in the lines of poets.

Posthumous afterlife. Federico Chabod, "Croce storico", Rivista storica italiana, 1953, the first portrait of Croce as historian after his death, and the Istituto italiano per gli studi storici carries the library into its second life, and Rosario Romeo, study #445, becomes the principal defender of Croce's memory after 1952, and Denis Mack Smith, study #444, turns his gratitude for the library into the extended refutation, the Storia d'Italia dismissed as "a partisan work written to exculpate retrospectively the author's own earlier sympathies for fascism", and the parenthesis called "historically naive and logically absurd", and Renzo De Felice, study #438, writes the long answer, Fascism with deep roots, not a bracket, and Eugenio Garin calls the parenthesis formula "falsissima in sede storiografica", and Gennaro Sasso and Giuseppe Galasso call it a political slogan, not a historiographical contribution, and the Taccuini di lavoro appear in six volumes, 1987-, and the Taccuini di guerra 1943-1945 in 2004, and the Carteggio Croce-Gentile in its integral edition in five volumes, 2014-2024, and the debate Croce framed, parenthesis or roots, still sets the terms of the argument.

## Aesthetic logic

Croce's aesthetic is the philosopher at a long table in a dark house, with four panes turning above him and one candle carried past him, and the table is always smaller than the library it stands in. Nothing in his work arrives as a mood: the parenthesis is drawn as a bracket and snapped shut, the four forms of the spirit are counted and then turned in their order, the lyric is sorted from the non-poetry by hand, the manifesto is signed and the page becomes a wall, the diary is filled square by square, and the piece that follows him must therefore be built from distinctions and verdicts, not from atmospheres. The palette follows the same law: the Naples ink in black and paper cream for the tribunal and the letters, the night-shirt white for the library at night, the candle gold for the religion of liberty, the Hyksos sand for the parenthesis, the iron grey for the law taken apart, the tribunal red for the diagnosis pinned to the door, and the dark of the great house around the one lit room, because the night is the portrait. Colour must arrive as a distinction, the four panes lighting in their order, the labels fluttering away, the three tokens staying put, never as decoration laid on top. His time is the long duration that ends in a verdict, so the loop must never merely repeat: the bracket must keep opening and snapping shut, the candle must keep passing from hand to hand, the calendar must keep filling square by square, and the end must come from the last entry in the Taccuini, because after a life of thinking there is exactly one convergence, and it is the library given away, and the giving away is the point.
