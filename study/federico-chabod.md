## Evolution

Three phases, and the phases are the argument, because Chabod's work is one long movement from the text to the archive to the institution, and each phase keeps the last one inside it. Phase 1, the Machiavelli and Milan scholar, 1924 to 1938: narrow, documentary, and polemical, the Turin thesis on the dating of the Prince, 1924, and the two Prince essays, 1924-1925, which made him, at 23, a voice in the Machiavelli controversies against Ercole and alongside Croce, and then the archival turn, Berlin under Meinecke, the Simancas trip where he met Braudel, and the Milan studies, Lo stato di Milano nell'impero di Carlo V, 1934, and the religious-history essay, 1938, built from documents in the Milanese and Spanish archives, and alongside them the Enciclopedia Italiana entries of the 1930s, "Illuminismo", "Rinascimento", "Borghesia", which compressed whole fields into single authoritative articles, see M1, M2, M3, M6, M7, M8, and the method is date the text, read the archive, answer the reigning doctrinaire. Phase 2, historicism, war, and the lecture as resistance, 1939 to 1945: by 1939 Chabod had worked out "a sophisticated version of historicism as the most reliable standard of historical research", accepting the reality of power politics as a fact while defending the cognitive potential of historical knowledge, and the 1942 essay on the concept of the Renaissance states the separation of spheres, see M5, and in the winter of 1943-44, under German occupation, he taught the Milan lectures on the idea of nation and the idea of Europe while German platoons marched outside, the classroom as a political act, see M14, M15, M16, M22, and in 1943 he joined the Resistance with the Action Party, and in November 1944 he hiked through snow into France to elude German troops, and he returned to Aosta in May 1945, and in the summer of 1945 he opposed French annexation of the Aosta Valley, arguing from historical continuity and at the same time demanding regional autonomy on federalist lines, and in September 1945 both goals were secured and he was elected the first president of the Region of Aosta Valley, and after a violent attack by the opposition in March 1946 he stepped down that summer, see M23, and the phase is the hinge of the career: the historian who had studied power from the outside now exercised it, and then deliberately quit politics for history. Phase 3, the institution-builder and synthesizer, 1946 to 1960: Rome chair, 1946, director of the Naples institute, February 1947 to his death, editor of the Rivista Storica Italiana, member of the Bureau of the International Committee of Historical Sciences, 1950, Sorbonne lectures, 1950, organizer of the epoch-making 1955 CISH congress in Rome, engagement for East European historians after 1956, and the one great book of the period is the 1951 foreign-policy history, a synthesis that is also a portrait of an age, with the Italian state, society, ruling class and political culture as its real subjects, see M9, M10, M11, M12, M13, and the late lectures on method and on contemporary Italy were published only after his death, see M18, M19, M20, M21, and the arc is from the text to the archive to the institution: first the dating of the Prince, then the state of Milan, then the school that would make the next generation of historians, De Felice among its first fellows, see M24. Posthumous afterlife, 1961 to 1995: the late lectures and the collected works appear after his death, L'idea di nazione and Storia dell'idea d'Europa and L'Italia contemporanea in 1961, the Einaudi Opere 1964-1972, and the third Milan volume into 1985, Lezioni di metodo storico in 1969, Idea di Europa e politica dell'equilibrio in 1995, and the Academia essay of 2010 notes the works "have been unfashionable for some years" but that interest in big historical questions and reliable historical knowledge is "making a comeback".

## Aesthetic logic

Chabod's aesthetic logic is that the document is the picture, and the document is not decoration, it is the mechanism, because a historian who keeps historiography clean from the passions of politics must show his sources, and the sources are the voices, and the voices are the archive, and the archive is the state, and the state is the idea, and the idea is the nation, and the nation is the Europe, and the Europe is the balance. The logic runs in three moves. First, the document: read the document until it speaks in its own voice, see M20, and the voice asks its own question, not the historian's, and the question is the past's, and the past's is the first-hand, and the first-hand is the reconstruction, and the reconstruction is the history, and the history is the document, and the document is the piece, and the piece must be a document before it is a picture, because the picture without the document is the myth, see M3, and the myth slides off the real, and the sliding off is the error. Second, the idea with a history: the nation, see M14 and M15, and Europe, see M16, are not places but ideas that change across the centuries, and the changing is the writing and rewriting, and the rewriting is the timeline, and the timeline is the colour, and the colour is the border, and the border moves, and the moving is the history, and the history is the idea, and the idea is the piece, and the piece must let the word be rewritten each century, because the word that does not change is the doctrine, and the doctrine is the error, see M18, and the error is the anachronism. Third, the absence: the historian absents himself, see M19, and the chair empties, and the documents arrange themselves, and the arranging is the story, and the story is truer for the chair being empty, and the empty is the responsibility, and the responsibility is the cognitive, and the cognitive is the document, and the document is the piece, and the piece must let the chair stay empty, because the narrator who fills it is the passion, and the passion is the politics, and the politics is the contamination, and the contamination is what the whole method exists to keep out, and the keeping out is the logic, and the logic is that the pieces that follow from it must show the document and the idea and the empty chair, and must report the positions of the 1943-44 lectures, taught under occupation, with attribution and never as decoration, because the teaching was the resistance, see M22, and the resistance is the point.
