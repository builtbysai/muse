# Sableraph (Raphaël de Courville), study #213

**Identity:** French code artist based in Berlin, @sableraph ("Code Artist •
Processing Community Lead @processing_core @processingorg • Co-host
@CreativeCodeBLN • Twitch #WCCChallenge"; 2,004 followers, 103 posts,
public). Born in Paris; creative coding since 2012 (a workshop with Julien
"v3ga" Gachadoat); Processing Community Lead Fellow at the Processing
Foundation since 2022 (announced on the Processing forum, Oct 10 2022);
founding member and co-host of Creative Code Berlin (since 2012); runs the
weekly #WCCChallenge creative coding challenge (weekly themed challenge on
his Discord, submissions reviewed live on Twitch Sundays); author of 100+
Creative Coding newsletters; past work includes interactive installations
with the Graffiti Research Lab Germany and ART+COM, a published Processing
library, and shader work. Network-adjacent pick per the lammetje-nl
template: he is the host of the Weekly Creative Coding Challenge that #210
Newyellow joined weekly through 2023 ("決定這學期在政大教課的時候，要每周都參加
@sableraph 的 Weekly Creative Coding Challenge", 2023-06-17), and he was on
the candidate shortlist in #211's block.txt. This is a WEB+IG HYBRID study:
103 posts pulled via instagram-cli (2018-06-06 to 2026-10-30), 20 frames
downloaded via og:image extraction (curl iPhone UA), all 20 visually
inspected in depth (10 generative works, 3 plotter videos, 3 community/
interview frames, 2 birb studies, 2 live-code/device frames), plus the web
leg (his own Processing Community Forum intro post, hive.blog's WCC
description, meetup.com Creative Code Berlin, the 2025 Advent Collaboration
artist blurb, IG captions).

**Core engine: THE PIECE IS A CONTRACT WITH TIME AND THE MARKET, AND THE
PRACTICE IS THE INFRASTRUCTURE THAT KEEPS THE CONTRACT.** His systems do
not complete inside a viewing session: a square decelerates over a thousand
years (1000 years), a cloth gains one stitch per day until 2163 (A Loom For
The Parcaes), a text erases itself as money is spent on it (Adam). The
watchable behavior is patience made visible: you return days apart and the
angle has changed, the thread has grown, the text has thinned. Around these
glacial engines runs a second engine, social rather than algorithmic: the
weekly challenge he hosts, the Sunday Twitch reviews, the Twitch
live-coding, the newsletters, the Berlin meetup he has co-hosted for over
a decade. Nobody else in this series builds community at the same rate they
build pieces; for Sableraph they are the same machine.

**Signature devices (observed):**
1. **The thousand-year deceleration (1000 years).** Originally published
   on Hic et Nunc in May 2021, edition of 1000. His caption: "This square
   will slow down over the next 1000 years until it finally comes to a
   rest on March 21st 3021." The 2026 repost ("1000 years (port), 2026.
   Out of series Lua/L5 port") shows the piece running on a handheld game
   console (f00): a light-blue rotating star/polygon on a dark screen, the
   console on a wooden table. The port proves the mechanism: the engine is
   so simple it runs anywhere, and its only parameter is the year.
2. **The daily stitch (A Loom For The Parcaes).** Posted 2022-08-08, dated
   January 12th 2022, released on Versum. "This generative cloth will be
   completed on the 31st of May 2163, adding a stitch per day, counting up
   from April 12th 1983." p5.js, html canvas, css. The caption tells the
   Jacquard history (punch cards, early computers) and the Parcae myth
   (Clotho, Lachesis, Atropos). f02 shows the cloth itself: a near-black
   field of fine vertical striations, illegible as a single frame,
   meaningful only as a rate. The calendar is the render loop.
3. **The self-erasing text (Adam).** August 2021, on Hic et Nunc. "An
   artwork that erases and overwrites itself gradually as more money is
   spent on it. The text is from Book IV Chapter 2 of The Wealth of
   Nations by Adam Smith. Each of the 10000 editions is priced at 3.9508
   Tez (XTZ) corresponding to the 39508 characters of the text. The
   purchase of one edition erases the same amount of characters from the
   text." f03 shows the dense text field, greyscale bands formed by tiny
   type. The market is an eraser; buying destroys. (The March 2021
   carousel caption shows he released on HEN deliberately after Tezos's
   proof-of-stake made it "much reduced impact," so the economics are
   part of the artwork, not its excuse.)
4. **The numbered Observations (the lab notebook, summer 2021).** Nine
   numbered monochrome motion studies, July to August 2021, posted as
   videos: f19 (Observation 3): sweeping soft spiral arcs on black; f16
   (Observation 6): comet-like white trails in a radial swirl, thin inner
   frame; f14 (Observation 9): two half-light half-dark split discs
   emitting concentric ring grids across the whole frame. f21 ("It's
   taking shape(s)", July 2) is the soft grey haze just before the series
   starts. Strict black and white, numbered in order, no titles beyond
   the number: the artist's lab notebook published as art.
5. **The birb system (2020-2022, dozens of posts).** One cute primitive,
   a round doodled bird, varied endlessly: ink-blot variation sheets (f11,
   2021-09-14: dozens of black ink birbs on white); a single outline
   birb with a red glowing heart (f06, 2022-02-24); a polished
   illustration version for Aurora (f10, "I made @auroramusic into a
   Birb"); birb-themed HEN collectibles arranged as flag-like red, white
   and blue clusters (f28, March 2021); cockatoo birbs, Halloween birbs,
   birb in progress posts. Device: personality through tiny deviations
   of one primitive, the opposite of his glacial systems: fast, cute,
   daily.
6. **The plotter as the hand.** An AxiDraw that draws his doodles: f30
   (Dec 2020 video) is the machine close-up, pen nibbling birbs onto
   paper, captioned "I just love doodling birds... So I built a simple
   tool to bring my sketches to the axidraw." Then he replaces the pen:
   f91 (Dec 2020, "#axidraw #plottertwitter #sesameseeds #spirals") is the
   plotter fitted with a dispenser drawing a spiral of black sesame
   seeds; f90 ("Spring", Jan 2021) is the pen drawing a tiny sprout
   squiggle on a vast blank sheet, the restraint is the piece.
7. **The live-coded shape soup (Twitch, May 2021).** f22 ("Live coded on
   Twitch on Sunday May 30th 2021"): dense translucent overlapping
   circles, triangles and diamonds in red, cyan, yellow, green and navy,
   multiply-style blending, playful and loud. The performance is the
   piece: coded in front of an audience, saved as a carousel frame.
8. **The pixel matrix (The Matrix, May 2021).** f23: a photograph of two
   monitors, laptop and Dell, one showing a colorful pixel grid, the other
   a cyan pixel grid, the studio as the frame. The grid is the medium and
   the hardware is the gallery.
9. **The coded diamond (Carat Clouds, Advent Collaboration Day 3,
   Dec 2025).** f01: a large emerald-cut gem at center, coded facets in
   cyan and violet, ringed by painted reaching hands in clouds on deep
   blue, a collaboration combining "coded diamond & painted hands."
   Device: generative precision meeting painterly body, the only piece
   where code renders something precious.
10. **The instrument builder (2026).** f56/f57: a Korg nanoKONTROL2 MIDI
    controller hooked to a p5.js sketch ("MIDI is not just for music! ...
    an add-on library to connect the Korg nanoKONTROL2 to a @p5xjs
    sketch"), him on camera with headphones in front of tall cartoon
    blob characters. The knobs are the interface; the sketch is the
    instrument. A natural late-stage move for a live-coder.

**Failure modes (observed limits, for prompt grounding):**
- The glacial pieces are unreadable as single frames: f02 (the loom) is
  nearly pure black at still-image resolution; the mechanism only exists
  across visits. A prompt must commit to the rate of change, not the
  frame.
- The text erasure has a floor: Adam consumed by all 10000 editions
  would be an empty field; the piece is most alive mid-erasure. Design
  for the middle of the process, not the end state.
- The cute primitive has a ceiling: birbs saturate fast (dozens of
  posts), and the polish of the Aurora piece (f10) breaks the doodle
  contract. Keep the primitive crude; the deviations are the content.
- Video posters hide the motion: f62's gaussianSplatting reel poster is
  a door photo; Observation motion is inferred from titles and stills.
  Never claim a motion behavior the still did not show.
- Scale honesty: the 1000 years port works because the engine is one
  number (angle) plus one date; any engine that needs a render farm
  cannot be ported to a handheld, and that portability is the proof.
