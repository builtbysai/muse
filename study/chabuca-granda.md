## M1. The divorce that made the composer

She married at twenty-two, divorced at thirty-two, and began composing at thirty, and the order is the mechanism. Maria Isabel Granda y Larco was born on Friday 3 September 1920 in the Cotabambas Auraria mining settlement in Apurimac, more than 4,000 metres up, the daughter of the mining engineer Eduardo Antonio Granda San Bartolome and Isabel Susana Larco Ferrari of Trujillo, and the family moved to Lima in 1923, and she spent her childhood to the age of twelve at a ranch in Bajada de los Banos in Barranco, and she sang from the age of twelve, a soprano, in the school choir, vice-president of the Asociacion de Canto, and in 1937 she formed the duo Luz y Sombra with Pilar Mujica Alvarez-Calderon, singing the Mexican music of Guty Cardenas on Radio Miraflores and Radio Nacional and at the Teatro La Cabana, and in 1940 she formed a trio with Martha and Rosario Gibson, and she worked as a secretary, and she acted in the Asociacion de Artistas Aficionados, and then, in 1942, she married the Brazilian military man and aviator Enrique Demetrio Fuller da Costa, and they had three children, Eduardo Enrique, Teresa Maria Isabel and Carlos Enrique Fuller Granda, and the marriage was brief, and it ended in divorce in 1952, and the divorce was seen as a scandal by the society of Lima at that time, and her deployment as a cantautora began precisely after the divorce. The mechanism is the scandal as the permission: the respectable married woman could not compose, and the divorced woman could, because she had already broken the rule that mattered, so the smaller rule, that a woman of her class did not write songs, no longer held her, and she took the name "Chabuca" then, a diminutive that is also a mask, a name that is not the baptismal name, and she wore it for the rest of her life.
