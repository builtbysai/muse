## Core engine

**THE ETHER IS FULL; THE ARTIST IS THE TUNER: scan what is already
being transmitted, recorded, or archived, then slow it, loop it,
degrade it, and re-site it until it means something new.**

The named mechanisms:
1. **Intercept-and-dramatize.** 1992: buys a radio scanner from a
   friend, starts working intercepted mobile-phone conversations
   into music in real time, adding musical and emotional weight to
   fragments that were never meant to be heard. The moniker is the
   method: scanning the airwaves for content. Corpus continuation:
   "recording cities whilst travelling instead of taking photos"
   (2025-11-14); the device itself exhibited as a relic (The Horror
   Show, Somerset House, 2022, f05), photographed B&W like a museum
   object. Watchable behavior: a sensor sweeps a band, fragments
   are caught, held, weighted, released.
2. **Slow, loop, degrade.** The Esprits de Paris method: Paris field
   recordings slowed down and looped until the distortions become
   the composition. Ongoing studio ritual: 15-second Strega loops
   for a 12-hour radio broadcast at Het Concreet (2023-11-16);
   Make Noise + Strymon tape-loop sessions (2018-10-27, f01). The
   material is never fresh; it is worn down until it speaks.
3. **Archive-as-instrument.** 5,000+ hours of spoken-word archives
   (2025 Strom newsletter); Scanner:Library (Soniccouture, 2026):
   401 recordings, most recovered from cassettes (police-scanner
   intercepts, electronic improvisations, location audio), three
   identical layers, 10M+ combinations, 200 presets. The Instagram
   feed itself is a daily visual archive of other artists' works.
   Mechanism: a finite pile of fragments recombined combinatorially,
   never repeated.
4. **Site-specific spatial sound.** Sound designed to fit and reveal
   a space's behavior: Bridging the World (2014, Museum of London
   Docklands), a walk-through river of hanging speakers; Ricochet
   (Tang Teaching Museum): slowed bat echolocation mapped to an
   elevator's rebounds between floors, spatial behavior mirroring
   as composition; Salles des Depart and Channel of Flight,
   permanent installations in a working public morgue (Raymond
   Poincare hospital, Garches); Water Drops (Rijeka Airport);
   Sound Curtains (Science Museum London). Corpus: the morgue
   installation confirmed permanent, 2025-11-14, f18.
5. **Public-participation collage.** Deloitte Ignite (artsdesk Q&A):
   the public emailed in sounds for a fortnight; he wove them into
   the installation after manipulating them "though having been
   manipulated by Scanner's fiendish software, they are unlikely to
   be easily recognisable." Bridging the World took public bridge
   recordings and personal stories. The crowd supplies the raw
   fragments; the system anonymizes them into material.
6. **Serial collaboration.** Every era is defined by a partner: Mike
   Kelley (Esprits de Paris, 2001), Stephen Vitiello (Sound
   Surface, 2004), Wayne MacGregor (Qualia, 2004, Royal Ballet),
   Joel Cadbury (The Big Dance, 2012), Erik Kessels + Stefano Pilia
   (Un'immagine, 2025). Corpus: "I'm a serial collaborator." 75
   contemporary dance productions scored. The partner changes the
   instrument.
7. **Score-to-image.** Re-soundtracked Godard's Alphaville (1998,
   performed worldwide); live-scored Harry Smith's Early
   Abstractions (1946-52) at Harry Smith at 100 (Brighton, Nov
   2024, f15: performer as silhouette under the projection);
   Un'immagine: 30,000 images from the 7M-photo Publifoto archive
   stitched into one continuously moving photograph, live-scored
   for 500 guests at Gallerie d'Italia (2025). Images exist; he
   gives them new time.
8. **Rework, re-present.** Greengrassi 2022: "surround sound work
   that reworks early material of mine in a new way"; Electric
   Dreams opening performance, OGR Turin, 31 Oct 2026. Nothing is
   finished; everything is re-tuned for the new room.

## Evolution

- **1982-1991:** begins composing for professional productions
  (Eyebeam). Early releases on his own? no, debut later.
- **1992-1997:** buys the radio scanner (~1992); debut CD
  Scanner on Ash International (Touch subsidiary), working with
  Mike Harding. Co-founds the I/O/D webzine with Matthew Fuller
  and Graham Harwood (1994, one of the first experimental
  e-zines). Mass Observation (1994), Scanner2, Delivery (1997).
  Sets up and curates The Electronic Lounge at London's ICA,
  1994-2000.
- **1998-2000:** Surface Noise performed on a London bus for
  Artangel; Imaginaria 99 Award for Digital Arts (ICA); becomes
  'Professor Scanner' at John Moores University, Liverpool.
  Alphaville re-soundtrack premiered, performed worldwide.
- **2001-2004:** Esprits de Paris developed with Mike Kelley;
  Salles des Depart permanent installation in the working morgue
  at Raymond Poincare hospital, Garches (2001); Channel of Flight
  (2003). Sound Surface with Stephen Vitiello (2004), the first
  Tate Modern sound-art commission. Qualia for Wayne MacGregor,
  Royal Ballet (2004).
- **2007-2013:** Kirikou & Karaba score (2007); Philips Wake-Up
  Light sound design (2009). The Big Dance (2012): score for 1,000
  dancers in Trafalgar Square, broadcast on Channel 4. Live_
  Transmission: Joy Division Reworked with Heritage Orchestra
  (Brighton Festival 2012). Scanner: A Month in the Life of an
  Artist, Melkweg Amsterdam (2013).
- **2014-2019:** Bridging the World speaker-river installation
  (Museum of London Docklands, 2014); Vex house with Chance de
  Silva architects, Stoke Newington (2017, permanent soundtrack;
  RIBA London Award 2018, Grand Designs). Cisco telephone-system
  sound worldwide (2019). VR ballet Nightfall score (Dutch
  National Ballet, 2016).
- **2020-2023:** lockdown fecundity: nine albums since 2020
  (Musicians' Union). An Ascent (DiN, 2020). Scanner Sunday
  livestreams (2021). Tape-loop rituals documented; Het Concreet
  residency (2023). Corpus becomes a daily diary in 2023 (273
  posts).
- **2024:** Esprits de Paris (multi-screen audio-visual w/
  Kelley, homage to EVP researchers Jurgenson/Raudive) enters the
  Centre Pompidou permanent collection. Own label Alltagsmusik
  ("Everyday Music") launched: Alchemeia (Jan 2024), The Phenol
  Tapes (recorded 2017, Captiva Island FL), The Berklee Sessions.
  Harry Smith at 100: live scoring of Early Abstractions,
  Attenborough Centre Brighton, 15 Nov 2024.
- **2025:** Savvy Award, Amsterdam ("world's only award for
  innovation across all disciplines"). Un'immagine with Erik
  Kessels + Stefano Pilia (Gallerie d'Italia Turin, Sept):
  30,000 images from the 7M Publifoto archive stitched into one
  continuously moving photograph, live-scored for 500 guests
  (f17). Forces, Reactions, Deflections (Quiet Details, geophone-
  sourced).
- **2026:** Scanner:Library (Soniccouture, Oct; 2 years in the
  making): 401 original recordings, three layers, 10M+
  combinations, 100+ presets designed by him. Electric Dreams:
  Art & Technology Before the Internet opening performance, OGR
  Turin, 31 Oct 2026.

## What to take for prompts

Rimbaud is overwhelmingly sonic, so every prompt is an honest
translation of a mechanism into a visual system, never a claim
about his visuals. His own visual grammar (device portraiture,
gear still-lifes, stage silhouettes under projections, the
curved wooden Esprits de Paris wall, gallery halls walled in
archival photographs) is decoration; the mechanisms are the
drivers. The translatable moves: scanning a band for fragments
and weighting them (intercept-and-dramatize), wearing material
down by repetition until it speaks (slow/loop/degrade), a finite
pile of fragments recombined combinatorially (archive-as-
instrument), behavior mirroring between a space and a field
(elevator rebound logic), crowd-supplied raw material the system
anonymizes (public-participation collage), the partner changing
the instrument (serial collaboration), giving existing images new
time (score-to-image), and re-tuning finished work for the new
room (rework).
