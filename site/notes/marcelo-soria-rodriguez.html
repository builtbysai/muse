Core engine

THE PIECE IS A PROGRESSIVE PAINTING MACHINE AND IDENTITY IS THE PARAMETER VECTOR: every system he hand-codes paints itself progressively through time (the painting process is the artwork, and viewers may pause it mid-stroke or watch it vanish and repaint); the work traverses a parameter space bound to a homage aesthetic (Delaunay's concentric rings, straight-line string fields, contrapuntal voices); and identity, whether a Twitter account, his daughters' drawings, or himself, enters only as data that informs the system's choices, with human-like imperfection deliberately kept against machine precision.

Signature devices observed

  1. The polycircle engine (Entretiempos). His flagship system, an Art Blocks project (1,000 iterations, minted March 2022). The Art Blocks interview states it directly: "Entretiempos emerges from the intersection of concentric rings and the shapes that are formed therein, as an approximation to some of the elements that Sonia Delaunay used in her paintings." His stated goal was to recreate Delaunay's style and "explore what happens when you traverse parameter space with that aesthetic." Observed: f06 (Entretiempos #635) dense overlapping ring clusters with heavy black hatch and guilloche line textures; f05 ("the system in its almost final form," Dec 2021) a huge red disk with translucent intersecting circles and arcs; f04 (Nov 2021, "delaunay + the passage of time at different time scales") colliding arc groups in yellow, cyan, black. The engine's watchable behavior is rings spawning and intersecting; intersections are the subject, not the rings.
  2. Normal side vs reveal side. f07 (Entretiempos #907, exhibited at the Venice Biennale 2022 Decentral Art Pavilion) shows the two faces of one work: the normal side is fine-lined, hatched, densely structured; the reveal side is smooth blurred radial color bands (yellow, green, teal, navy). One system, two readings: the mechanism exposed and the mechanism dissolved.
  3. The progressive paint machine (with vanishing mode). From the interview: the system lets viewers pause the artwork and "see how it paints progressively and by parts." Pressing "r" then "y" activates a painting-vanishing mode "to see how the artwork is created within small time-scales." He describes spending hours watching outputs, "pausing in some of them, reloading them, enjoying the paint process." The work is not an image; it is a performance of painting. There is also "a hidden Easter egg in every work."
  4. Human-like imperfection kept against machine precision. From the interview: he is interested in "the emotions that the artwork may trigger, when the produced image has human-like imperfections or when the image retains all its geometrical purity with machine precision." His machine-feeling inquiry: "Potential machine consciousness and emotions are a topic that interests me much: what would a machine feel with these generative works?" The roughness is a parameter, not a bug.
  5. Identity as parameter (the sabbatical system). His first proper code-based generative work (sabbatical era): a system in the style of Spanish painter Eusebio Sempere built from Twitter data in Python, where the idea was "to create some sort of identity from a given Twitter account, using several data points to inform the choices in the system." This is the ancestor of everything after: identity as a parameter vector, not a portrait.
  6. String theories: form from pure straight lines. A series "that explored how shapes and spaces are generated from pure straight lines." f11 (centrifugal detail, Oct 2022) shows this instinct pushed to violence: dense diagonal red line fields cutting across gold and blue masses, where line density alone becomes form.
  7. Contrapuntos: voices in counterpoint. His "first long-form generative artwork" (2021): musical counterpoint as a drawing system, consistent with his piano playing and composition background. Multiple independent stroke-voices that obey a shared rhythmic law without mirroring each other.
  8. The centrifugal spiral. f10 (Oct 2022, "centrifugal"): a roaring spiral mass on red ground, layered arcs of cream, black radial streaks, gold masses, a pink core ringed in red, painted in heavy kinetic strokes. The piece is pure rotational energy; the watchable behavior is a whirlpool that never settles.
  9. The childlike discovery machine. "el inefable momento del colorido descubrimiento" (May 2022), created for Art Basel Hong Kong 2022 with 1,200 unique outputs: "a tribute to my daughters' drawings and to everyone's journeys of discovery." f08 shows the mechanism: rainbow crayon arcs, scattered hearts, starbursts and streaks on coral ground, deliberately naive marks generated by code. The same hand that plots guilloche chooses to draw like a child.
  10. The self-trained model era. "humans loving each other" (Sept 2024, f12): the caption is explicit, "(custom models trained on my own hand-coded generative systems)." Soft monochrome blobby ghost-figures. Identity Studies Series 0 (July 2026, three years in the making): "nine unique works on the states identity moves through: intermediate, dreamt, synthetic, but identity nonetheless. made by hand, code, and machine; each a 1 of 1." Series 1 (Oct 2026, 20 1-of-1s): starts from Ortega y Gasset, "I am myself and my circumstance... AI has now entered that circumstance." f16 (Series 0 cover): blue dancers mid-motion against a violet grain wall. f15 (Series 0 No.02): a grainy ritual scene, golden cross over a crowd. f17 (Series 1): hooded fabric-swaddled faces, baroque chiaroscuro. The arc closes: the artist's own generative corpus becomes the training data, and the model becomes the new other.
  11. Generative Dialogues: the minute composition. Generative Dialogues V (Blanc! festival, La Nau Barcelona, Oct 23-24, 2026, presented by The Generative Art Museum): "Six artworks, sixteen synchronised screens, and a new composition every minute. An invitation to pause, observe, and discover how the same set of rules can unfold into endless possibilities." His contribution, "variaciones del yo": "Intersecting circles, fragmented perspectives, and shifting resolutions give visual form to the limits of our perception. The work invites us to pause and influence its rhythm, turning contemplation into an experience of shared transformation." f19 shows its poster: pixelated purple and magenta banding, olive arcs, black blob masses with green circle targets, raw digital edges.
  12. The photographic eye underneath. The account's first decade (2011-2019) is abstract photography: f00 (2014, "dis/continued") light through a slit in a dark door; f01 (2014) a keyhole view of a blurred foot; f02 (2019, "geometrías cotidianas") a chair edge in orange and dark blue. He found geometry with a camera for ten years before he found it with code. "ópera posthumana de gramática sintética" (Jan 2025, f13): minimalist organ-pipe bars in orange, red, black, white on sage green, the quietest frame in the sample.

Evolution: early vs recent

  • 2011-2016: abstract photography diary. Light slits, keyhole views, interiors, "madridismos," travel, daily geometries. No code; the geometry is found, not made.
  • 2016-2017 (sabbatical): the hinge. Photographic work with coloured wooden pieces (a motif back to 2004, "Rearrangements of coloured wooden pieces"), and the first code system: Sempere-style portraits built from Twitter account data in Python. Identity becomes a parameter.
  • 2019: an acrylic-on-cardboard tribute to Sonia Delaunay (per the interview); the motif painted by hand first, code later.
  • 2020: lockdown. Leaves the corporate job "for good just before the pandemic," dedicates himself full time to art, starts coding the ancestor of Entretiempos mid-2020. First reads about NFTs in December 2020 (the Beeple auction), followed by "three months of non-stop research."
  • 2021: the generative explosion. 164 genart posts in one year. First fxhash project deployed Nov 13, 2021 (fxhash.xyz/generative/65, "4 test images from my project"). Contrapuntos (first long-form). "the system in its almost final form" (Dec 2). The iillucid aesthetic hardens: concentric rings, polycircles, hatch, Delaunay homage.
  • 2022: the institutional year. Art Blocks "In Conversation" interview (Feb 10); Entretiempos mints 1,000 iterations on Art Blocks (March); Entretiempos #907 (normal and reveal sides) shown at the Venice Biennale Decentral Art Pavilion (April); the Art Basel Hong Kong 2022 Tezos interactive fxhash installation (May 27-29) with eight co-artists, plus his tribute piece "el inefable momento del colorido descubrimiento" (1,200 outputs, playable); "centrifugal" (October), the violent spiral; "amanece" (May), the quiet honeycomb-grid detour.
  • 2023-2024: a quieter IG (24 posts across two years). "humans loving each other" (Sept 2024): the first caption to name custom models trained on his own hand-coded generative systems. The loop starts eating itself.
  • 2025: "ópera posthumana de gramática sintética" (Jan, companion piece, minimalist bars); "non-quantum superpositions of human/machine emotional states" (referenced as the predecessor of Series 0); "from the archives of unborn images" (Dec, raw gestural rings on mustard).
  • 2026: the identity years. Identity Studies Series 0 (July, 9 works, three years in the making, "intermediate, dreamt, synthetic"); Series 1 (October, 20 1-of-1s, Ortega y Gasset, "AI has now entered that circumstance"); Generative Dialogues V at Blanc! festival (Oct 23-24, 16 synchronised screens, a new composition every minute); "variaciones del yo" (circles, fragmented perspectives, shifting resolutions, influenceable rhythm).
