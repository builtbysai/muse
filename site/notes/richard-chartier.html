LA-based sound artist, composer, and graphic designer. Curator of LINE (a 12k sublabel, founded 2000 after Deupree suggested on the phone that Chartier's rejected "Series" become a 12k offshoot). As Pinkcourtesyphone he makes the pop-facing register: pink monochrome covers, camp luxury. His graphic design IS his visual practice: 25 years of covers as a single ongoing design system.

Core engine

Reduction as instrument: isolate one sonic or visual variable at the threshold of perception and repeat it until the space it occupies becomes the content. Silence is the material, monochrome is the field, typography is the image.

The named mechanisms:

  1. The threshold test. Sound placed at the edge of audibility; the listening room and its background

noise finish the piece. The mechanism is the same as Deupree's zone test (#228) but pitched one step lower: where Deupree keeps a loop until nothing sticks out, Chartier keeps reducing until the silence around the sound becomes audible content.

  1. File-exchange composition. SPEC. (12k1007, 1999/2000 with Deupree): exchange of source files, each

remixing the other's material. The collab protocol is the compositional method.

  1. The cover system as score. The LINE visual identity is codified like music: white header band,

black sans ARTIST, thin rule, italic serif title; below it, one monochrome field with one object or gradient (f11, f15, f18, f19). Twenty-five years without breaking the system; evolution happens inside it (Basinski's icy paintings, the Koenig tonometer rods, Strecker's blurred pink gradient).

  1. Monochrome + small object. The Pinkcourtesyphone cover grammar (f01): one flat color field, one

small thing on it, minimal lowercase typography. Reduction made commercial: the cover is the hook.

  1. Atmosphere as document. Personal photography: pale grey skies with faint double rainbows (f05),

bare branches against cobalt (f06), overcast seascapes (f08). His eye photographs the same near-nothing his ears compose; the Sugimoto-adjacent taste is explicit (Specification.Fifteen was built against Sugimoto's Seascapes, Hirshhorn 2006).

  1. The curator's ledger. The Dublab show "between two points" (running since 2017, f17: black ground,

thin white lowercase sans, orange date) is a monthly spine; the feed's recent era is the label's archive (LINE 25th anniversary monument f14, Transparency 15 years, CoH anniversaries). The system catalogs itself.

Evolution

  • 2017-2019, the personal notebook: camp flea-market paintings (f02), vinyl piles for performance

evenings (Propaganda, Tietchens visible; f03), prismatic diffraction experiments (f04), atmospheric phone photos. Pinkcourtesyphone EPs; the monochrome cover system already fixed (f01).

  • 2020-2023, the curator's ledger: Dublab show becomes the spine; LINE promos dominate; text graphics

bolder (f10 blackout poetry); Basinski paintings enter the cover program (f11); Serra steel plates with Evelina Domnitch, playful (f09).

  • 2024-2026, institutional minimalism: anniversary monuments; the LINE header system perfectly codified

(f15 Chroma); camp-pink register persists in the margins (f16 macaron box). The feed is a label archive with a human margin: the flea-market eye never left, it just got footnoted.

What to take for prompts

Chartier's grammar is the purest reduction vocabulary of the whole 12k strand: threshold-of-perception material, monochrome fields, one small object, typography as image. For generative pieces the translatable systems are: a field that holds near-nothing so the near-nothing becomes content; a codified cover system where evolution happens inside fixed rules (the fly equivalent of his header band); monochrome gradients with one accent (the Basinski ice + single red line); slow accumulation toward an anniversary monument. Interaction is never earned in this work: watch-only. The pink/camp register is the decoration that proves the minimalism is a choice, not a limitation.
