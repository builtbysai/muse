Shelf IV, The Living Sketch

Aluan Wang (王奕方 / Wang Hsin-Jen) — study #211

studied 2021-09-01/7 min read/1,473 words
Identity
Taiwanese creative coder and new-media artist (b. 1982, Taichung; art school, trained in watercolor and ink before code). Instagram @ileivoivm (the crypto-art name "Ileivoivm" is "Aluan Wang" encrypted with an Enigma machine; bio: "Living w/ digital art for more than 10 years"; 1,476 followers, 305 posts, public; site aluanwang.com). First Taiwanese artist to release on Art Blocks (Good Vibrations, 2021). Began in interactive design on large-scale installations; known in Taiwan's audiovisual scene for geometric visuals with electronic breakbeat rhythm. Network-adjacent pick per the lammetje-nl template: dialog() 2024 TAIPEI co-exhibitor of #209 Che-Yu Wu and #210 Newyellow (niewmedia lineup), and Project Percentage co-artist with both (TaiwanInVienna / FAB DAO / Ars Electronica 2022). This is a WEB+IG hybrid study: 271 posts pulled via instagram-cli (2021-09-01 to 2026-10-25), 22 stratified across eras, 21 frames downloaded via og:image, 21 visually inspected in depth, plus web leg (Verse, matters.town Chaos trilogy exegesis, tzapac Art Basel 2022, ICONIC InkField).

Core engine: EVERY WORK IS A THREE-ACT PLAY ABOUT ENCRYPTION, SAMPLING, AND GROWTH — the system consumes something (his NFT collection, Turner watercolors, his own past works, nature's parameters) and re-grows it encrypted. His own line: "Algorithms are used to remodel history and point cloud to subvert conventional thinking." The ICONIC line that names it: "simple rules, repeated with small drift, grow into something complex and alive." Watchable behavior: you never see the source, you see its remnant — a flow field that refuses to be pure Perlin (it carries deformed samples of works he collected), a watercolor that is Turner's clouds run through his sampling, a new trilogy act that re-grows from the pixels of the last.

Signature devices (observed):

  1. Self-sampling flow fields (the anti-Perlin). Chaos Research (fxhash,

Dec 2021, <1 month after fxhash launched): the flow is Perlin noise PLUS "mysterious signals" — deformed IPFS versions of his own Tezos NFT collection, which become the basis for the dynamic changes, particles, colors, and shapes. A rebellion against Perlin-noise dominance (the matters.town exegesis frames it as the generative-art answer to Fidenza).

  1. Regenerative trilogies (remnant inheritance). Research > Memory >

Culture: "the latter produces new works from the remnants of the former." Chaos Memory (Apr 2022) samples and compresses Research; its hidden IPFS files hold sampled William Turner watercolor bitmaps (the Romantic landscape painter was his lifelong pursuit as an art-school watercolorist); pressing Q/W/E/R reshapes the work into rectangle, square, or circle ("a single scale and perspective cannot fully present the appearance of Memory"). Chaos Culture (May 2022, Art Basel HK, circular petri-dish frame) samples both and was the trilogy's conclusion. All his works are three-act plays (audiovisual roots: Transonic 2012, Moving Resonance 2011).

  1. Parametric plants (Polypaths, since 2023). "In nature, every plant

emerges like a finely tuned set of parameters in motion." Polypaths: phyllotaxy (opposite/alternate/whorled), internode length adjusting to light, leaf margin (entire/serrated/undulate), branching angle, node density — the plant's posture and rhythm as a parameter vector. 776 artworks on Verse (July 2024), Galerie Met Berlin solo exhibition (Aug 2025). Frames f00, f02, f16, f17: ringed petal heads on trailing stems, luminous night vine in gold and silver, a procedural plant wreath in a tan roundel (fern fronds, seed pods, butterfly forms).

  1. The gesture as data (InkField, Transient Labs 2026). Fifty 1/1

interactive works; each piece "records the act of painting as data, then re-performs that gesture live in the browser each time it is opened." The digital brush is "rigged like a real one, a spring held between hand and tip, so that moving fast thins the line and moving slow lets it press and bloom." His hand learned watercolor and ink before code; the code makes the hand visible again.

  1. One signal, two instruments (audiovisual symmetry). Good Vibrations

(Art Blocks 2021): sound and visuals from one signal, "perfect symmetry" between them; influenced by Golan Levin and by Toshio Iwai's visualized instruments (his dialog() 2024 Q&A answer). He airdropped the B-side to all GV owners: "a score or a menu," the work's sound-and-visual logic exposed — the second act. Live form: the 2025 Sound Art Festival at C-LAB (frame f13): two performers at laptop tables before a wall of flowing orange liquid light.

  1. Interacting systems, unpredictable interplay (Equinox). Verse View

series, July 2024, 64 artworks, 24-hour ranked auction: "several interacting systems whose interplay results in unpredictable outcomes, embodying seasonal transitions." Frames f09-f11: dry-brush gestural abstraction on white, gold torn against grey.

  1. The daily log landscape (2026). "今天日誌，我們有河了:)" (2026-10-05,

frame f18): a cartoon-flat landscape — blue sky, tree, hatched purple mountains, yellow field, power lines — a system that gains one feature per day. Dev-adjacent frames: parametric quadruped creatures labeled by breed (J 邊牧 collie, K 柴犬 shiba, frame f19: "開發這part 讓我心情很愉悅" — "developing this part makes me happy"); a raw dev screenshot of the vector plant tool with 3D coordinate tooltip visible (frame f21).

  1. Ink-wash data landscapes. Frame f12 (2024-03-11): Chinese ink-wash

mountains rendered in code, overlaid with fine golden thread-lines connecting nodes like constellations above a hatched "lake." Frame f03 (2022-07-14): watercolor wash abstract with hand-drawn contour loops. GeoPunk (Path series, hicetnunc): encrypted digital landscapes whose sound and description are in Morse code pointing to real GPS locations.

Evolution:

  • 2011-2015: audiovisual era (Transonic, Moving Resonance), Taipei Arts

Festival top honors 2012 and 2015; breakbeat + geometric visuals.

  • 2019-2021: crypto-art / encryption era: Path series, GeoPunk (Morse-code

landscapes, MoCA Taiwan), Good Vibrations on Art Blocks (first Taiwanese artist on the platform).

  • Dec 2021-May 2022: Chaos trilogy on fxhash (Research/Memory/Culture),

synced with fxhash's own launch; Chaos Culture at Art Basel Hong Kong 2022's Tezos exhibition (250m², 8-artist interactive fxhash installation with Jinyao Lin).

  • 2023-2024: plant logic (Polypaths, 776 works, Verse July 2024; Galerie

Met Berlin solo Aug 2025); diary series (Diary of the Sea: flat Matisse-like cutouts, 2023-09); ink-wash algorithm phase (Automatic Messages: L-system painting machine; Equinox: interacting systems, July 2024); dialog() 2024 Asian Generative Art Exhibition (Tokyo/Taipei).

  • 2025: institutional sound (Sound Art Festival, C-LAB); ART TAIPEI x

COMPUTEX 2026; National Taiwan Museum of Fine Arts contexts.

  • 2026: InkField on Transient Labs (50 1/1 interactive works, gesture as

data, "Inside inkField" interview series July 2026); the daily-log landscape and creature systems in development (Sep-Oct); Art Jakarta 2026 with Galerie OVO ("Nature's Second Language").
