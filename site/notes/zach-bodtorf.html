Zach Bodtorf - study #226 (2026-10-31)

studied 2026-10-31/8 min read/1,542 words

Identity

  • Instagram: @zachbodtorf (verified via instagram-cli account-search + user-profile; id 17841400062640047).
  • Name: Zach Bodtorf; creator account, US, PUBLIC, 2,515 followers, 684 posts, bio of three wave emojis, no website, not verified.
  • Background: audio mastering engineer (Vermont) who stumbled into generative art in 2018 while writing code to analyze audio dynamics for mastering work (StandardVision "Artist Spotlight: Generative Artist Zach Bodtorf", 2018-11-08). Mastered Argot's Oh? EP; did illustration/animation for Du Blonde's "Holiday Resort" and was called her "coding wizard" for the game GardenBoy (The Line of Best Fit, 2019-02-18).

Core engine

THE SOUND IS THE WEATHER: import audio (or pink noise) as a table of 10+ million points, slice it into ~5ms micro-samples, and feed those samples as if they were meteorological readings into wind-mapping/plotting software (Python matplotlib), so sound becomes streamlines, wind barbs, contour lines, and scalar colormap fields. His own description (StandardVision, 2018-11-08): "I trick meteorological wind plotting software to receive fragments of audio rather than wind data." Extra Chill (2019-03-01) on the Argot cover: built with "a Python script similar to the one that the National Weather Service uses to create wind maps."

Evolution (early vs recent, dated)

  • Jan to Jun 2018: personal photos only (cats, flowers, escalator, concert). No art.
  • 2018-07-06: FIRST GENERATIVE POST "#wave a497 {sound+wind plot}". The wave series (hex-coded titles like a497, 8089, 1731, fedf) = daily sound+wind-plot glyphs.
  • Jul to Nov 2018: dense white line fields on near-black (f00); monochrome cube-grid line rain (f01); "5ms of pink noise" minimal prints, 33x33 inches (caption 2018-10-14). StandardVision SVLA1 screen takeover (four stories tall, Downtown LA), announced on IG 2018-10-30 (285 likes, 104 comments: his most-commented post).
  • Nov 2018 to Feb 2019: scalar colormap turbulence in hot/inferno palette (f02); streamplot waves over dusty rose (f03); thresholded contourf ink washes (f04); near-white minimal dome (f05; Agnes Martin influence, per StandardVision); translucent chartreuse fill_between curves (f06); faceted 3D trisurf "diamond shard" renders on black (f07); stippled B/W density surfaces (f08).
  • Spring 2019: topographic contour-line triangle (f09); teal terrain ridgelines (f10); framed coral-ridge prints shown as physical objects (f11); crisp geometric masks (moon disc + staircase) over a starfield (f12); GAN experiments (2019-07-16 caption: generating little snippets of audio with GANs "perfect to use as data").
  • Mid to late 2019: terracotta chevron-brick videos (f13); nested translucent red squares, Albers-like (f14); diagonal gradient bars (f15); Blender 3D noodle experiment captioned "Initial failed attempt" (f16, 2019-07-31, #b3d); flat geometric minimalism in terracotta/grey (f17, 2019-10-31).
  • 2020: stark black-and-white boolean mask geometry (f18, 2020-10-18).
  • 2021: Seaglass series (2021-10-13): "sixteen 3x4 arrangements of digital sea glass pieces formed using a combination of overlapping Bezier curves and squares," inspired by "old wine bottles, car tail lights, and medicine vials"; released as 16 NFTs at 0.65 ETH with ARTXCODE (nftnow, ~Dec 2021). Squarewave series (2021-11-16): "glyphs generated using pulse waves, where the amplitude alternates evenly between a minimum and maximum value... inspired by types of signal processing used in music."
  • 2022: data visualizations for Greg Davis's "New Primes" release on grayfade (inner-magazines, 2022-09-22). Last IG post 2022-05-29 ("From the archives // July 2019"): account quiet since.

Frame notes (20/20 inspected, frames2/f00-f19; oldest to newest)

  • f00 (2018-09-13): hundreds of overlapping translucent white curved lines on dark green-black, a fabric-like wave mass. Mechanism: dense line field (matplotlib overkill); this is the signature that inspired Penne's layered-line approach.
  • f01 (2018-10-05): isometric wireframe cube grid in greys on off-white, with vertical line rain over the cubes. Mechanism: line density over a 3D grid.
  • f02 (2018-11-06): marigold flat background with a stepped block of red-orange fractal turbulence intruding. Mechanism: hot-colormap noise field with a geometric mask.
  • f03 (2018-11-29): dense wavy red/yellow streamlines over a dusty-rose band. Mechanism: streamplot of a flow field; the wind plot made visible.
  • f04 (2018-12-29): pink/magenta wash with dark navy blobs and thin striations. Mechanism: thresholded scalar field (contourf with few levels).
  • f05 (2019-01-18): near-white minimal dome silhouette with faint hatching, on near-white. Mechanism: single filled curve, extreme restraint.
  • f06 (2019-01-26): overlapping translucent chartreuse parabolas. Mechanism: layered fill_between curves with alpha.
  • f07 (2019-02-06): faceted polyhedral "shards" floating on near-black. Mechanism: matplotlib 3D trisurf renders of generated meshes.
  • f08 (2019-02-16): B/W grainy surface with a diagonal ridge. Mechanism: stippled/scatter density over a curved surface.
  • f09 (2019-02-26): triangular pyramid of hundreds of concentric contour rings in tiers. Mechanism: contour lines over a triangle-shaped domain.
  • f10 (2019-03-16): diagonal teal/green ridged terrain. Mechanism: shaded relief of a noise field with viridis-like colormap.
  • f11 (2019-04-20): framed physical print of a coral-pink ridged mound over pink/yellow gradient. Mechanism: ridged 3D surface, presented as a print.
  • f12 (2019-05-11): black starfield with a pinkish moon disc and a descending staircase. Mechanism: crisp geometric masks composited on a noise field.
  • f13 (2019-06-02): terracotta chevron bricks with soft gradient shadows (video thumbnail). Mechanism: geometric tile pattern, animated.
  • f14 (2019-06-20): concentric translucent rose squares with a dark center. Mechanism: nested alpha fills (Albers-like).
  • f15 (2019-07-17): diagonal peach/salmon/charcoal gradient bars. Mechanism: gradient-filled diagonal bands.
  • f16 (2019-07-31): vertical stack of irregular cream tube-rings on grey, captioned "Initial failed attempt" (#b3d). Mechanism: Blender 3D tube curves; a medium experiment outside matplotlib.
  • f17 (2019-10-31): flat terracotta/grey/white diagonal bar composition with crisp shadows. Mechanism: hard-edged geometric minimalism.
  • f18 (2020-10-18): interlocking black circles and white diamonds on white. Mechanism: boolean mask geometry, no gradients.
  • f19 (2022-05-29): dense black line-field of palm-frond streamlines fanning from vertical spines, captioned "From the archives // July 2019". Mechanism: streamline plot of a synthetic vector field; the wind-plot engine abstracted from sound.
