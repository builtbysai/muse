Chris Hyson (study #252)

London producer / composer / multi-instrumentalist, studied for the generative-doodles prompt pipeline, 2026-11-01. Verified subject: @chrishyson ("Chris Hyson", public, 3,645 followers, user_id 17841401575150197, chrishyson.com). His bio is the whole method in two words: "texture collector". Caution: @snowpoet_ is his band's account, not his; his Snowpoet co-leader is @laurenkinsellavoice (study #251).

Core engine

Four interlocking mechanisms:

  1. Texture collecting as daily practice. The bio states the program outright, and the corpus proves it: dozens of sky, bird, sunset, sea, beach, snow and cloud photographs spanning 2012 to 2026 (f00, f01 and the recurring hashtag captions "#seasons ... #skies #colours", "Lots of #birds #flying #about", "Icy Water"). The watchable behavior is obsessive gathering of fleeting light on natural surfaces, then redeployment: those collected textures come back as album art (f09, the faded halftone lake of Still Life; f15, the underwater blossom of Sidekick). He photographs the world the way other producers sample records.
  2. The long ferment: write, record, wait years. His 2017-11-17 caption is the mechanism in plain language: "about 2 years ago, i went to the studio with @lloydhaines and @superstaralexomg to record a bunch of music that i'd semi written. Since recording i've been mixing and playing around with it." Bandcamp describes the debut solo LP Sidekick (2023) as "a long time in the making". The Human Error EP, Still Life EP and Sidekick all gestated for years between session and release. Nothing is finished; everything is left to mature and re-touched across seasons.
  3. Layered collage, distressed surfaces. His artworks across the years share one grammar: a face or object buried under accumulated layers, with light arriving through the stack. Double exposure plus scratches and dust (f07, Human Error, 2017); impasto flower field with a face dissolving into blossoms (f05, Snowpoet debut, 2016); faded halftone aging with torn paper edges (f09, Still Life, 2019); torn paper plus a giant eye plus pastel washes (f13, Jordan Rakei's What We Call Life, 2021); underwater refraction over blossoms (f15, Sidekick, 2023). He made Snowpoet's Heartstrings artwork himself (2025). The damage is never decoration: scratches, tears and water are how time gets into the image.
  4. Texture generated communally, live. f11: three men from behind at a wall of synths and modular gear in magenta studio light, captioned "4 day synth/music making bender with @jrakz" (2020-03-04, naming Jordan Rakei, opa audio, streetrat, stuartmccallum). f12: motion-blurred home jam, turning knobs on an arpeggiator "eating Bourbon biscuits" (2021). The watchable behavior: bodies around machines, knobs turning, texture emerging from patch cables and arpeggiators rather than from a score. The bender is a method, not a party.

Supporting: the Snowpoet songcraft formula, poetry over a slowly accumulating bed. From the start: Lauren Kinsella's spoken word poetry over chimes, piano and pattering drums (Butterfly EP, 2014; named inspirations Bjork, Joni Mitchell, Tom Waits, Sylvia Plath). Heartstrings (2025) was written through group improvisations in the studio, "unfiltered and alive" in his words. And the arrangement grid as canvas: f02 shows him mixing with Alex Killpartrick at a DAW in 2014; f16 (2024) is the view from behind the engineer at a huge vintage console, captioned with his 1-1 production teaching. The timeline is a compositional surface.

Evolution

  • 2012-2016: the conservatoire bassist. Royal Academy of Music jazz course, trained as a double-bassist, Yamaha Jazz Scholarship 2011. Co-founded Snowpoet with Lauren Kinsella (met at RAM): Butterfly EP December 2014 (poetry, chimes, Wurlitzer), piano EPs with Kit Downes, debut album Snowpoet January 2016 (floral cover by snowskull, f05). Corpus opens 2012-09-27 (f00, beach snapshot) with the texture habit already visible in the sky photos of 2013 (f01).
  • 2017-2021: the sideman and ferment years. Bill Laurance tours; film composing (Pink Wall SXSW premiere 2019, A Million Eyes 2019 with the warm domestic-light still of f10, Castle in the Ground, She Is Love BFI 2022); solo EPs Human Error (f07, the 2-year ferment session with Lloyd Haines and Alex Haines) and Still Life (f09); deepening Jordan Rakei partnership (synths and co-writing on What We Call Life, Ninja Tune 2021, f13). Identity shifts from bassist to keys/producer across these years.
  • 2022-2026: the producer-for-hire peak. Debut solo album Sidekick (E2/Edition, March 2023) with its underwater visual world (f14 announcement roses, f15 cover); production credits stacking up in 2025-2026 (Frida Touray Mending EP, Alison Sudol album, Laura Mvula, Tom Iansek / #1 Dads "Every Little Fire", Oli Rockberger Chambers, Marnie Glum Fairweather, Antenoch Dovetail); 1-1 production and composition teaching (2024, f16); Snowpoet Heartstrings May 2025, produced by him with his own artwork; Abbey Road week writing and recording with Jordan Rakei (f17, 2025); new father October 2026. One-liner: a conservatoire bassist turned texture-hunting producer, who spent a decade building a London network of collaborators and now trades in slow-fermented, layered, collage-like music.
