Evolution: a sketcher who learned code

Phase 1 — the Daily ink years (2012-2019)

102 of his 254 posts are from 2018 alone. The Daily series (Daily 26, 49, 66, 83, 100, 115, 130, 145...) is a sustained ink-sketching practice: crosshatched urban sketches of Taipei life — a bakery shelf of bread loaves (Daily 26, 2018-10-17), Taipei 101 with fireworks on New Year's Eve (Daily 100, 2018-12-31), a commuter napping on the MRT (Daily 115), a figure at a computer (Daily 83, 2018-12-14), a breakfast plate with cartoon toys (Daily 145, 2019-10-15). Medium: pen on tan or white paper, white highlight pen for light. He calls it 慢寫 (slow sketching), not 速寫 (fast sketching) — the hand is the instrument, the page the ground. This never leaves him: every later generative phase renders like one of these pages.

Phase 2 — the AR years (2017-2020)

Per Creative Bloq: began interactive new media 2014; AR works from 2017; official Meta Spark teaching partner for Taiwan; several official Meta global contest wins. On IG: "喜歡這種光的感覺 #augmentedreality" (2020-05-28) — an ink sketch of someone sleeping at a desk with books, posted as an AR piece (the light is the AR layer over the drawing). The pattern is set: the DRAWING is the anchor, the technology is a layer on top. He never abandons the pen.

Phase 3 — the NFT years (2021-2022)

2021-09-26: "最近做了一個系列的互動 NFT 放在 #hicetnunc 上，順利賣光了！ 還售出了一件二手的" — an interactive NFT series of FOUR ROOMS, each room a different meaning; owning one room reveals the others. Sold out on hicetnunc. objkt/akaSwap footprint: Constellation Array (星叢陣列) series, Creative Media Gathering 創意媒體聚 #39 數位紀念章, dialog() Taipei PoA. MoCA Taipei and Jut Museum exhibition credits (Creative Bloq).

Phase 4 — the generative pivot (2022-2023)

The hinge. He becomes a teacher of the medium: "政大課程備課中，這周要教的是 noise 的應用" (2022-10-17) — teaching noise/flow fields at NCCU (National Chengchi University); Hahow online generative-art course (2023-05-02: "稱自己為藝術家其實有點不好意思... Hahow 的生成式藝術[課]"). Weekly participation in @sableraph's Weekly Creative Coding Challenge through 2023: generative succulents (2023-06-17), a WCC piece trying "noise 以外技巧來產生 輪廓，然後加上一些手繪的風格" (2023-06-25 — contours WITHOUT noise, plus hand-drawn style; this is the whole thesis in one caption), a Vera Molnar WCC (2023-11-05). First physical print output, framed (2023-11-20). fxhash mint with "線條模式" line mode (2022-12-28, #p5js #fxhash).

Phase 5 — competition-grade (2024-2026)

"A Dance with History" shortlisted in Combine24, the Finnish National Gallery generative-art competition (2024-08-05; confirmed on highlight.xyz: 10 shortlisted works, his entry from newyellow.eth). Generative Möbius ring with the UV twist done right (2024-09-05 — his caption details the math trouble: "Mesh 的 UV 其實會轉 180 度，很容易對不起來"). Lotus-leaf development with blade-vs-lens angle normal math (2024-04-16). Flat-style overlapping layouts (2024-03-28). Particle AR commission (2023-09-27). Meta Spark shutdown -> Lens Studio pivot (2025-04-01: "從去年 Meta Spark 宣布停止服務後就開始研究 Lens Studio 濾鏡"). Co-organized "N{}U — Undefined: 一切尚在編碼中" generative exhibition with NCCU blockchain-course students (Dec 2025, via c2x3). TGDF 2026 noise-application talk in Unity3D (2026-07-10 posts).

The arc: hand (2012) -> hand+phone (2017) -> hand+chain (2021) -> hand+code (2022) -> hand+code+institution (2024). The pen never leaves; the input systems get more powerful around it.

Core engine

A SKETCHBOOK PAGE THAT COMPUTES ITSELF. Every piece is drawn as if by his ink pen — hand-jittered strokes, hatch shading, paper-cream or night-black grounds — but the marks are placed by a visible algorithm (flow fields, contour rules, UV twists, growth parameters, blend math). Decoration = the pen/plotter look. Driver = algorithmic mark placement that stays legible as hand-drawn. A piece fails his standard when the algorithm's output looks vector-perfect or rendered instead of drawn.

Testable form: given any output, you must be able to point at a stroke and say which rule put it there (field direction, contour level, jitter seed, blend overlap) AND the stroke must look hand-made.
