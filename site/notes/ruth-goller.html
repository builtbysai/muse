Core engine

Two interlocking mechanisms:

  1. Detuning as composition. Each Skylla song lives in its own intuitive tuning, so muscle memory dies and the ear has to lead ("at that point muscle memory doesn't work anymore so I have to trust my ear completely", web). Her own caption, playing around with micro-tuning on "I is One (M10)": "enjoying the tension it adds". Harmonics carry melody over detuned beds; the bass is played as an upper-register instrument (Jazzwise). The instrument is deliberately made unfamiliar, so that every performance is a first encounter.
  2. Voice-bass duet, then choir. The base unit is her voice entwined with bass guitar in improvised tandem, intimate and unmediated (barefoot pedalism: f00, feet on the red rug working Whammy / Freeze / octave, captioned "footfoot"). On SKYLLUMINA this expands into a choir of one: detuned bass under a self-arranged soprano spectra, with a DIFFERENT drummer on every track (Skinner, Rochford, Bex Burch, Frank Rosaly, Jim Hart). The gatefold liner notes spell the material: "electric bass in multiple intuitive tunings", "sea of low bass tones" (f15).

Supporting mechanisms: alpine folk-horror staging (Wintercroft polygonal deer mask at a Cafe Oto solo set, f08; cloaked horned figure with staff in the snow forest for Skylla M4, f09); environmentalism as material (Dolomite with Ghezzi = a water-particle journey told in 4 languages, home recorded); a pedalboard philosophy where the foot is a second pair of hands.

Evolution

  • 2016-2018: home-Dolomites bass life. Early posts from the Dolomites; the bass as domestic object and mountain practice partner. 2018: SKYLLA born at Klaeng Festival, Cologne (f03, first outing in a dark concert photo).
  • 2019-2021: sideman circuit and debut. Vula Viel sideman (Bex Burch's project, 10 evidenced ties; her own sideman years, not her band). 2021: SKYLLA released on Vula Viel Records; artwork by Paula Rae Gibson (f06); Mike Watt calls in as a fan and 2022 Pedro-show guest (f10, screenshot of his post).
  • 2022-2023: touring scale. Skylla UK tour; Alabaster DePlume sideman at arena scale (supporting Bon Iver in Tilburg, f12; Koko Camden). Solo sets in masks and fog.
  • 2024: the double release year. SKYLLUMINA on International Anthem; Dolomite EP with Maddalena Ghezzi on Dengyue (home recorded, water journey in 4 languages); SKYLLA reissue with full gatefold credits; "Skylla 3 begun" (f11, home selfie with pop filter and condenser mic: "SKYLLA 2 in the making").
  • 2025-2026: residency and preview circuit. Tasmania / Dark Mofo trip (f16); remixes; Silver Birch and It's In The Sea with Peter Wareham; Brussels Jazz Fest; Oct 2026 China tour with Alabaster (Xi'an, f17, SoundFact/Xiaohongshu watermarks); forthcoming third album previewed in Sheffield Oct 2026.
