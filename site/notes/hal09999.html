Study #217: HAL09999

studied 2026-10-31/14 min read/2,788 words

Identity

  • Name: HAL09999. French generative artist based in France.
  • Instagram: @hal09999 (verified via instagram-cli profile read 2026-10-31: name "hal09999", bio "light, matter, dimensions" with wave/radiance glyphs, country France, creator account, 40 posts, 969 followers, public, links to danslesnuages.xyz/wwwaves "u-WwWave, ripples through digital spacetimes" and http://danslesnuages.xyz).
  • Own site: danslesnuages.xyz ("in the clouds"), with dedicated work pages (e.g. /A-theory-of-General-Instability, /wwwaves).
  • In parallel with his art, he teaches computer science, electronics, physics, and digital arts, and has worked as scientific and technical advisor for other contemporary artists and international galleries (per the Responsive Dreams artist bio, Sept 2025). This is the biographical key to the work: the equation-titled studies are a teacher's lab notes made public.
  • This study was candidate 3 in study #216's ordered list, fully verified there (@hal09999, France, wave-physics bio) but superseded by candidate order (Kazuhiro Tanimoto). #216's subagent pulled 43 posts but the pull mixed in third-party feature posts from tgamxyz (The Generative Art Museum, Barcelona), responsivedreams, zardoz.club, and unframed_show, and downloaded frames from the TGAM feature account instead of the artist's own feed. This run re-pulled and filtered to his own 38 posts; the 5 third-party posts were kept separately as network evidence (third_party.json), not as primary material.

Core engine

THE EQUATION IS THE PIECE, AND INTERFERENCE IS THE ENGINE: HAL09999 treats physics as the computation, not the theme. Wave sources emit fields; where the fields interfere constructively, matter appears as dots, particles, and structures; where they cancel, voids open. The mechanism is stated outright in his own captions ("More interferences, creating dots where the waves meet...", "How particles appear"), and the study titles are literally equations (a(t) proportional to e^Ht for cosmic inflation, sums of phase-shifted wavefunctions, Dirac bra-ket vacuum expectation values). Sound is synthesized from the same signals (binaural WebAudio, 12-second etudes, "air vibrations" between audio sources and the viewer). Works are named like lab experiments ("Etude [641a3e79]", hash-suffixed), distributed on chain (fxhash, objkt/Tezos), and in one case are an actual playable browser game with entropy operators. A teacher of physics teaching a browser to do physics: the artwork is the field behaving.

Signature devices observed

  1. The two-source interference field (H-Emitters, "Transformations du champ"). Two or more point sources emit concentric wave rings across a dark field; where rings meet, bright interference dots condense and dark nodal lines cross. f07 (H-Emitters iteration 48): two emitters top and bottom, cyan concentric rings, vertical nodal banding between them. f16 (etude d692f8b0, titled with the inflation equation): orange concentric rings from a single central source on a horizontal axis, hourglass nodal pattern. f15 (etude d224fb30): dense radial moire field, fine white-copper lines radiating from a center, a diffraction-grating look. f17 (Transformations du champ [0f3c1893]): diagonal red-hot wave bands, color fields layered over the wave field. His TGAM caption: "a real-time quasi-simulation of wave patterns... multiple sources generate endlessly evolving forms, while a live binaural composition makes the phenomenon resonate and create similar patterns in the air between the audio source and the viewer." Mechanism to copy: field = sum of sinusoidal sources with phase; render constructive maxima as dots, color by field amplitude.
  2. The 2.5-million-particle instability universe ("A theory of General Instability"). fxhash (FX1-19004), April 2023, shown at Proof of People, ZeroSpace NYC (2023), projected at the Fibonacci celebrations in Pisa (Nov 2025). His words: "2.5 millions particles are exposed to instabilities of a digital scalar force field. This quasi-simulation explores the shapes that result from a universe originated from changing sequences of events, with original irregularities of the order of less than 1%." That is cosmological inflation stated as a generative parameter: a near-uniform field with sub-1% seeded irregularity, instability does the rest. f01: a circular cosmic disk, dark core with electric-blue filaments and violet-red nebulous ring; f02: dark field with magenta plasma, bright white filaments in a clumpy core, black void bubbles. The minter gets a 4K PNG; the work is a minted quasi-simulation, not a fixed image.
  3. The waveform ribbons ("Fonctions d'onde", wave-function studies, objkt/Tezos). "Local realism is an optical illusion." f03: translucent colored ribbons (cyan, magenta, yellow-green, violet) undulating across black, a horizontal standing-wave band at center, concentric ring emitter sources at top corners. The wave function made watchable: each oscillator is a ribbon, phase relationships visible as ribbon crossings. This is the quantum-mechanical cousin of the interference-field device, rendered as line geometry rather than dots.
  4. The entropy game ("THE PHASE EXPERIENCE / Complex Assemblies"). An experimental browser game, live on fxhash since Aug 22, 2024, "playable on any computer with a browser and a GPU," desktop/GPU required. "An experimental game where the player can interact with algorithmic structures, using entropy operators. Play with order and chaos in your browser." All structure characteristics are chosen from the mint hash, "analyzed in many ways" (his caption promises a future write-up). "This software now lives also onchain, written in JS." f08 (the Episode 0 release poster): bright prismatic voxel cloud, colorful pixelated blocks dissolving in white glow; f09 (the "Distribution" poster): dotted orbital trajectories around a rust-orange glowing core, a molecular-diagram look, "THE PHASE EXPERIENCE" edge text. The interaction model that matters for the prompt pipeline: the player injects entropy into a running system. Order versus chaos is the gameplay, and it is inside the system's own logic.
  5. The refractive artificial volumes ("Volumes Artificiels I"). Algorithmically generated meshes rendered transparent with light sources in a 3D render engine (2024). f06: close crop of a glassy faceted shard, white-blue caustic refractions on black, an ice-crystal look. The physics interest moves from waves to light transport: refraction and reflection as the visible behavior. Note the numbering "I", implying a series.
  6. The fragmented continuum / the inflation shell. Two 2024-2025 studies about one idea: a continuous field rendered as discrete particles at a propagating front. f11, the "Fragmentation of the continuum" poster (2024-09): a concentric iridescent ring tunnel, gold core, teal/violet oil-slick rings on near-black. f13, the "Inflation study [5b635bf1]" (2025-02, titled with the inflation equation a(t) proportional to e^Ht): a luminous expanding shell built from millions of particles, red-orange rim, silver-white core, blue accents, a shockwave-front look. The watchable behavior: a front moves outward, and behind it the continuum is particles.
  7. The vacuum fluctuation studies (2025-2026 bra-ket era). Titles in Dirac notation: (0+|Phi*|0-) and (0|Phi dagger|0) => target, "Happy Pi Day", "gamma -> 0^R+ -> gamma" (pair annihilation), study 463-535, an "Upcoming experience" teased for April 15, 2026. f18 (Study 08db3433, 2026): a blue smoke ribbon curling vertically, an ethereal filament; f19 (Expansion study 45ec0dfa, 2026): a rust-red volumetric rounded form with a glowing cyan blade cutting through it and a small cyan capsule floating nearby, pink wireframe torus detail. The mechanism: virtual particles flicker in and out of a near-empty field (wave sum yields particle: his caption "wave + wave -> dot: How particles appear"), sparse, dark, meditative. The newest work is volumetric and expansion-themed, tying back to the inflation equation of 2025.
  8. The cosmic nightscape (early era). f00 ("A long starry night", Nov 2022): a generative starfield, teal gradient sky, dense white star dust thickening toward a dark horizon, three.js/WebGL. f04 ("2022 A generative odyssey", iteration 2): a dark planet limb against a blinding white-red glow, an occultation composition. The early work is space-illustration mode; everything after is physics-simulation mode. The evolution from illustrating space to simulating fields is the whole arc.
  9. The numbered etude as release unit. Nearly every 2025-2026 post is "Etude [hash]" or "study [hash]": 12-second video renders (one 180-second extract), custom JavaScript/WebGL software, audio synthesis (binaural in later studies), posted as regular practice. The etude is the unit of the practice the way a daily sketch is for a draftsman: one equation, one parameter set, twelve seconds of field behavior. Each frame is signed with the etude ID and often his handle, like a lab notebook page.
  10. The silicon note. Jan 2025: "Been working hard on a novel method of silicon exploitation for the next phase of this experimental project." The post's image (f12) is a macro photograph of a circuit board (component labels C11, D20, dark field, glowing contacts): the hardware that runs the simulations shown as an object. The method itself is undocumented in the accessible material; no further silicon posts followed.

Evolution: early vs recent

  • 2022-11: three.js/WebGL cosmic nightscapes ("A long starry night", "2022 A generative odyssey"). Illustrating space.
  • 2023-04: the physics turn. "A theory of General Instability" (fxhash, 2.5M particles, scalar force field, inflation language) and "Fonctions d'onde" (wave functions, objkt). The cosmic imagery stays but the driver becomes quasi-simulation.
  • 2023-09 to 12: odyssey iterations continue; the "ENTROPY | Digital/analog" carousel (unretrieved frames).
  • 2024-08: material and light studies (Volumes Artificiels I, refraction) run alongside the emitter studies (H-Emitters iteration 48); then the game: THE PHASE EXPERIENCE / Complex Assemblies goes live on fxhash Aug 22, with entropy operators, mint-hash-analyzed parameters, and on-chain JS. The ambient etudes (ring tunnels) run through Oct.
  • 2024-09: "Hyper-rotational" particle volumes and "Fragmentation of the continuum" (the particle shockwave shell).
  • 2025-01: winter "silicon exploitation" work.
  • 2025-02 to 05: the numbered etude era: 12-second WebGL studies with equation titles (inflation, phase sums, interference-to-particle), binaural audio synthesis. Interference is now the explicit, named mechanism.
  • 2025-09 to 12: "Transformations du champ" matures: TGAM commissions it, Responsive Dreams features it, zardoz.club collects edition #17, it shows at Blanc! (Generative Dialogues V) and at NEW RETINAS Part Two in Madrid (ART LAB Siroco, Dec 5).
  • 2025-12: "Expansion studio".
  • 2026-02 to 05: the bra-ket era: vacuum fluctuation studies in Dirac notation, an April 15, 2026 "upcoming experience" teaser. The newest inspected frame (Expansion study, 2026) is volumetric: rust-red particle volume cut by a cyan blade.
  • 2026-10-28: TGAM posts about Transformations du Champ at Blanc! three days before this study, confirming the work is live and current.
