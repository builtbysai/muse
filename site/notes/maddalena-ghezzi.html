Core engine

THE COLLABORATION IS THE INSTRUMENT. Across 448 posts there is no solo record, no lone vehicle: every project names its people, and the account reads as a decade-long practice of making things with others. The engine runs through two interlocking mechanisms.

(1) The duet as crystallography. The Minerals series is the headline vehicle from 2020 on: one mineral plus one collaborator equals one EP, each release moulded around the collaborator's sonic material (her own framing, echoed in her captions: "Minerals is a series of collaborators with musicians I love", 2020-05-14). Amethyst with double bassist Thodoris Ziarkas, Halite with pianist Ed Blunt, Opal with guitarist and vocalist Francesca Naibo, Emerald with synth player Maria Chiara Argiro (her "sci-fi feminist EP"), Dolomite with bassist and vocalist Ruth Goller, Calcite with Maurizio Ravalico still in progress ("Rocks will be played, literally!", Hundred Years Gallery, May 2022). Each EP takes the shape of its partner the way a crystal takes the shape of its lattice. Her name for the operating principle is the "dual journey": collaboration as a musical event and an emotional event at once. Her own line on how it works: "I have the feeling the music gets there first. I mean, it kind of cuts through our structures and veils and tells you: 'hey, this is a good path or no don't go that way.'" Moment's Notice curator George Nelson names the gift exactly: "selfless collaboration."

(2) The non-musical source as score. Every project starts outside music and is translated in. Minerals: minerals. Monologo Addosso: Elena Cornaggia's poetry cycle, set by three voices and a guitar (Beatrice Arrigoni, Ghezzi, Naibo; produced by Luca Xelius Martegani at niton_lab; Habitable Records, 08/03/2026). Lockdown work: the Vita Sackville-West and Virginia Woolf letter chapters, read as chapters (frames f07). The Sara Serpa workshop piece: the book The Hidden Life of Trees, staged as a tree trunk at the water's edge (frame f09). Dolomite: the mountain range itself, an "exploration of a dolomite plateau" with Goller (frame f11 flyer), sung in English, Italian, Ladino and German, the two voices "following each other like water particles" (Betty Accorsi, Jazz In Europe). The 2025-2026 "3M words" residency: a year-long self-imposed residency in motherhood, led by her son Ennio, producing 12 collages and the track "tree human" (frame f16: overlapping magenta and peach letter-M ink stamps on paper; words by @emmaiduma: "For you I must become a tree, every tree is the opposite of wandering"). "Rupture and repair": a track plus a 2.5 metre tapestry graphic score sewn over six months (frame f17: two women holding the embroidered banner "NELL'UNIVERSO ROTTO DI UN GRIDO MI VERGOGNO DELLA MIA RABBIA", baby Ennio in the grass in front). Her voice is itself described as a drawing instrument ("When I sing I always feel like my voice is drawing a path in my body", ConnectsMusic interview), and the pen-drawing practice runs in parallel through the whole corpus: the Duomo facade (frame f01), station drawings and postcards with Ed Blunt (Stations / Stanza Da Tre, 2017-2018), crystal collages as EP artwork (frame f10, the Opal cover: rhodochrosite, quartz, amethyst, agate on white).

Evolution timeline

  • Pre-IG (web-evidenced): born in Milan; moved to the UK in 2009. Studied jazz singing with Brigitte Beraha; Libero Canto; improv and composition with Sara Serpa. Her singing palette runs through Mongolian throat singing, Yiddish and Russian songs, and Maqam music (interview claims; only Yiddish is echoed in the corpus, 2017 #songsofresistance concerts with a "yiddish tutor"). As a kid she imitated people's way of talking and recorded them on a tape machine (ConnectsMusic: "I think I haven't lost that approach").
  • 2017-2019, the ensemble and drafting years (IG opens): the account opens 2017-01-19 and posts at its heaviest (125 posts in 2017, 92 in 2018). FUWAH, her band, starts as a duo with double-bassist Luca Pissavini and grows to a quartet (Filippo Cozzi sax, Fabrizio Carriero drums); "Eŭropo: sen limoj" launches 10/04/2019 on Argomm, a women-led narrative with lyrics by eight women writers in eight languages. Stanza Da Tre with Ed Blunt (@edblunt123): drawing and jazz gigs, women-composers concerts. Parasang live electronics nights. Pen drawings of the Duomo, stations, postcards (#ayearinpostcards with Eloise Oatley), Lumineuse magazine articles. Music here means playing in groups; the visual practice is already a second instrument.
  • 2020-2022, the concept series as engine: Minerals replaces the band as the headline vehicle. Recording at niton_lab becomes the ritual space; releases on dengyue.records. Amethyst (Ziarkas, second recording 2020-03, named from 2020-05); Halite (Ed Blunt on piano via @edbluntmusic, full release 2020-11-27); Opal (Naibo, release 22/02/2022, "a tale of finding clarity" per her interview); Emerald (Argiro, 2023). Pandemic solo video works in parallel: "Loss of Face" (Sara Serpa weekly assignments), "Transitions" (DIY travel video and audio), the Vita and Virginia Woolf letter chapters. Posting volume collapses (17 in 2021) then steadies.
  • 2023-2026, the cross-disciplinary author: Monologo Addosso grows from 2023 sessions to the 2026 Habitable album ("Quando il cervello" = "translating into music the sound of a healing process", her caption). Dolomite EP 17/05/2024 ("egoless" collaboration, Truth and Lies, 9/10). Sara Serpa 8-week composition workshop. M3 Mutual Mentorship for Musicians cohorts, "Re-member Water" co-composed with Melissa Almaguer (2024-2025). The sewn tapestry graphic score ("rupture and repair", Nov 2025 to May 2026). The motherhood residency ("3M words", 2025-2026). Recent captions read as process journals: how a piece was made, healing, collaboration as journey. Current cycle: Monologo Addosso release and the November to December 2026 tour with Ruth Goller and Maria Chiara Argiro (Sheffield 25/11, Coventry 26/11, Birmingham 27/11, London 04/12, her own tour post).
