Core engine

Two interlocking mechanisms, stated as what they ARE:

  1. Melody as a baton, roles as costumes. The two engines of his work are the relay and the rotation. On Emong's "Starting With A Cherry" (2023-04-16) "il tema e la rilevanza melodica passano di voce in voce: dalla chitarra al sax, dal sax al trombone" — a theme is never developed, it is handed off, instrument to instrument, each new voice changing what the theme is. And the ensemble is deliberately built for role mobility: "La scelta della formazione, insieme a quella del repertorio, valorizza la mobilità del ruolo di ognuno di noi all'interno del gruppo per creare un suono unitario e coeso e contemporaneamente fluido e sfuggente" (2023-04-11) — a unified, cohesive sound that is simultaneously fluid and elusive, because nobody owns a role. The mechanism: music composed around passing and swapping rather than building; the baton never stops, the costumes keep changing.
  1. Compose, decompose, recombine — in public. On the Move (founded December 2023 with Beatrice Arrigoni, his first band without guitar) takes Michael Rosen's migration poems as raw material: "Sono poesie meravigliose che raccontano di migrazione, ricerca e movimento... Testi emozionanti che cercheremo di comporre, scomporre e ricombinare per restituirne, e speriamo amplificarne, la forza comunicativa." (2023-12-13). A poem is shredded and rewoven live, in voice + electronics, in front of an audience — "un viaggio di suoni e parole intorno al tema della migrazione" (Medimex 2026). The guitarist's defection makes the theme autobiographical: "il primo gruppo in cui non suonerò la chitarra ma un set di elettronica e slide guitar" — the player migrates too, abandoning his primary instrument for the electronics desk. Migration is simultaneously the subject, the method, and the personnel move.

Supporting mechanisms:

  • Mobile equilibrium between writing and freedom. The Giacomo Ganzerli Trio (bassless: drums, tenor sax, guitar + live electronics and drum machine) is built around a balance that never settles: "Jazz moderno ed elettronica dal vivo si inseguono dentro un suono essenziale e in continua trasformazione... un equilibrio mobile tra scrittura e libertà, materia elettronica e presenza acustica" (2026-08-31, announcing "The Art of Leaving Blankness" on nusica.org).
  • Portrait-as-documentation rollout. The Three Knots pre-release was a serialized weekly portrait gallery under #maipiùsenza — Evita Polidoro ("Ha una varietà timbrica e un'inventiva stupefacenti", 2023-04-12), Manuel Caliumi ("l'elemento propulsivo, nervoso, denso, viscerale", 2023-04-21), Federico Pierantoni ("Fede canta come pochi musicisti che mi capita di ascoltare", 2023-04-26) — each player introduced through a functional sketch of their role in the sound, a band documented character by character.
  • Bells and civic ritual as score. The Campanone performance (2021-10-25, Bergamo's Città Alta): "Suonare sui rintocchi del Campanone per poi proseguire in un viaggio sospeso nel tempo per raccontarne il significato e il suo ruolo nell'immaginario collettivo attraverso musica, poesia e danza" — the city's bell strikes set the temporal frame; music, poetry and dance expand a civic sound into narrative.
  • Partisan memory re-scored. 25 aprile 2026: their Bella Ciao with Orchestra MoltiMondi opens with Giacomo Ulivi's "Lettera agli amici" — "le parole immortali di Giacomo Ulivi, partigiano modenese di 19 anni... scritta subito prima della sua fucilazione, avvenuta a fianco del Duomo il 10 novembre 1944" (2026-04-25) — collective memory as the spoken preface to a re-harmonized folk anthem.

Evolution (dated, evidenced)

  • 2020: electric guitarist, analog obsessions. House concerts, full-analog tape sessions at Ritmo e Blu (2020-02-16); Blend 3 sideman (Andrea Grossi's trio, founded 2017, "without a predetermined set, utilizes composition and improvisation as its only resources").
  • 2021: power-trio years. Mopoke (with Giacomo Papetti, Filippo Sala) releases "Trovare" (Kandinsky Records, June 2021). The Campanone bell performance in Bergamo's Città Alta (nighttime courtyard, purple wash, Dudù Kouate and Filippo Sala on hand percussion; f02).
  • 2022: going leader. Emong teaser: "Esce oggi allo scoperto il primo progetto a mio nome!" (2022-04-05). Parallel: Blend 3's "Songs and Poems" gives Beatrice Arrigoni's voice to Grossi's chosen poems (2022-04-30) — the poetry-plus-band precursor to On the Move.
  • 2023: Three Knots + the electronics turn. Emong's "Three Knots" on nusica.org (released 18.4.2023; web: nusica.org's 23rd production, Bonifati's first record as leader; title = the knots of wind an Emong kite needs to fly — the group is named for a kite that flies in feeble, almost absent wind). Voted "miglior Nuovo Talento" Top Jazz 2023 (Musica Jazz); Emong 3rd "formazione dell'anno", Three Knots 6th "disco dell'anno". December 2023: On the Move duo founded with Arrigoni — guitar abandoned for electronics + slide guitar (f08, the brick-wall announcement portrait).
  • 2024: Axes + Rosen excerpts. Andrea Grossi Blend 3 "Axes" with Jim Black (We Insist! Records, released 17.5.2024; web: jazzjournal.co.uk — "Michele Bonifati's buzzing guitar", polymodal 20-minute suite). Second On the Move excerpt: Rosen's "Nothing" (2024-03-04).
  • 2025–2026: three lanes at once. Mopoke's "Vaga Percezione" (barlyrecords, released 6.11.2026; Lisbon Italian Jazz Days 2025). Bella Ciao with Orchestra MoltiMondi (25.4.2026). On the Move showcased at Medimex Taranto (18.6.2026, Selezione Nuova Generazione Jazz; f14 poster). Ganzerli Trio's "The Art of Leaving Blankness" (nusica.org, out Jan 2026; web: bassless trio, Daniele Nasi tenor sax, Bonifati guitar, fall 2026 tour f15 poster). The Arc: sideman guitarist → bandleader of a mobile-roles quartet → defected electronics performer in a migration-poetry duo, running all three lanes simultaneously.
