Core engine
THE RESOURCEFUL TRANSLATOR + THE POOR MAN'S SUBSTITUTE She takes an effect seen elsewhere — Takawo's drawingContext.filter blur, Delaunay's Orphism, Richter's 4900 Colors, textbook DLA, Turing patterns — and rebuilds it from the poorest means available in her stack, publishing the code. The translation gap IS the art: "My loving Processing must get over the p5.js drawingContext!", "The Beauty of Mistakes: My Julia Set 'Fail'", "Poor Man's DLA." Her stated pleasure: "I love to deal resourcefully with how can I make something with the available stuff only... this delight brings me the real pleasures of creative coding."

Signature devices (observed, code-verified):

  1. Rhythmic shooting instead of randomness. Poor Man's DLA (2019-10, revisited

2026-02): cells fired in straight lines from an angle that doubles each step (entryR += entryR; entryR %= TWO_PI) toward a seed ring; collision sticks the cell. Final iteration uses NO random() at all — pure rhythm grows moss that looks like real diffusion-limited aggregation. "Complexity doesn't always require randomness; sometimes, pure rhythm is enough."

  1. Stacked transparent layers with per-layer blur. To fake p5.js's

drawingContext.filter = "blur(...)" in Processing: 12 transparent PGraphics layers, random circles per layer, filter(BLUR, random) per layer, composited. Applied twist: the black-halo bug (transparent background RGB 0,0,0 bleeding into blurred edges) diagnosed and turned into colored shadows — blur radius as a color-delivery mechanism.

  1. Vector-field watercolor. Particles advected through a noise/attractor field

with high-transparency strokes; thousands of soft overlapping circles become watercolor washes (Walk Between Raindrops, 2019-08; greens/teals on warm paper, with node-garden line clusters knitting through the wash).

  1. Characteristic circle packing. (2021-07) Dense packing where each circle gets

"character" — faces, rings, hatching, varied fills — plus a big organic negative-space void in the center holding a symmetric fern/plant motif. Packing is the substrate; character is the content.

  1. Environmental Game of Life. "Castle Walls" (2019-09, refactored 2026-09):

cells hold continuous health 0–100, a global envFactor sweeps 0.1→0.8 so the cause-and-effect is visibly obvious (2026 refactor explicitly fixes the 2019 version's "fatal lackluster visual impact"), corner-rounding joins when full-life neighbors touch, offline frame rendering. Rules bend to the environment.
