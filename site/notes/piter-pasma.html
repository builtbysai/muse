Core engine

THE SAME SDF IS BOTH THE SCULPTURE AND THE DRAWING: Pasma defines 3D form once, as signed distance fields, then runs it through two backends that are really one idea. The hand-written Monte Carlo pathtracer (WebGL2, 180 lines of JS plus 200 of GLSL in the 2021 "Algorithmic Sculpture 1") makes the SDF read as physical matter, photorealistic bronze, rusted patina, glowing interiors. His own program "rayhatcher" converts the exact same light field into plottable line orders: "it creates hatched drawings of the 3D forms defined by SDFs, using raymarching" (Art Blocks interview), with shading oriented along surface tangents and line order optimized for the plotter. The plotter's physical constraints (no erasing, pen width, travel time, the clip that wiggled and ruined the line in "XIX The Sun") are baked into the algorithm instead of cleaned up afterward. The code is artisanal and tiny by discipline: 6370 bytes for the whole Skulptuur drop (Art Blocks project 173, 1000 photorealistic SDF sculptures rendered in real time, image quality improving while watched, keys 0-7 controlling speed, with "a greeting list in there for my friends"), 42 lines of code for a 2025 Genuary duotone, about 10KB of JS+GLSL with no libraries for the 2024 corroded-bronze renders, 59 lines for a generative asemic writing script. Minimal code as both aesthetic and constraint. Demoscene roots are literal: "In the beginning there was code, and the code had a groove"; he scores pieces with his own music (soundcloud tripzilch) and once emulated a TB-303 in 150 lines of integer-math JavaScript.

Evolution

  • 2020, plotter era: Axidraw plots with gel and ballpoint pens. Contour tunnels (f01: heightmap of a spiky function on a tunnel, marching-squares iso-contours, occlusion via circular clipping mask), circle networks, the four-hour single-line dendritic drawing (f02, 0.19mm Uni-ball Signo, "Four hours, one continuous line", the line goes around the figure twice for tone contrast), and the rayhatcher invention (f04 "Nyarlathotep Road pt. 3", 0.25mm Rotring, 10,844 strokes in about two hours, hatching from SDFs plus ray marching). Mostly black and white pen on paper.
  • 2021, dual track: plotter continues (Genuary 2021 dailies: f05 "Circles only. Lots and lots of circles" Apollonian packing; f06 hatched rectangles plotted) while a photoreal 3D pivot arrives. Hand-written Monte Carlo pathtracer (f08, torus tower with glowing interior, 180+200 lines). September 27, 2021: Skulptuur on Art Blocks, 6370 bytes, 1000 editions, render refines while watched live. Hic Et Nunc from March 2021, Foundation later that year, Dot Produkt and Hypergiraffe on fxhash in November.
  • 2022: Genuary dailies again (f09 blue-noise dithered galaxy, f10 diffusion-limited-aggregation "Abstract vegetation" with a nod to Tyler Hobbs in a "Next Next Fidenza" caption, f11 asemic writing in 59 lines), stipple nebulae, the Versum release including generative poetry ("I AM YOU"). 2023 is near-silent in the pull (4 posts; possibly API pagination holes).
  • 2024: translucent and colored raymarched renders (f12 Genuary "THE DROSTE EFFECT" blob with subsurface glow and live browser version, his own music), f13 corroded bronze "licorice raspberry duality anti memorial" in about 10KB of code, rayhatcher framework upgrades (shape outlines), Epihyperderpflardioids live on phone. Feral File N=12 micro-community (code-sharing, July 2023, curated by Aaron Penne).
  • 2025: bold duotone SDF posters (f15, black interlocked tubes on golden yellow, halftone grain, 42 lines of code, live source on netlify), AI normal estimation to hatch photographs, "Nanolichen" shattered basalt (f17), Impossible Sentinels on fx(hash) via Bonhams for Cure Parkinsons (Cure3 2025), Galerie Data FORGED IN DATA (digital works turned into bronze, ceramic, wood).
  • 2026: straight-line plotter experiments (f18, star polygons from overlapping strokes, "plots coming soon, probably"), MASKS on Art Blocks April 23, 2026 (128 editions, 0.05 ETH; Bre Pettis of Bantam Tools plotted gigantic physical versions), and recent posts resurfacing older algorithms (f19 "Eriban" from 2022/11/02, line order optimized for plotter; Fraglines from 2024).

Frame notes (20, all visually inspected)

  • f00 (2020-08-13): Photo of a physical plotter drawing pinned by a binder clip, red surface behind. Fine blue ballpoint linework fills a large partial disc with triangulated mesh faces, packed circles, swirling contours; top right of the paper blank where the pen lost contact. Caption: "XIX The Sun", a badly attached clip made the board wiggle and the pen lose contact. Process photo as much as artwork.
  • f01 (2020-09-08): Black-on-white contour lines forming a tunnel of warped organic lobes converging to a dark vanishing point low center. Caption: "I made a tunnel renderer!   • f02 (2020-10-01): Video thumbnail with play button over a close-up of a plotter head mid-stroke, drawing a dense black dendritic lichen-like branching figure on white paper. Caption: "Four hours, one continuous line", 0.19mm Uni-ball Signo on bristol, 14x42cm; the single line goes around the figure twice for tone contrast. Motion from the plotter video.
  • f03 (2020-11-11): Dense grey stipple render of huge hollow tubes and elbows receding into fog, titled "Conduit Dimension, Elbow Quadrant". Caption: shading oriented along surface tangents, 450k short lines, "so yeah, no plot today". Rayhatcher digital render, not plotted.
  • f04 (2020-12-29): Photo of a physical plot: black band across the top, white field below with wavy vertical black columns of dense curving hatch (twisted rope forms), horizontal hatching along the bottom. Caption: "Nyarlathotep Road pt. 3", 0.25mm Rotring pen, lines from a hatching algorithm using signed distance fields and ray marching, 30x11cm, 10,844 strokes, about two hours.
  • f05 (2021-01-16): Apollonian-style circle packing: large white outlined circles shrinking toward diagonal bands of dense tiny black dots, black and white. Genuary 2021 day 16, "Circles only. Lots and lots of circles."
  • f06 (2021-02-09): Video thumbnail, close-up of plotter pen head drawing hatched rectangles and square outlines on white paper. Caption: "Where the circles are not. The plot!! Swipe for closeups and plotting videos!" Black 0.25mm Rotring Rapidograph on 30x42cm bristol. Process video.

  • f12 (2024-01-06): Video thumbnail of a translucent pink-peach SDF blob with iridescent subsurface glow and glassy refractions. Genuary 2024 day 3, "THE DROSTE EFFECT"; live version on netlify; background music his own (soundcloud tripzilch). Motion implied from the video only.
